## Key Discussion Areas (Prepare to contribute)

### 1. Tooling & Data Pipeline
- Current bottlenecks in the MR workflow toolchain?
- What data flows do we need to automate or improve?
- Are there integration gaps between platforms?
- What should the CI/CD / asset pipeline look like for WP2?

### 2. Processes & Workflows
- What would a "proper playbook or template" look like for your area?
- Where do we have repeatable work that could be templated or automated?
- Are there handoff points that feel fragile or unclear?
- What approval gates or quality checks are missing?

### 3. AI in the Platform
- Where could AI genuinely help us (inference, training data, content generation, QA)?
- What are the technical and operational constraints? (latency, compute, licensing)
- Which use cases are "nice to have" vs "high-impact"?

### 4. Metrics & KPIs
- What metrics actually matter in your day-to-day work?
- How do we measure success for this phase?
- What visibility do we need into platform health, team velocity, quality?
