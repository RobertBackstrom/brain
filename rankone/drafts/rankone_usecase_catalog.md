# RankOne Insights — use-case catalog (Johan's dump, 2026-07-03) + narrowing

Johan posted this in the Insights Feedback WhatsApp group after Robert's value/moat/personas answer, framed as "analyses and problems we have historically already solved for clients." He asked to "keep narrowing it down." Captured verbatim below, then a proposed narrowing.

## Johan's 13 use cases, as posted

**Go-To-Market & Marketing**
1. **Player Acquisition (UA):** identify unconventional proxy games with high volume + strong affinity to build lookalike audiences that lower CAC.
2. **Creator Selection:** beyond reach — find creators with the highest *conversion* (inflow) toward a niche, and offer direct activation.
3. **Activate Future Intent:** find creators/profiles already primed for a game who just need a final nudge.
4. **Expand Target Audience:** identify "the second ring" of games around the core audience to scale reach via fast-growing affinity overlaps.

**Game Design & Product Strategy**
5. **Feature Scoping:** validate demand for specific mechanics from actual playing behavior — which features are critical vs unnecessary risk.
6. **Concept Generation:** find "blue oceans" — underserved audiences and new concepts where unexpected genres/interests overlap.
7. **Pricing Strategy:** analyse scope/features vs strongest gateway/proxy games + web context → recommend an optimal price point.
8. **Platform Strategies:** map audience platform preferences; does behaviour for this game type differ across platforms?

**Market Intelligence & Positioning**
9. **Competitor Analysis:** track highest-volume/overlap games; spot fast-rising trends among direct competitors in real time.
10. **Churn Analysis (audience leakage):** who's stealing our players — where the audience goes and what they play when they stop.
11. **Market Position:** find the ultimate gateway/proxy games around your inspirations via authentic player affinity.
12. **Audience Trends:** map the audience lifecycle — recently finished, playing now, heading next.

**Business Case & Publishing**
13. **Pitch Deck / Business Case Analysis:** stress-test incoming pitches; validate studio business cases with hard data before investing.
14. **Market Validation:** prove a viable niche exists by showing RankOne can reach + activate that specific player type.
15. **TAM / Sales Estimates:** calculate TAM + sales estimates from overlap % vs known player bases + historical sales.

(He noted it's "way too many" — meant as inspiration for what it *can* do, not a shortlist.)

## Proposed narrowing — the 3 "hero" use cases to build around

Pick the ones that (a) hit a real budget line, (b) are uniquely RankOne, (c) map cleanly to a visualized feature + an AI-agent flow. My pick:

1. **Creator selection → lower CAC** (his #2, my #1). Clearest money, clearest "guaranteed conversion not just reach" differentiation, and it ends in *activation* (reachable profiles) — un-scrapeable. This is the flagship.
2. **Audience lifecycle / trends** (his #12 + #9 + #10 fused). This is the Pulse timeline — the recurring-use hook and the moat (nobody else has daily snapshots since 2024). Sell the trend, not the snapshot.
3. **Market validation + TAM/sales estimate** (his #14 + #15). The one that unlocks the *publisher/investor* buyer and justifies a premium price — it de-risks a real spend decision.

Everything else (UA lookalikes, feature scoping, pricing, blue-ocean concepting, churn) becomes AI-agent territory: powerful in the agent, not worth a dedicated visualized feature yet. Frame for Johan: **3 visualized hero features + the agent for the long tail** maps 1:1 onto the two-tier free/paid + persona model we already agreed.
