## Company
- **RankOne Global AB**, org nr **559168-5325**, founded 2018, säte Holmsund (Umeå region), Sweden.
- **What it is:** a community platform / "gaming profile" — "Your Life in Games". Gamers manually curate what they've played / are playing / want to play. The aggregate of human-curated data powers player-behaviour insights and game discovery for developers (the **Insights** product, insights.rankone.global).
- Deliberately bets on **manually curated, authentic human data** over AI-generated/automated imports — the differentiator in an AI-content-flooded market.
- Apps: web + iOS (App Store, 2026) + Google Play. B2B pages at rankone.global/company.

## Traction (State of RankOne 2026-05-18)
- **Users:** 101,155 (+1,897 MoM). 2025 year-end was 95,000 — fully organic growth.
- **Reach:** 77.8M (total Twitch following of users); 73M at 2025 year-end.
- **MAU:** 20%.
- **Data:** 2.6M+ manually categorized game relations (+51% in 2025); 413,000 written reviews (+60% in 2025).
- **Runway:** to 2027-06-24.
- Product roadmap: Profile Similarity (matchmaking/discovery), WTP-Drafter (backlog picker), Personalized Feed, and an MVP `rankone_bot` for Twitch/streamer chats (moving from passive extension to active live-engagement).
