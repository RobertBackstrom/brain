# K&G Community Monetization Plan v1

## 1. The honest revenue maths, first

This is the part worth reading before tier design, because it sets expectations.

Known community size, from the current deck: **several hundred active Discord members**, a
curated 50-player test group, thousands of games played, "no dropouts". Call the addressable
core **400**.

Paid-community conversion for a pre-release game with an engaged core runs **5 to 15%**.
Take the optimistic end.

| Scenario | Patrons | Avg / mo | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative (5% of 400) | 20 | $9 | $180 | $2.2k |
| Realistic (10%) | 40 | $11 | $440 | $5.3k |
| Optimistic (15%) | 60 | $13 | $780 | $9.4k |

Against that, one Ark Island contributor at Prateek's rate (475 SEK/h, roughly 80,000 SEK/month
full time) is about **$7,500/month**. To fund a single developer you would need roughly **700
patrons at $11**, which is more than the entire current community converting at 100%.

**Conclusion: at today's community size Patreon cannot fund development, and should not be sold
internally as if it could.** What it genuinely delivers:
1. **Proof of willingness to pay**, which is the single most useful number to hold before a
   Steam page and the most persuasive slide in any publisher or investor conversation.
2. **Retention** of a community that is currently engaged and monetised at zero.
3. **A funnel to instrument**, so the Steam launch is not a cold start.

Funding becomes realistic only after the funnel grows, which makes the tabletop forums the
strategic priority rather than the Discord. See section 4.

## 2. Hard dependency: kng-003

**No paid tier launches before the original art package exists.** A Patreon page is a public
commercial surface built almost entirely out of art: header, tier cards, cosmetic rewards,
every social post driving to it. Today all of that art is sourced GW material (`kng-001`).

Sequencing consequence: **art commission is not parallel to the GTM track, it is upstream of
it.** Section 6 sequences accordingly.

## 3. Tier design

Built from what Robert confirmed: build access, campaign participation, early access. Priced
in USD because Patreon's base is USD and the community is international.

| # | Tier | Price/mo | Contains |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Onlooker** | Free | Dev diaries, roadmap visibility, Discord role. The top of the funnel, not a throwaway. |
| 1 | **Gutter Runner** | $7 | Everything above, plus the **playable build**. The main conversion step. |
| 2 | **Gang Boss** | $15 | Build access, plus **persistent Forsaken Campaign participation**: a named gang, territory, ladder placement. |
| 3 | **Founder** | $35 | Above, plus name in credits, feature voting, a guaranteed launch key, and locked-in early-access pricing. |

Design notes:
1. **Build access at tier 1, not free.** It is the single strongest lever, and thousands of
   games already played prove the demand. Putting it at $7 makes the first step small.
2. **Campaign participation is the retention tier, and it is the smart one.** It sells the
   persistent layer rather than the client, so the recurring logic is honest: you are paying
   for an ongoing world, not re-buying software monthly. It also maps to what already retains
   these players.
3. **Cosmetics deliberately excluded from v1.** They are pure art spend and blocked on kng-003.
   Add them in v2 once the art package lands, as the natural tier-2 upsell.
4. **Founder is capped and time-boxed.** Scarcity is what makes a high tier convert, and the
   locked launch pricing gives it a concrete value rather than vanity alone.

## 4. Growth: the tabletop forums are the real channel

The Discord is the conversion surface. It is already close to saturated, so growth has to come
from outside it. Per the deck, the pools are Broheim moderators and Discord admins already
acting as advocates, tabletop fan sites, and a Mordheim subreddit with 3M+ views in a year.

The constraint: these are **other people's communities**, run by gatekeepers, and they are
hostile to overt commercial promotion. So:
1. **Lead with the campaign, not the Patreon.** The persistent world is the thing that is
   interesting to a forum. The paid tier is discovered afterwards.
2. **Named advocates over broadcast.** Moderators are already advocating. Give them Founder
   tier free, permanently. It costs nothing and buys the only distribution that works here.
3. **Never post the paywall into a community you do not own.** Post the campaign result, the
   dev diary, the ladder. Let people arrive.
4. **This is where the naming cleanup pays for itself.** These forums are exactly where a
   "1:1 Mordheim ruleset" claim spreads fastest and does the most damage with GW.

## 5. Open decision, not in this plan: the mod forum

Robert raised paid access to mod forums restoring a full Mordheim experience, nominally
disconnected from Ark Island. **Deliberately excluded from the tier table above** pending
legal review.

The concern in short: monetised routing to infringing material is inducement, and in the EU
linking for profit carries a presumption of knowledge of infringement (GS Media, Filmspeler,
Ziggo). It also undercuts the good-faith reading of the kng-003 cleanup, which is the main
asset we are about to buy.

**Recommended posture:** let the mod community exist independently, with no Ark Island
involvement, no link from any paid surface, and nothing behind the paywall. The community
benefit survives; the liability does not attach. Route to Lawyer before revisiting.

## 6. Sequence

**Phase 0, now, blocked on nothing.** Terminology cleanup in the deck and build: remove the
"1:1 with original Mordheim ruleset" line, rename Possessed and Promethium, drop the "Games
Workshop, please roll for initiative" slide, fix the dead pitch URL, re-attribute the
testimonials. Audit the 2026-07-17 teasers and already-aired submissions for sourced art.

**Phase 1, art commission (kng-003).** Key art, 3 to 5 gang plates, capsule set, Patreon tier
art. Brief already written: `brand/brand_guidelines_v1.md` sections 4 and 5.

**Phase 2, soft launch to the Discord only.** Tiers 0 to 3 live, no external promotion. Target
the realistic case, roughly 40 patrons. Instrument conversion rate as the number that matters.

**Phase 3, forum expansion.** Advocate seeding, campaign-led posts, no paywall posts. This is
the phase where funding either becomes plausible or is confirmed as a stretch.

**Phase 4, Steam.** Page goes live once the art is original and the naming is clean, carrying
a proven conversion rate and a warm list rather than a cold start.

## 7. What to measure

1. **Free-to-paid conversion rate.** The one number that transfers to a publisher conversation.
2. **Tier-1 to tier-2 upgrade rate.** Tests whether the persistent campaign genuinely retains.
3. **Monthly churn.** Above 10% means the campaign layer is not doing its job.
4. **Advocate-attributed signups**, to tell whether forum seeding works before scaling it.
