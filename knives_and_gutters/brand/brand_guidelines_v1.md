# Knives & Gutters - Brand Guidelines v1

Built by merging the Games Workshop-lineage Mordheim pitch with the current original-IP
K&G pitch, per Robert's call on 2026-08-31. Section 6 is the delta register: everything
that carried over from the licensed era and has to be replaced before anything ships.

**Read section 6 first.** It is the load-bearing part of this document.

---

## 1. What the brand is

Knives & Gutters is a PvPvE gang-skirmish RPG set in the ruined city of **Wissenstadt**,
in a region called **Forsaken**. It descends from tabletop skirmish gaming: warband
persistence, permadeath, consequential dice, campaigns that run for months.

**Positioning line (from the current deck, keep):**
> Victory should feel stolen, not given.

**Tone paragraph (from the current deck, keep):**
> Forsaken is a place where ogres collect debts, vampire lords don't bother to ask, and
> goblins think you look better in pieces. If your crew has the nerve to chase glory
> here, don't expect fairness, just the privilege of dying interestingly.

That paragraph is the single best piece of brand voice we own. It is original, it carries
no licensed terminology, and it has already been used externally (Second Wind submission,
2026-03-14). Treat it as the canonical tone reference.

---

## 2. Voice

1. **Dry, not gothic-purple.** The humour is deadpan and transactional ("ogres collect
   debts"). Never write cod-medieval or ye-olde register.
2. **Consequence over spectacle.** The pitch sells weight of decision, not scale of
   explosion. Copy should describe what a player loses, not what they unlock.
3. **Second person, present tense** for player-facing copy. "You keep what you can defend."
4. **Short declaratives.** The deck's strongest lines are five to twelve words.
   "Power is uneven, losses are permanent, and luck turns quickly."
5. **Never promise fairness.** Unfairness is the product, not a caveat.

**Words we own:** Forsaken, Wissenstadt, gangs, freelancers, the Forsaken Campaign,
Cycles of Chaos, Chapters of Chaos, town cryer, wanted lists, bounties.

## 3. Typography

| Role | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / display / body | **IM Fell DW Pica** | Already the de facto brand face: 2,716 uses across the current deck vs 308 Arial. Open licence (SIL OFL), free via Google Fonts, so no licensing exposure. 17th-century English punch-cut, irregular inking, period-correct. |
| Blackletter accent | *unresolved* | The GW-lineage deck used **Schoensperger** for headings (4 uses). See 6.7 before reusing it. |
| UI / functional | sans-serif, TBD | IM Fell is unreadable at UI sizes. The UI workstream needs a separate functional face. Not yet chosen. |

**Rules.** IM Fell for anything that wants to feel like a printed artifact (titles, section
heads, lore, store copy). Never letterspace it wide. Never set long body copy in it below
14px. It carries the brand on its texture, so do not clean up its irregularity.

## 4. Colour

No colour theme was ever authored: both decks carry stock Microsoft Office palettes
(#4F81BD accents and similar). The real palette is in the artwork. Sampled across the
current deck's key art:

| Role | Hex | Share | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground / ink black | `#000000` | 41.6% | Dominant field. Backgrounds, ink line, negative space. |
| Near-black | `#181818` | 9.3% | Panel fills, shadow bodies. |
| Parchment / paper | `#F0F0F0` → `#DBC681` | 9.1% | The lit ground. Aged toward ochre for anything "printed". |
| Aged gold / ochre | `#9B7C48`, `#7A5F38` | - | The one warm accent. Metal, candlelight, illumination. |
| Leather brown | `#604830`, `#675342` | 2.5% | Armour, straps, wood. |
| Cold stone grey | `#484848`, `#606060`, `#494D4E` | 6.2% + | Architecture, fog, dead light. |

**Rules.**
1. **Two-temperature discipline.** Cold grey environment, single warm ochre light source.
   Never a third hue family in the same frame.
2. **Black is a colour, not an absence.** Large true-black masses are correct and on-brand.
3. **No saturated spot colour.** No pure red, no cyan, no purple. If blood reads, it reads
   as rust brown, not `#FF0000`.
4. Ochre is the accent and it is scarce. It marks the thing the eye should land on.

## 5. Image style

The established look is **pen-and-ink grotesque on aged paper**: dense crosshatch, splashed
ink, deliberately imperfect register, figures pushed to caricature, skulls and chain and
tattered banner as recurring furniture, composition crowded to the edges with the light
carrying the read.

**Rules.**
1. Crowd the frame. Empty space is for the paper texture, not for breathing room.
2. Silhouette before detail. Every figure must read as a black shape first.
3. Imperfection is deliberate: off-register, ink spatter, foxed and stained paper.
4. Faces are characterful and ugly. No heroic idealisation.
5. Render lighting low-key, one warm source, deep falloff.

**Every current example of this style in our material is sourced GW art (confirmed
2026-08-31, see 6.1). The rules above are therefore a commissioning brief, not a description
of assets we hold.** An artist can be pointed at this section directly.

## 6. Delta register: licensed era → original IP

Robert's stated reason for holding Steam back is IP and trade-dress caution. This register
is that concern made concrete. Severity: **A** = blocks any public use, **B** = fix before
launch, **C** = tidy-up.

### 6.1 The deck art IS sourced Games Workshop material - CONFIRMED, severity A
**Confirmed by Robert, 2026-08-31.** Plates 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 14 and 27 in
`brand/deck_image_provenance_sheet.png` are **sourced GW / reference art**, not commissioned.
Item 11 (a photographed spread of the Mordheim rulebook) and items 07, 08, 09, 30 (painted
miniature photos) are in the same category and **must also be replaced before anything public**.

**Consequence: we currently own none of the artwork the brand is built on.**

What this does NOT mean: a visual *style* is not copyrightable. We can legally commission
original art that targets the same grimdark pen-and-ink register. Sections 4 and 5 of this
document stay valid as a *target*, and are in fact the brief for that commission.

What it DOES mean:
1. Every plate in the current deck is a **placeholder**. The deck is usable for private
   investor conversations at Robert's discretion, and for nothing else.
2. **No public surface can launch until an original art package exists.** That includes the
   Steam page, a Patreon or equivalent, social, and any festival or showcase submission.
   This is now the critical path for the whole go-to-market track, not a side task.
3. The logo work is less exposed: the round-3 concepts are original generations, and using
   Realm of Chaos (1988) as a *stylistic* anchor is legitimate. Do not trace it.

**Open exposure question, needs checking:** the teaser videos (`Knives&Gutters_Teaser1_*`,
2026-07-17) and the festival submissions already made (DevGAMM Gdańsk, Second Wind showcase
which aired 2026-04-23, TactiCon, CobraTekku) may already contain this sourced art. That is
material that is already public. Audit before assuming the exposure is only forward-looking.

Tracked as `kng-003`.

### 6.2 "Possessed" - severity A
Slide 7 of the current deck labels a 3D render "Possessed". That is a Games Workshop unit
name (Warhammer / Mordheim Possessed warband). Rename the unit and the asset.

### 6.3 "Promethium" - severity A
Slide 11's narrative event is "Activated Promethium". Promethium is Warhammer 40,000
terminology. It appears to have been swapped in to replace *wyrdstone*, which was itself
GW terminology, so the substitution moved from one GW term to another. Needs a genuinely
original substance name. This is a naming job worth doing properly since the substance is
the campaign's central macguffin.

### 6.4 "Inquisitor crackdown", "chaos spawn", "demon gates" - severity A/B
Slide 11. Inquisitor and Chaos Spawn are GW-loaded. "Cycles of Chaos" and "Chapters of
Chaos" are softer (chaos is a common noun) but sit adjacent to GW's Chaos and Space Marine
Chapters. Recommend renaming the two event names outright and keeping "Chaos" only as a
common noun.

### 6.5 "Feature set aligned 1:1 with original Mordheim ruleset" - severity A
Slide 14, current deck. This is a written statement that the product is an unlicensed
derivative of a specific GW game, sitting in a deck being shown to investors ten months
after GW formally declined the licence. Remove it. Rules systems are not copyrightable but
this sentence is an admission of copying, which is exactly what a licensor's lawyer reads.

### 6.6 Leftover GW-pitch furniture in the current deck - severity B
1. Slide 16 closes on **"Games Workshop, please roll for initiative"**. That line is still
   in the June 2026 deck.
2. Slide 16's "More Material" link is `https://arkislandstudio.com/demo/mordheim`, which is
   **dead (HTTP 404)** and still carries the Mordheim path.
3. Slide 16's six testimonials all name Mordheim and cite "Mordheim moderators" as sources.
   These are genuine quotes about the alpha, so the fix is attribution, not deletion:
   re-source them as tabletop-skirmish community quotes.
4. Slide 15, Robert's bio, still ends "an ideal fit for **Mordheim** in a self-publishing
   setup."

### 6.7 Schoensperger blackletter - severity C, verify
The GW-lineage deck used Schoensperger for display. Confirm its licence before it becomes
a brand face. Fraktur revivals are often free for personal use only. IM Fell is already
carrying the brand and is safely OFL, so the cheap answer is to not introduce a second
face at all.

## 7. What is deliberately not decided yet

1. **UI type and UI colour.** Depends on the UI workstream. IM Fell will not survive at
   UI sizes.
2. **Logo.** Round 3 concepts landed 2026-08-31 (`art/logo/concepts/round3/`), awaiting
   Robert's curation. The logo brief's two locked anchors are Realm of Chaos (1988) and
   the K&G metallic mockup. **Note:** the Realm of Chaos anchor is itself a GW book cover,
   so it is a legitimate *stylistic* reference but must not be traced.
3. **Character art pipeline.** Gen-AI is used for character base meshes with human-made
   final rigs, and that is disclosed in festival submissions. Keep the disclosure wording
   identical across submissions.
4. **Substance name** replacing Promethium (6.3).
