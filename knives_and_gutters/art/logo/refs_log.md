## Locked direction (re-confirmed)

- **Mark style:** illustrative wordmark with embedded ornament — *not* a clean wordmark, *not* a framed badge
- **Style language:** John Blanche / Realm-of-Chaos density + Gary Chalk crudeness, governed by 80s tabletop-cover tooling
- **Wordmark:** English only — "Knives & Gutters"
- **Composition:** two-line stack with blade-as-separator (from `kg_metallic_mockup.png`)
- **Detail level:** ornament density of `roc_logo02.png` — letters dissolving into figures, vines, skulls, weapons
- **Anti-references:** clean vector, AI-generic typography, modern 3D chrome, sci-fi sleek, Y2K, glossy plastic
