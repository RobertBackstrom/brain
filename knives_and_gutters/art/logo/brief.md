# Logo Brief — Knives & Gutters (v0)

## 1. Project context

Knives & Gutters is a **grimdark gang-skirmish, party-based online RPG (PvPvE)**, spiritually descended from Mordheim — warbands, ruined city, mutation, knives-out brutality. Co-developed with Jakob Koster. The mark needs to read as a **tabletop-era cover treatment**, not a modern game-tech logo.

## 2. What we need

- **Mark style:** illustrative badge / cover treatment (locked)
- **Wordmark:** English only — "Knives & Gutters"
- **Composition cue:** rectangular outer frame, blade-as-separator between the two words, hand-painted illustrative weapons / details flanking the type
- **Primary use:** Steam capsule, box-style cover art, website hero, merch
- **Variants required (at delivery):**
  1. Full-color on transparent
  2. White on transparent (for dark BG)
  3. Black on transparent (for light BG)
  4. Icon-only crop — one extracted element (per ptid-lp-logos pattern). Candidate elements: **outer frame motif** *or* a **single blade**. Either is acceptable; artist picks whichever reads strongest at 32px.

## 3. Tone & feel

Five anchor adjectives:

1. Grimdark
2. Hand-painted
3. Crude (in the Gary-Chalk sense — purposefully rough, not unfinished)
4. Weighty
5. 80s-tabletop

## 4. Must-haves

1. Every character of the wordmark is **individually designed and hand-painted** (per `roc_logo02` — top-priority ref)
2. Type uses 80s old-school metallic highlight technique (per WFRP corerules + pic5017352)
3. Real-ink outlines + painted highlights on illustrative elements (per kaltes-grottor + K&GLogo_FL1)
4. Coin-stamp feel on the wordmark (per BG2)
5. Blade-as-separator between "Knives" and "Gutters"
6. Outer rectangular frame
7. Legible at 32px (Steam capsule icon)
8. Holds up at 1:1 and 16:9 crop
9. Distinct silhouette
10. Rewards close inspection — detail density should reward extended looking

## 5. Must-avoid

1. AI-generic typography (the `ChatGPT Image 24 feb 2026` ref — keep its shape, drop its execution)
2. Clean / mechanical / vector-perfect linework
3. Trend-of-month stylings (chrome 3D, Y2K, glitch)
4. Effects achievable only with modern digital tooling — **govern by what was possible with 80s real-tooling** (ink, brush, gouache, paint)
5. Generic fantasy clichés (lone dragon, lone skull) — Mordheim-tone gang signifiers OK
