# Teef — Chinatown-gate concept · Flux prompts

**Purpose:** Teef pitch demo asset (within NDA Purpose). Shows Makosch AP's environment quality —
a Soho landmark rendered in the Teef client style guide at the atmospheric production quality of
our Knives & Gutters build.

**Fusion brief:**
- **Subject:** Soho Chinatown paifang gate (ornate Chinese archway, Wardour/Gerrard St entrance).
- **Style guide (Teef, Direction 02 "Casual" LONDON map screen, p.17):** stylized 2.5D isometric /
  elevated 3/4 game environment, semi-stylized 3D, brick London townhouses, red buses + phone box,
  neon shop signage, cobbles, white graffiti on shutters, orange gameplay target-rings, warm amber
  landmark glow against cool night.
- **Atmosphere (K&G actual build):** heavy volumetric fog, strong depth falloff, desaturated
  teal-green night gloom, warm fire/brazier accents, dramatic low-key single-source lighting.
- **Tool:** fal.ai Flux.dev (REST, FAL_KEY). 16:9 landscape. ~$0.025/img.

**Palette (hex, fed to Flux verbatim):** teal-green fog `#1e2e2b` / `#2a3f3a`, night-blue shadow
`#14202e`, warm amber `#e8a14b`, lantern red `#d22a2a`, neon magenta accent `#b03cff` (Teef, sparing),
orange target ring `#ff7a1a`, cobble grey `#3a3d40`.

**Shared style tail:** `stylized 2.5D isometric video game environment, semi-stylized 3D render of a
premium mobile game, elevated three-quarter camera, heavy volumetric atmospheric fog with strong
depth falloff, desaturated teal-green night gloom (#1e2e2b #2a3f3a) over night-blue shadow (#14202e),
punctuated by warm amber (#e8a14b) and lantern red (#d22a2a) practical lights, faint neon magenta
(#b03cff) signage glow, subtle orange (#ff7a1a) gameplay target rings on wet cobblestone (#3a3d40),
single dramatic low-key key light, deep soft shadows, faint white graffiti tags on shutters,
cinematic, high detail, soft ambient occlusion, painterly stylized textures, no text, no watermark`

## Variants
1. **Establishing / wide** — the paifang gate centered, flanked by narrow London brick townhouses
   with bay windows, red phone box + a red double-decker bus half-swallowed by fog behind, glowing
   red paper lanterns strung across the street, isometric establishing shot.
2. **Hero / closer** — the ornate Chinatown gate dominant, upturned tiled roofs and carved beams,
   dense fog behind it dissolving the street, lanterns and neon Chinese signage as the warm accents,
   one orange target ring glowing on the cobbles below, more atmospheric and dramatic.
3. **Deep-gloom variant** — same gate, pushed further into the K&G green-teal fog, fewer warm lights,
   a distant brazier fire as the only orange, maximum depth falloff and moody negative space.
