**(a) "Will we have enough time to iterate?" - strong YES, don't concede.**

**(b) "Is the 5-week build itself realistic with the lightest team?" - the real question. Answer honestly, then defend.**
This is where "cheapest + fastest" reads as "riskiest" to a buyer, so meet it head-on rather than waving it away.

Fewer people, all senior = less coordination drag, faster calls, no ramp-up. Light headcount is the *method*, not a corner cut.

- **Scope discipline is explicit.** M1 + first 30 seconds is non-negotiable polish; art depth on later missions is the planned flex. We protect the date by triaging scope, never quality.
