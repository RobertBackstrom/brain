## Commission an original art package. Nothing public ships until it exists.

Robert confirmed on 2026-08-31 that the illustration plates carrying the K&G brand are
**sourced Games Workshop / reference art**, not commissioned work (see `kng-001`). Also to be
replaced: the photographed Mordheim rulebook spread and the painted-miniature photos.

**We own none of the artwork the brand currently rests on.**

Style is not copyrightable, so the target look is still available to us. Sections 4 and 5 of
`brand/brand_guidelines_v1.md` are already written as a commissioning brief: sampled palette
(black ground, ochre `#DBC681` / `#9B7C48`, cold stone `#494D4E`, leather `#675342`),
pen-and-ink grotesque on aged paper, crowd the frame, silhouette before detail, low-key single
warm source. Hand that section to an artist as is.

### Blocks
1. Steam page (already being held for trade-dress caution, this is the concrete reason)
2. Patreon or equivalent community monetization, which needs art on day one
3. Social and any further festival or showcase submission
4. Brand guidelines v2 sign-off

### Audit first, this may already be public
The teaser videos (`Knives&Gutters_Teaser1_*`, 2026-07-17) and the submissions already made
(DevGAMM Gdańsk, **Second Wind showcase which aired 2026-04-23**, TactiCon, CobraTekku Dark
Dungeon Days) may already contain the sourced art. Check what actually went out before
assuming the exposure is only forward-looking. Retroactive exposure is a different
conversation from a forward plan.

### Scope to price
Minimum for a public launch surface: key art, 3 to 5 gang or faction plates, a store capsule
set, and Patreon tier art. The logo track is separate and already running (round 3 concepts
delivered 2026-08-31); it is less exposed because those are original generations.
