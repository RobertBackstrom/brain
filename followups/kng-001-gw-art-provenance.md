## Original question (kept for the record)

While sampling the brand palette out of `K&GPitch.pptx` (2026-06-11) I looked at the embedded
key art. The illustrations are pen-and-ink Warhammer plates in the unmistakable **John Blanche**
lineage, and at least one (`ppt/media/image2.png`) reads as a **scanned Games Workshop
illustration** rather than commissioned pastiche.

If that is right, the current investor deck, and any brand identity derived from its art, sits
on Games Workshop copyright. This is ten months after Owen Rees formally declined the Mordheim
licence (2025-10-03).

**What is needed:** Jakob and Fredrik confirm, per image, who made each plate in the deck.
Commissioned original in the Blanche *style* is fine. Scanned or traced GW artwork is not.
Anything in the second category gets replaced before it goes near a storefront or an investor.

This is the highest-value open question on the project and it cannot be answered from Drive.
