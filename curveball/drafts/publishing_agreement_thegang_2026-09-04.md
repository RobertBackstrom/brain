# Publishing Agreement

**Curveball**

This Publishing Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of last signature below, by and between:

**The Gang Studio AB**, reg. no. 559511-5568, having its principal place of business at Slakthusplan 3, 121 62 Johanneshov, Sweden (the "Developer"),

and

**Aurora Punks AB**, reg. no. 559256-9718, having its principal place of business at Timmermansgatan 43, 118 55 Stockholm, Sweden (the "Publisher"),

each individually a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## Background

A. The Developer has developed the game Curveball (the "Game") and owns, or holds the right to use, all intellectual property rights in the Game, its source code and its development tools.

B. The Game is substantially complete but has not been released commercially, and the backend it was built against is being retired.

C. The Publisher has rebuilt the project from source, placed it under version control and verified that it runs.

D. The Parties want the Publisher to finish the Game for PC, publish it on Steam and share the revenue the Game generates. The revenue structure set out in section 6 is the structure the Parties discussed and confirmed in writing on 30 June 2026.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Game"** means Curveball, including all builds, updates, patches, fixes and additional content released under this Agreement.

1.2 **"Steam App"** means Steam application ID 2805120, together with its store page, wishlists, followers, demo and event history.

1.3 **"Net Revenue"** means the amounts actually received by the Publisher from Valve Corporation for the Game, after Valve's share, refunds, chargebacks and platform taxes. No internal costs of either Party are deducted before the split in section 6.

1.4 **"Platform"** means PC through the Steam store. **"Territory"** means worldwide.

## 2. Grant of rights

2.1 The Developer grants the Publisher, for the term of this Agreement, the exclusive right to publish, distribute, sell, update and market the Game on the Platform in the Territory, and the right to make the technical changes described in section 3.1 for that purpose.

2.2 The grant is limited to the Platform. No other platform, format or media right is granted, and no ownership is transferred. Section 9 applies.

2.3 The Publisher may engage subcontractors to perform its obligations and remains fully responsible for their work.

## 3. Publisher undertakings

3.1 The Publisher shall complete the Game for PC. This includes replacing the dedicated servers with player hosted multiplayer, replacing the server authoritative backend with a service the Publisher operates, and the polish required for launch.

3.2 The Publisher shall publish the Game on Steam and manage the store page, updates and the release plan.

3.3 The Publisher shall run community work for the Game.

3.4 The Publisher bears its own costs. **No paid marketing is included, and neither Party commits to a media budget.** If either Party wants to spend on paid media, the Parties shall first agree in writing who pays and how the cost is treated.

## 4. Developer undertakings

4.1 The Developer shall grant the rights to the Game required for the Publisher to perform section 3, for PC worldwide, for the term of this Agreement.

4.2 The Developer shall carry out the transfer of the Steam App under section 5. Until the transfer is complete, the Developer shall give the Publisher user access to the Steam App and a Steam Web API publisher key for it.

4.3 The Developer shall give the Publisher admin or server access to the LootLocker game `a86igukp`, so that the Publisher can export the item catalogue, asset definitions and progression definitions.

4.4 The Developer shall answer technical questions about the code to a reasonable extent. **The Developer takes on no ongoing development obligation.**

## 5. Transfer of the Steam App

5.1 On signature of this Agreement, the Steam App shall be transferred from the Developer's Steamworks account to the Publisher's Steamworks account, using the app transfer tool built into Steamworks. The Publisher becomes the publisher of record.

5.2 Wishlists and followers are attached to the Steam App and transfer with it, as do the store page and the demo and event history.

5.3 Financial history does not transfer. The Developer keeps visibility over its own revenue from the period before the transfer, and the Publisher sees only the period after it. This follows from how Valve has built the tool and cannot be changed by the Parties.

5.4 The transfer requires a fully verified Steamworks account on both sides and Actual Authority for the initiating account. Each Party shall do what is needed on its own side without delay. No third party approval is required.

5.5 The Steam App returns to the Developer on termination, under section 12.

## 6. Recoupment and revenue share

6.1 The Publisher retains the first **100,000 SEK** of Net Revenue.

6.2 Net Revenue above that amount is shared **30 per cent to the Publisher and 70 per cent to the Developer, in perpetuity**.

6.3 No other amount is deducted before the split. Section 1.3 governs what Net Revenue is.

6.4 The split applies for as long as the Game generates revenue, subject to section 12.5.

## 7. Co-publisher

7.1 The Publisher may bring in one or more co-publishers, financing partners or marketing partners for the Game at its own discretion, without the Developer's consent and without any renegotiation of this Agreement.

7.2 **Any such party is paid solely out of the Publisher's own share under section 6.2.** The Developer's 70 per cent is not affected, and the recoupment amount in section 6.1 is not increased.

7.3 The Publisher remains solely responsible to the Developer for performance under this Agreement. The Publisher shall notify the Developer in writing when such a party is brought in.

## 8. Reporting, payment and audit

8.1 The Publisher shall report Net Revenue quarterly and pay the Developer's share within thirty (30) days of the end of each calendar quarter. The report shall state the amounts received from Valve, the deductions applied under section 1.3, any recoupment applied under section 6.1, and the resulting split.

8.2 Payments are made in SEK against the Developer's invoice, issued on receipt of the report.

8.3 The Publisher shall give the Developer user access to the Steam App in the Publisher's Steamworks account, so that the Developer can see sales data directly and check it against the report.

8.4 The Developer may, once per calendar year and at its own cost, have the underlying records audited by a certified public accountant. The Developer shall give at least ten (10) business days written notice, and the audit shall take place during regular business hours. If the audit shows a discrepancy to the Developer's disadvantage of more than five (5) per cent for the period audited, the Publisher shall pay the shortfall without delay and bear the cost of the audit.

8.5 Each Party is responsible for its own taxes on amounts it receives.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 The Developer remains the owner of the Game and of all underlying material, including the source code. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Game.

9.2 The Publisher acquires no ownership of the Game. The Publisher's rights are limited to those granted in section 2 and end when this Agreement ends.

9.3 The Publisher retains ownership of the general tools and services it has built that are not specific to the Game, for example its item grant service. The Developer receives a free, unlimited and perpetual licence to use those tools and services for the Game.

9.4 The Developer is responsible for ensuring that the Game and the material it provides do not infringe the rights of any third party.

## 10. Source code and hand-back

10.1 The Publisher keeps the source code in a private version controlled repository. The Developer's delivery of 4 June 2026 is kept untouched as a baseline, so everything the Publisher has added can be read out as a delimited set of changes.

10.2 The Developer may at any time request the complete current source code, including the Publisher's changes. The Publisher shall deliver it free of charge within ten (10) business days.

10.3 On termination of this Agreement for any reason, the delivery under section 10.2 happens automatically, without a request, within ten (10) business days.

## 11. Term and termination

11.1 This Agreement applies from signature. The revenue share in section 6 applies in perpetuity to the revenue the Game generates, subject to section 12.5.

11.2 If the Publisher has not launched the Game within **twelve (12) months** from signature, the Developer may terminate this Agreement by written notice. On such termination the Publisher's right to publish ends and the Publisher retains no revenue share.

11.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

11.4 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters bankruptcy or company reorganisation, or ceases to conduct its business.

## 12. Effect of termination

12.1 The Publisher's right to publish the Game ends, and the Publisher shall stop selling the Game within a reasonable time.

12.2 The Publisher shall transfer the Steam App back to the Developer within **thirty (30) days**, with wishlists and followers intact. The Publisher keeps visibility over the financial history of its own period, in the same way the Developer keeps its own earlier period, in accordance with section 5.3.

12.3 The source code is handed over under section 10.3.

12.4 Revenue accrued up to termination is reported and paid under section 8.

12.5 The revenue share in section 6.2 continues after termination for revenue the Game generates, **except** where this Agreement is terminated by the Developer under section 11.2 or for the Publisher's uncured material breach, in which case the Publisher's revenue share ends on termination.

12.6 Sections 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17 and 18 survive termination, and section 6 survives to the extent the Publisher retains a revenue share under section 12.5.

## 13. Console and mobile

13.1 This Agreement covers PC through Steam. Console and mobile versions are not included.

13.2 If the Developer decides that a console or mobile version is relevant, it shall notify the Publisher in writing. The Publisher then has **sixty (60) days** of exclusive negotiation on those platforms. If the Parties have not reached agreement within that period, the Developer is free to place them elsewhere.

## 14. Credit and marketing

14.1 The Game is credited to the Developer as developer and to the Publisher as publisher.

14.2 Both Parties may use the Game in their own marketing and reference lists.

## 15. Confidentiality

15.1 Neither Party shall disclose the terms of this Agreement or non-public information about the other Party's business or about the Game.

15.2 Disclosure is permitted to advisers, and to potential co-publishers, financing partners and marketing partners under an equivalent obligation of confidentiality, and where disclosure is required by law or by a stock exchange.

15.3 This section applies during the term and for three (3) years after termination.

## 16. Warranties and indemnity

16.1 Each Party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it.

16.2 The Developer warrants that it owns or controls the rights granted in section 2 and that the Game does not infringe the rights of any third party.

16.3 The Publisher warrants that it will perform its obligations professionally and comply with Valve's terms for the Steam App.

16.4 Each Party shall indemnify the other for documented loss arising from a breach of its warranties in this section, including reasonable legal costs. The indemnified Party shall notify the other without delay and shall not settle a claim without the other Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 17. Liability

17.1 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, loss of business or loss of data.

17.2 Section 17.1 does not apply to gross negligence, wilful misconduct or fraud.

## 18. General provisions

18.1 **Independent parties.** The Parties are independent contractors. Neither Party may bind the other.

18.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Section 7 is not an assignment and does not require consent.

18.3 **Amendments.** Amendments to this Agreement are valid only in writing and signed by both Parties.

18.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the Parties' entire agreement on its subject matter and replaces all earlier oral and written understandings, including the term sheet dated 31 August 2026.

18.5 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is responsible for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

18.6 **Severability.** If a provision is held invalid, it shall be limited or removed to the smallest extent necessary, and the remainder of this Agreement stays in force.

18.7 **Notices.** Notices are given in writing by email to joel.edstrom@thegangstudio.com for the Developer and to robert@aurorapunks.com for the Publisher.

18.8 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by Swedish law. Disputes shall be settled by the Stockholm District Court (Stockholms tingsrätt) as court of first instance.
