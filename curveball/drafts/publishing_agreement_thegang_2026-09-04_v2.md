# Publishing Agreement

**Curveball**

This Publishing Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of last signature below, by and between:

**The Gang Studio AB**, reg. no. 559511-5568, having its principal place of business at Slakthusplan 3, 121 62 Johanneshov, Sweden (the "Developer"),

and

**Aurora Punks AB**, reg. no. 559256-9718, having its principal place of business at Timmermansgatan 43, 118 55 Stockholm, Sweden (the "Publisher"),

each individually a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## Background

A. The Developer has developed the game Curveball (the "Game") and owns, or holds the right to use, all intellectual property rights in the Game, its source code and its development tools.

B. The Game is substantially complete but has not been released commercially, and the backend it was built against is being retired.

C. The Publisher has rebuilt the project from source, placed it under version control and verified that it runs.

D. The Parties want the Publisher to finish the Game for PC, publish it on Steam and share the revenue the Game generates. The revenue structure set out in section 6 is the structure the Parties discussed and confirmed in writing on 30 June 2026.

E. The Parties confirm that the work the Publisher carried out on the Game before the date of this Agreement was carried out with the Developer's consent and is covered by this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Game"** means Curveball, including all builds, updates, patches, fixes and additional content released under this Agreement.

1.2 **"Steam App"** means Steam application ID 2805120, together with its store page, wishlists, followers, demo and event history.

1.3 **"Net Revenue"** means the amounts actually received by the Publisher, or by a Publisher Group Company on the Publisher's behalf, from Valve Corporation for the Game, after Valve's share, refunds, chargebacks and platform taxes. Net Revenue also includes amounts received for the Game from other sources, including sales of Steam keys through resellers and bundle revenue, on the same basis. No internal costs of either Party are deducted before the split in section 6.

1.4 **"Platform"** means PC through the Steam store. **"Territory"** means worldwide.

1.5 **"Publisher Group Company"** means Creation Zero Point Holding AB, reg. no. 559182-7471, and any other company under common control with the Publisher. The Publisher's Steamworks partner account is operated within the Publisher's group by Creation Zero Point Holding AB.

1.6 **"Effective Date"** means the date of the last signature to this Agreement.

1.7 **"Launch"** means the Game being made available for sale to the general public on the Steam store, whether in early access or as a full release.

1.8 **"Post-Termination Revenue"** means the amounts actually received by the Developer, or by any party publishing or distributing the Game with the Developer's authority, from sales and other commercial exploitation of the Game on personal computers after termination of this Agreement, after the storefront's share, refunds, chargebacks and platform taxes.

## 2. Grant of rights

2.1 The Developer grants the Publisher, for the term of this Agreement, the exclusive right to publish, distribute, sell, update and market the Game on the Platform in the Territory, and the right to make the technical changes described in section 3.1 for that purpose.

2.2 The grant is limited to the Platform. No other platform, format or media right is granted, and no ownership is transferred. Section 9 applies.

2.3 The Publisher may engage subcontractors, and may perform its obligations through a Publisher Group Company, and remains fully responsible for their work. The Publisher remains the Developer's sole counterparty under this Agreement.

2.4 During the term the Developer shall not itself publish, distribute, sell or otherwise make the Game available on any storefront for personal computers, and shall not licence or authorise any third party to do so. This applies whether or not the version concerned includes the Publisher Contributions.

2.5 The Developer grants the Publisher a non-exclusive licence to use the name, logo and other trade marks of the Game to the extent needed to publish and market the Game under this Agreement.

## 3. Publisher undertakings

3.1 The Publisher shall complete the Game for PC. This includes replacing the dedicated servers with player hosted multiplayer, replacing the server authoritative backend with a service the Publisher operates, and the polish required for launch as the Publisher reasonably determines.

3.2 The Publisher shall publish the Game on Steam and manage the store page, updates and the release plan. The Publisher decides the release date, the release form, pricing and discounting for the Game.

3.3 The Publisher shall use reasonable efforts to run community work for the Game.

3.4 The Publisher bears its own costs. **No paid marketing is included, and neither Party commits to a media budget.** If either Party wants to spend on paid media, the Parties shall first agree in writing who pays and how the cost is treated.

## 4. Developer undertakings

4.1 The Developer shall grant the rights to the Game required for the Publisher to perform section 3, for PC worldwide, for the term of this Agreement.

4.2 The Developer shall carry out the transfer of the Steam App under section 5. Until the transfer is complete, the Developer shall give the Publisher user access to the Steam App and a Steam Web API publisher key for it.

4.3 The Developer shall give the Publisher admin or server access to the LootLocker game `a86igukp`, so that the Publisher can export the item catalogue, asset definitions and progression definitions.

4.4 The Developer shall answer technical questions about the code to a reasonable extent. **The Developer takes on no ongoing development obligation.**

## 5. Transfer of the Steam App

5.1 On signature of this Agreement, the Steam App shall be transferred from the Developer's Steamworks account to the Publisher's Steamworks partner account, which is operated within the Publisher's group by Creation Zero Point Holding AB on the Publisher's behalf, using the app transfer tool built into Steamworks. The transfer shall be initiated within five (5) business days of the Effective Date and each Party shall complete the steps required of it without delay. The Publisher becomes the publisher of record and remains solely responsible to the Developer under this Agreement.

5.2 Wishlists and followers are attached to the Steam App and transfer with it, as do the store page and the demo and event history.

5.3 Financial history does not transfer. The Developer keeps visibility over its own revenue from the period before the transfer, and the Publisher sees only the period after it. This follows from how Valve has built the tool and cannot be changed by the Parties.

5.4 The transfer requires a fully verified Steamworks account on both sides and Actual Authority for the initiating account. Each Party shall do what is needed on its own side without delay. The transfer is made through Valve's own tool and requires no approval other than Valve's processing of the request.

5.5 The Steam App returns to the Developer on termination, under section 12.

## 6. Recoupment and revenue share

6.1 The Publisher retains the first **100,000 SEK** of Net Revenue.

6.2 Net Revenue above that amount is shared **30 per cent to the Publisher and 70 per cent to the Developer, in perpetuity**.

6.3 No other amount is deducted before the split. Section 1.3 governs what Net Revenue is.

6.4 The split applies for as long as the Game generates revenue, subject to section 12.5.

6.5 The revenue share in section 6.2 is deferred consideration for the Publisher's investment of work in completing and publishing the Game. It is not consideration for continuing services, it is not terminable by notice, and it continues regardless of the term of this Agreement, subject only to section 12.5.

## 7. Co-publisher

7.1 The Publisher may bring in one or more co-publishers, financing partners or marketing partners for the Game at its own discretion, without the Developer's consent and without any renegotiation of this Agreement.

7.2 **Any such party is paid solely out of the Publisher's own share under section 6.2.** The Developer's 70 per cent is not affected, and the recoupment amount in section 6.1 is not increased.

7.3 The Publisher remains solely responsible to the Developer for performance under this Agreement. The Publisher shall notify the Developer in writing when such a party is brought in.

## 8. Reporting, payment and audit

8.1 The Publisher shall report Net Revenue quarterly and pay the Developer's share within thirty (30) days of the end of each calendar quarter. The report shall state the amounts received from Valve, the deductions applied under section 1.3, any recoupment applied under section 6.1, and the resulting split.

8.2 Payments are made in SEK against the Developer's invoice, issued on receipt of the report. Payment made by a Publisher Group Company on the Publisher's behalf discharges the Publisher's obligation.

8.3 The Publisher shall give the Developer user access to the Steam App in the Publisher's Steamworks partner account, so that the Developer can see sales data directly and check it against the report. That access is limited to the Steam App and gives no visibility of any other title in that account.

8.4 The Developer may, once per calendar year and at its own cost, have the underlying records audited by a certified public accountant. The Developer shall give at least ten (10) business days written notice, and the audit shall take place during regular business hours. If the audit shows a discrepancy to the Developer's disadvantage of more than five (5) per cent for the period audited, the Publisher shall pay the shortfall without delay and bear the cost of the audit.

8.5 Each Party is responsible for its own taxes on amounts it receives.

8.6 If refunds or chargebacks are received after a quarter has been reported, the Publisher may set the amount off against later quarters. A quarter without revenue is reported as nil.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 The Developer remains the owner of the Game and of all underlying material, including the source code. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Game.

9.2 The Publisher acquires no ownership of the Game. The Publisher's rights are limited to those granted in section 2 and end when this Agreement ends.

9.3 The Publisher retains ownership of the general tools and services it has built that are not specific to the Game, for example its item grant service. The Developer receives a licence to use those tools and services for the Game, free of charge for as long as this Agreement is in force and for as long as the Publisher retains a revenue share under section 12.5, and thereafter on the Publisher's then applicable commercial terms.

9.4 The Developer is responsible for ensuring that the Game and the material it provides do not infringe the rights of any third party.

9.5 The Publisher retains ownership of the copyright in the code, assets, tools and documentation it creates under this Agreement (the "Publisher Contributions"). The Publisher grants the Developer a licence to use the Publisher Contributions as part of the Game for the term of this Agreement, and after termination on the terms of section 10.4. Section 9.1 is read subject to this section.

## 10. Source code and hand-back

10.1 The Publisher keeps the source code in a private version controlled repository. The Developer's delivery of 4 June 2026 is kept untouched as a baseline, so everything the Publisher has added can be read out as a delimited set of changes.

10.2 The Developer may at any time request the complete current source code, including the Publisher's changes. The Publisher shall deliver it free of charge within ten (10) business days. Source code delivered under this section may be used by the Developer for verification, security and continuity purposes only. While this Agreement is in force the Developer shall not exploit it commercially on any platform, and shall not authorise a third party to do so.

10.3 On termination of this Agreement for any reason, the delivery under section 10.2 happens automatically, without a request, within ten (10) business days.

10.4 Where this Agreement is terminated other than under section 11.2 or for the Publisher's uncured material breach, the licence in section 9.5 becomes perpetual and irrevocable on termination. Where this Agreement is terminated under section 11.2 or for the Publisher's uncured material breach, the Developer may use the Publisher Contributions only against payment of the Publisher's documented development cost for them, or, at the Developer's option, the Publisher shall deliver the source code without the Publisher Contributions.

## 11. Term and termination

11.1 This Agreement applies from the Effective Date. The revenue share in section 6 applies in perpetuity to the revenue the Game generates, subject to section 12.5.

11.2 If the Publisher has not Launched the Game within **twelve (12) months** from the Effective Date, the Developer may terminate this Agreement by written notice given within thirty (30) days of the end of that period. The right lapses on Launch and on the expiry of that notice period. The twelve month period is extended day for day by (a) any delay caused by the Developer, including any delay in completing the transfer under section 5 or in providing the access under sections 4.2 and 4.3, (b) any period during which the Publisher has suspended work or distribution because of a third party claim covered by section 16.2, and (c) any event of force majeure under section 18.5. On termination under this section the Publisher's right to publish ends and the Publisher retains no revenue share.

11.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

11.4 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters bankruptcy or company reorganisation, or ceases to conduct its business.

## 12. Effect of termination

12.1 The Publisher's right to publish the Game ends, and the Publisher shall stop selling the Game no later than on completion of the transfer under section 12.2.

12.2 The Publisher shall procure that the Steam App is transferred back to the Developer within **thirty (30) days**, with wishlists and followers intact. The Publisher keeps visibility over the financial history of its own period, in the same way the Developer keeps its own earlier period, in accordance with section 5.3.

12.3 The source code is handed over under section 10.3.

12.4 Revenue accrued up to termination is reported and paid under section 8.

12.5 Where this Agreement is terminated other than under section 11.2 or for the Publisher's uncured material breach of section 3.1, 3.2 or 8, the Publisher's revenue share continues after termination. From termination the Developer shall pay the Publisher thirty (30) per cent of Post-Termination Revenue, and any part of the amount in section 6.1 that the Publisher has not yet retained shall first be paid to the Publisher out of Post-Termination Revenue. Sections 8.1, 8.2 and 8.4 then apply with the Parties reversed, so that the Developer reports and pays and the Publisher may audit, and the Developer shall give the Publisher user access to the Steam App for that purpose.

12.6 Sections 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17 and 18 survive termination. Section 8 survives termination and applies as set out in section 12.5, and section 6 survives to the extent the Publisher retains a revenue share under section 12.5.

## 13. Console and mobile

13.1 This Agreement covers PC through Steam. Console and mobile versions are not included.

13.2 If the Developer decides to develop, publish or licence a console or mobile version, or receives an offer to do so, it shall notify the Publisher in writing. The Publisher then has **sixty (60) days** of exclusive negotiation on those platforms. If the Parties have not reached agreement within that period, the Developer is free to place them elsewhere.

## 14. Credit and marketing

14.1 The Game is credited to the Developer as developer and to the Publisher as publisher.

14.2 Both Parties may use the Game in their own marketing and reference lists.

## 15. Confidentiality

15.1 Neither Party shall disclose the terms of this Agreement or non-public information about the other Party's business or about the Game.

15.2 Disclosure is permitted to advisers, and to potential co-publishers, financing partners and marketing partners under an equivalent obligation of confidentiality, and where disclosure is required by law or by a stock exchange.

15.3 This section applies during the term and for three (3) years after termination, and as regards payment and reporting details for as long as payments continue under section 12.5.

## 16. Warranties and indemnity

16.1 Each Party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it.

16.2 The Developer warrants that it owns or controls the rights granted in section 2 and that the Game does not infringe the rights of any third party.

16.3 The Publisher warrants that it will perform its obligations professionally and comply with Valve's terms for the Steam App.

16.4 Each Party shall indemnify the other for documented loss arising from a breach of its warranties in this section, including reasonable legal costs. Where a third party claims that the Game infringes its rights, the Developer shall at its own cost defend the claim and shall indemnify the Publisher for damages, settlements and reasonable legal costs. The indemnified Party shall notify the other without delay and shall not settle a claim without the other Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The Publisher may suspend distribution of the Game while such a claim is pending. Such suspension is not a breach of this Agreement and does not count towards the period in section 11.2.

## 17. Liability

17.1 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, loss of business or loss of data.

17.2 Section 17.1 does not apply to gross negligence, wilful misconduct or fraud.

17.3 Amounts payable under section 6 or section 12.5 are direct loss for the purposes of this Agreement, and section 17.1 does not exclude a claim for them.

## 18. General provisions

18.1 **Independent parties.** The Parties are independent contractors. Neither Party may bind the other.

18.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Section 7 is not an assignment and does not require consent, and neither is the Publisher's performance through a Publisher Group Company under section 2.3.

18.3 **Amendments.** Amendments to this Agreement are valid only in writing and signed by both Parties.

18.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the Parties' entire agreement on its subject matter and replaces all earlier oral and written understandings, including the term sheet dated 31 August 2026.

18.5 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is responsible for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

18.6 **Severability.** If a provision is held invalid, it shall be limited or removed to the smallest extent necessary, and the remainder of this Agreement stays in force.

18.7 **Notices.** Notices are given in writing by email to joel.edstrom@thegangstudio.com for the Developer and to robert@aurorapunks.com for the Publisher. Notice of termination under section 11 shall in addition be sent to the receiving Party's registered address as stated at the top of this Agreement.

18.8 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by Swedish law. Disputes shall be settled by the Stockholm District Court (Stockholms tingsrätt) as court of first instance.

18.9 **Successors.** The Developer shall not sell, assign, transfer or exclusively licence the Game, the Steam App or the intellectual property rights in the Game unless the transferee first undertakes in writing to the Publisher to be bound by sections 6, 8 and 12.5 as if it were the Developer. The Developer remains liable for the performance of those sections if it does not obtain that undertaking. The Developer shall notify the Publisher in writing of any change of control of the Developer within ten (10) business days.
