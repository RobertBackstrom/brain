## 3. The recoup

Each side recovers **its own** carried cost at **2,5x** on release, in first position, pro rata
between the two if net revenue is short. Nobody recovers anyone else's and nobody is paid twice for
the same hour.

| | Carried, 12 mo | Recoup at 2,5x |
|---|---|---|
| **Scenario A**, AP takes the developer seat (Oskar) | Rift 204 000 · AP 180 000 | Rift 510 000 · AP 450 000 · **960 000** |
| **Scenario B**, Rift takes the developer seat (Chris or Dima) | Rift 210 000 · AP 180 000 | Rift 525 000 · AP 450 000 · **975 000** |

Two things about the shape are worth knowing before Victor asks. **The developer seat is close to
cost-neutral either way**: Chris at 50 percent costs 50 500 against 50 000 paid, and Oskar at
50 percent costs 40 000 against 50 000 paid, so on that seat AP is slightly ahead. Which means on
these allocations **Rift's risk is almost entirely Jesper's seat and AP's is almost entirely
Robert's**. Each side is carrying its own principal rather than padding a number, and that is the
honest answer if the model is challenged.

## 4. Why not the literal version

Robert's instruction read literally is full real cost times 2,5. That is **4 725 000** to recover
from revenue on top of the 1 860 000 already paid in cash, which needs 90 865 units, roughly
**252 000 wishlists at launch** against 2 741 store followers today. It never pays out, and asking a
financier for 4,7 MSEK of revenue participation on a 2,16 MSEK project whose own analysis puts year
one net at 562 000 would cost credibility on everything else in the plan. Robert took the
under-recovery version instead.

## 6. AP's 30 percent, and the answer to Robert's question

**Has LUG been offered this before? The multiple yes, the rest no.**

1. The gated page LUG holds and the Discord proposal both say: **Aurora Punks defers 300 000,
   recouped at 2,5x, 750 000, on release, then 15 percent revenue share.** A 2,5x recoup is already
   on the table and is not new.
2. **A 30 percent revenue share has never been offered.** It is double what they have seen.
3. **A recoup based on real cost has never been offered.** The base LUG has seen is a defined
   300 000. The v4 base is 390 000, a modest move. The rejected literal version would have been
   1 890 000, six times it.
4. The 2025 proposal to Hing floated "a hybrid model, lower or no monthly fee plus recoup and
   revshare, once scope is known" but never put numbers to it.

**So the aggressive part is the 30 percent, not the recoup.** Going from 15 to 30 doubles what
leaves the project before the financiers see anything, on the one number the whole plan already
rests on. It needs arguing explicitly rather than appearing in a term sheet: AP bills 20 000 a month
for an executive producer whose real cost is 35 000, it originated the deal, it wrote the plan, and
it holds both counterparty relationships. That is a real case. It is also a doubling, so expect it
to be negotiated, and decide in advance what AP will settle at.

**Robert's decision: the 30 percent stays inside AP and is not mentioned to Rift yet.** The Rift
material therefore describes Rift's return as cash plus the 2,5x recoup and says nothing about a
revenue share in either direction. That is a normal prime-and-partner asymmetry and the deck states
nothing false. Note the exposure though: **if Rift later learns AP holds 30 percent of net while
Rift holds none, it will read as withheld rather than as never theirs.** Worth deciding when that
gets said rather than letting timing decide it.

## 6b. The UI artist reservation, added 2026-09-08

Robert: *"I think we should set aside money and time for at least 2 months of UI work from an
artist. This could be deducted from the UX time slot the latter part of the project."*

Icon and HUD asset production was never designer work and had no line in the budget. It is now
carried **inside the design seat**, so no number in section 2 changes:

- The design seat runs at **60 % of full time through month 5**, then **30 % from month 6**, once
  the loop is settled and the role is mostly product ownership.
- That frees **2,1 months** of the seat (0,3 x 7 months).
- **Two of those months go to a UI artist**, half time across **months 6 to 9**, producing HUD
  assets and icons against the phase 1 designs, in the build before public playtest 2 in month 8.
- Monthly cost to the project is unchanged: 55 000 paid, 72 000 real cost, 17 000 carried by Rift.

**The caveat that has to be honoured.** An artist's rate is not a designer's rate. The freed 2,1
seat-months buy two artist-months only if the artist costs roughly what the designer does. If the
artist is cheaper the reservation stretches, if dearer it shrinks. Settled in the review month
against Rift's own rates, and the fallback is fewer artist weeks rather than a bigger number. Do not
let this become a mid-term budget increase request.
