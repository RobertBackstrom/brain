# Why this exists

Four facts have been sitting in our material as open questions since 26 August, described as
"things the review month answers" rather than as things anyone asked for. Two of them are one-line
requests. One of them, the wishlist count, is worth more than every other assumption in the
commercial model put together: it sets the whole sales band, and the recoup crossing point is
expressed in wishlists.

Robert flagged this 2026-09-07: an unknown that a single question resolves should be a question,
not a permanent caveat on a pitch page.

## The draft

> Hey Anthony, Magnus,
>
> Four things I need from your side before we lock the review month, all of them stuff you have and
> we don't.
>
> 1. **Current wishlist count.** This is the big one. Our whole sales model runs off it and right
> now we are deriving it from the follower count, which gives a band four times too wide to plan
> against. One number off Steamworks.
> 2. **Playtest data from the two playtests.** Session length, tutorial completion rate, and where
> players quit. Even rough numbers change what we prioritise first.
> 3. **The current build**, not the public demo. The demo is the August 2025 one and the December
> refactor changed a lot.
> 4. **Engine and version**, plus whatever repo access you can arrange. We have costed the netcode
> work as a range because nobody on our side has read a line of it.
>
> One and two we can use immediately. Three and four are what the review month starts on.
