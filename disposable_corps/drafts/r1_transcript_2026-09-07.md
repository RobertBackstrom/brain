STRATEGIC INSIGHTS
The "Death Spiral": Multiplayer-only games face a unique failure mode. If a player buys the game and cannot find a match within 3–5 minutes, they often refund immediately. This creates a negative feedback loop where low CCU drives higher refund rates, further lowering CCU.
The $15-20 Barrier: At this price point, players expect a level of polish and server stability comparable to mid-tier "AA" titles. Without a marketing budget, your primary acquisition tool is the Wishlist-to-Sale conversion (typically 10-20% over the first year).
Matchmaking Health: While a 1v1 game can survive on 50 CCU, a 5v5 game requires 10 active players per match. To ensure multiple matches are running across different skill levels and regions, 500 global CCU is generally considered the "emergency floor."
