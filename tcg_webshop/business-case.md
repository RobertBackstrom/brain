# TCG Webshop — Business Case (one-page verdict)

**Date:** 2026-06-17 · **Ticket:** tcg-001 (epic mkt-000, Personal Listings) · **Frame:** personal-economy side venture, not billable
**Sources:** `web-scan.md` (grading market + legal + competitor scan, 2026-06-16)

## The call: **Conditional GO - but reframe what "the webshop" is for.**

Don't build a destination webshop to sell raw Pokémon singles. As a singles store you'd be competing head-on with **Cardmarket**, which owns EU liquidity, traffic, and price transparency - margins on commodity singles are razor-thin and the traffic isn't yours to win. That version is a no.

The GO is a **brand + service hub**, not a primary sales channel: a small curated storefront for **graded / premium / sealed** inventory, fronting a **Nordic grading-concierge service**, while commodity singles flow out through **Cardmarket + eBay** via the Lister pipeline you're already building. The webshop is the shop window and trust layer; the marketplaces are the till.

## Why (the 4 reasons)

1. **Singles retail is a losing fight; the margin is in graded + service.** Cardmarket already does cheap singles better than a standalone shop can. The defensible, higher-margin lanes are graded-card sales and the grading concierge (broker submissions to PSA-Frankfurt / Beckett-Germany, batch Nordic volume intra-EU, retail attach around the slab). See `web-scan.md`.
2. **A marketplace-integrated EU grading brand already failed** (Cardmarket Grading, dead 2024) - so don't grade in-house, and don't try to out-Cardmarket Cardmarket. Broker trusted slabs instead; that's the only durable shape.
3. **The infrastructure mostly already exists.** Personal Listings (mkt-000) is building the Tradera/eBay listing backbone; Step 2 is Pokémon. A webshop that piggybacks that pipeline + a Shopify-class storefront is low-overhead and fits the personal-economy frame. No new entity, no inventory war-chest.
4. **Timing is genuinely good for the service angle.** PSA Frankfurt opens its dealer channel July 2026 and Beckett is already grading in Germany - the Nordic "drop your cards, we handle grading + sell the slab" concierge has a real, fresh window before someone else takes that seam.

## The one real risk to respect
Low margins + inventory cash tied up + counterfeit/mis-grade liability. Mitigated by: staying asset-light (sell owned + consigned cards first, not a bought inventory), brokering not grading, and an insurance/authentication step before touching customer cards.

## Recommended first move (cheap, this is the test)
1. **Validate the grading-concierge demand before building commerce infra.** Stand up a landing page (the existing concept at pitch.runatyr.games/tcg-shop is the seed) with a Nordic "grade your Pokémon cards via us, batched to Frankfurt" waitlist. Cost ~zero, tells you if the service has pull.
2. **Keep selling owned cards now** through Lister → Cardmarket/eBay; that's live cashflow and real margin data with no new build.
3. **Park the full webshop build** until (1) shows demand. If the waitlist converts, build the storefront around graded/premium + concierge - not singles.
4. **Rename before anything public** - "MasterCard" has a hard Mastercard trademark conflict (route to Lawyer).

**Bottom line:** Yes to a Pokémon presence, no to a singles webshop. Build the brand + Nordic grading-concierge hub on top of the Lister pipeline, test the concierge demand for ~zero cost first, and let the marketplaces carry the volume.
