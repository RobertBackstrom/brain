## SLIDE 2 — The Mission

Intent: show we understand the assignment before talking about ourselves.

What you have asked for, as we read it from your 12 August feature list:
- Four new heists adapted from PAYDAY 3: Turbid Station, 99 Boxes or Touch The Sky, Syntax Error, Under The Surphaze
- The four Heist Royale stages upgraded
- Progression rework: EXP to currency, free skill and weapon selection
- Five new weapons
- Stealth ingredient expansion
- Shield enemy, one more difficulty
- Restart and partial escape
- UI/UX overhaul
- PUBG engine and 3C changes

The creative challenge:
Two IPs whose players want different things. Our own audience work says the crossover affinity
runs through PAYDAY 2, not PAYDAY 3: PD2 carries roughly 30k concurrents against PD3's ~900, and
PD2 shows a 4x affinity index against PD3's 1.25x. The PUBG base is exactly neutral on stealth
and strong on loud co-op PvE and SWAT-style tactical play. That is a sequencing and positioning
argument, not a case for cutting stealth.

The technical challenge:
Engine and 3C work inside PUBG, in Unreal, with multiplayer and live-service constraints, and a
cert candidate that has to be in Krafton's hands at month 10.

The delivery challenge:
23 roles from a standing start to peak inside four months, two IP holders in the approval chain,
and 12 months to Gold. That is a scheduling problem before it is a staffing problem.

## SLIDE 7 — Ways of Working

Intent: embedded to collaborate, autonomous enough to deliver.

Ownership and decisions:
- One producer owns the plan and is your single point of contact
- Game direction sits with our design lead, working to your creative approval at milestones
- We bring decisions to you with a recommendation, not a menu

Communication:
- Leads on site at Birger Jarlsgatan 61, the rest of the team hybrid. Both studios are Stockholm-based
- Milestone reviews at each of the eight gates, weekly written status in between
- One shared backlog, visible to you

Risk and mitigation, the four that matter:
1. **Ramp to 23 people inside four months.** Both studios are naming people now rather than recruiting later. The month 1–2 ramp is deliberately shallow so the seniors set the foundations before the team lands
2. **Two IP holders in the approval chain.** Milestone gates are set against Krafton's cert candidate date at month 10, and we work back from it rather than towards it
3. **Technical integration with the PUBG engine.** Dmitry was on Irons 1. Month 1 is technical mapping, and the plan is re-cut against what it finds rather than defended
4. **Scope growth.** Every milestone carries a named deliverable list. Additions are re-planned in the open, with a number attached
