# Project Irons 2 — dev brief

**26 Aug 2026 · internal, do not forward outside AP**

## What it is

Starbreeze are building a second PAYDAY x PUBG crossover mode, funded by Krafton. The first
one shipped inside PUBG as "New Mode: PAYDAY" (Heist Royale). Internally everyone calls it
Irons. We are pitching to co-develop the second installment.

We are **not signed yet**. The pitch went in on 18 August, I met Tobias Remmers (PAYDAY
Franchise Director) plus their engine and level design leads on Monday, and they are now
setting up contractor accounts and source access for our side. That is a good sign but it is
not a contract, so treat everything here as confidential and don't plan your autumn around it
yet.

The shape we proposed: **September as a paid evaluation month**, contracted on its own, so
neither side commits to thirteen months on estimates made before anyone has seen the trunk.
If it goes ahead, the team starts small in September and ramps from there.

## The feature list

This is Tobias's own list from 12 August, his words, comparing what exists in Heist Royale
today against what they want in the second one. Red items are their open questions, blue are
their notes.

**Stages.** Currently Road Rage, Nightclub, Bank, Diamond District. Pitched: Turbid Station,
99 Boxes, Syntax Error, Under The Surphaze. *Open: replacing 99 Boxes with Touch The Sky.*
The new heists are generally bigger and more complex than the first set, and they are
estimating on the assumption of some redesign and complexity removal.

**Weapon and skill acquisition.** Today 5 fixed classes. Pitched: EXP converted into virtual
currency, freely selectable skills and weapons, loadout customization. *Open: how many new
skills.*

**3Cs and movement.** PUBG 3C maintained with movement changes. Pitched: **PUBG engine
modifications required, collaboration with the PUBG team required.** Risks involved, scope
TBD. This is the line to read twice.

**Weapons.** Today 25 existing PUBG weapons fixed to classes. Pitched: Rotary Gun, Grenade
Launcher, Akimbo (or a secondary GL), Thanatos .50 Cal, Flamethrower. *Open: Akimbo vs another
GL.*

**Weapon balance and gunplay.** Improve combat balance, adjust weapon stats, improve weapon
feel. Explicitly **not** a full gunplay overhaul with new animations.

**Difficulty.** Two levels today (Normal, Hard). One additional level.

**AI enemy.** Today Dozer, Cloaker, Sniper. Adding a Shield enemy, specced from TREATY colors,
integrated with AI spawn groups.

**UI/UX.** Improvements to HUD, safehouse menu, level completion screen, in-game guidance,
marketing key art and more. Should match the look and feel of the PAYDAY games.

**Restart / return system.** Today every heist ends by returning players to the main menu, no
restarts, and every player has to reach a specific spot to escape. Pitched: restart/return
system and partial escape, so fewer than 4 players can escape. *Will likely need guidance from
Krafton.*

**Stealth mechanics.** Today a suspicion counter rising from kills and cameras, plus body
disposal in dumpsters. Pitched: lockpick and safecracker minigames, WIFI hacking, QR code,
motion and floor sensors, security drone, laser detection. They are deliberately avoiding the
expensive PAYDAY-style systems: no security rooms, lures, detection bar, guard search state,
pagers or SO animations.

**Onboarding and tutorial.** Today mechanics are explained as images in a safehouse menu.
Tutorial updates for stealth, weapons, skills and difficulty.

**Game mechanics.** Scope and complexity still to be defined against the final heist list.
They may remove high-complexity side objectives such as the loot bag scanner. Ziplines are
already out. *If Touch The Sky replaces 99 Boxes, degrading loot goes too.*

**Cosmetics.** Today 4 sets and 8 masks. Cosmetics, especially masks, imported from PAYDAY 3
as directly as possible.

**HR1 stage updates.** New systems such as stealth mechanics added to the four existing
heists. *Open: adding new heist features such as Termite.*

## Their milestone shape

First Playable, Pre-Alpha, Alpha 1, Alpha 2, Beta, Gold, Support. Roughly two months each
except Gold and Support at about one. Their own headcount sketch peaks at 30 to 40 through
Alpha and Beta. Ours is a good deal leaner and that is deliberate, which is what the pitch
argues.

Starbreeze provide VO and music. QA sits outside the dev headcount.
