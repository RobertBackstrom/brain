## What this is
Vincent ("Donvino") runs **simsum**, the studio behind *Gamestonk Simulator: Gone Rogue*. He sent
Robert the **Crack a Pack publisher deck** on Discord 2026-08-10 before shopping it to publishers,
asking for a read. This project covers that relationship: the Crack a Pack deck, the older
GameStonk marketing conversation, and anything that follows.

Crack a Pack is an incremental collectathon on Steam: crack booster packs, place cards on a mat,
earn coins, burn cards for prestige, complete the set.
