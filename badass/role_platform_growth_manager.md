## What the role does (endorsed definition)
Owns **audience and user growth for BADASS's own platform and 1st-party products** — the Steam/console games and the AR app — as opposed to per-project client marketing. Concretely:
1. Community building around the 1st-party titles
2. Converting broadcast audiences into owned users (wishlists, installs, retention)
3. Storefront and partner channels that drive platform adoption
4. Go-to-market for platform releases

## Cost-center placement
Sits on the **Platform (runway / Dell Capital-funded)** side of the model, not Customisation — because it grows the product **we own**, not a client deliverable. This is consistent with the v3 rule that Platform is runway-funded (BadassXR core + 1st-party products) while Customisation is client-cost-recovered.
