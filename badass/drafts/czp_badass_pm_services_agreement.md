# **PROJECT MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Project Management Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date of the last signature below by and between:

**Creation Zero Point Holding AB**, a Swedish corporation with company registration number 559182-7471, with registered office at c/o NeCo Software, Brännkyrkogatan 10B, 118 20 Stockholm, Sweden (hereinafter “Creation Zero Point Holding” or “the Contractor”)

and

**BADASS STUDIOS LIMITED**, a UK corporation with company registration number 12906303 and registered office at 256 Martin Way, Morden, England, SM4 4AW (“Client”).

Contractor and Client are hereinafter referred to as the **“Parties”** and each individually as a **“Party”**.

# **1. SERVICES**

**1.1 Scope**
The Contractor shall perform project management services for the Client's BADASS XR platform and its customisation work, performed at the Client's direction (the “Services”), including but not limited to: Jira backlog audit and estimation; roadmap and resource-demand modelling; staffing and P&L planning support; Jira project structure design and implementation; sprint and standup participation; and related project-management coordination. The work is performed remote.

**1.2 Performance**
The Contractor will perform the Services with a high degree of skill, care, and professionalism as expected of a leading professional in its industry.

**1.3 Core Personnel**
The Services shall be performed by Robert Bäckström as Project Manager.

**1.4 Subcontracting**
The Contractor may engage qualified subcontractors upon prior written notice to the Client; the Client’s consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. The Contractor remains fully responsible for subcontractor performance and for ensuring written agreements covering confidentiality and IP assignment consistent with this Agreement.

**1.5 Governance and Working Hours**
Each Party shall appoint a Point of Contact (PoC) authorised to give and receive day-to-day directives. The Contractor's PoC is Robert Bäckström; the Client's PoC is rosemary@badass-studios.com. Unless otherwise agreed, availability is Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET/CEST. Nothing herein shall be construed to control the manner or means of performance; the Contractor remains an independent contractor.

# **2. COMPENSATION**

**2.1 Service Fee**
As full and complete consideration for the Services, the Client agrees to pay the Contractor as follows:

(a) *Services performed 22 March 2026 to 20 May 2026.* A fixed fee of **GBP 2,600**, covering 40 hours of PM services, itemised in the Engagement Time Summary provided to the Client. The Parties acknowledge that approximately 20.8 further hours of PM services were delivered within this period beyond the 40-hour fixed scope; these additional hours were provided by the Contractor at no charge, as a goodwill contribution, and are not invoiced.

(b) *Services from 21 May 2026 onward.* Charged at **GBP 65 per hour**, invoiced monthly in arrears on actual hours worked. The Contractor will provide a line-item time record with each invoice.

All payments are contingent upon the Client’s acceptance and the Contractor supplying the Services in a timely and skilful manner. The Client will not be responsible to reimburse the Contractor for any expenses unless pre-approved in writing.

**2.2 Additional Services**
Services materially beyond the scope in Section 1.1, or any other resources and their corresponding fees, shall be agreed upon in writing by the Parties prior to commencement of such work.

**2.3 Taxes; Currency; Late Payment**
Fees are exclusive of VAT and similar taxes. Where applicable, reverse charge shall apply and the Client shall account for VAT. Payments shall be made in **GBP**, in full without set-off or deduction other than legally required withholding; if withholding applies, the Client shall gross-up so that the Contractor receives the net amount that would have been payable absent withholding. Payment terms: **Net 10 days from invoice date**. Late payments accrue interest at the lesser of 1.0% per month or the maximum permitted by law, plus reasonable collection costs.

# **3. OWNERSHIP OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

**3.1 Background IP; Residuals; License Back**
As between the Parties, the Contractor retains all right, title and interest in and to any pre-existing or independently developed tools, templates, frameworks, methodologies, and generalised patterns (“Contractor Background IP”). No ownership in Contractor Background IP is assigned to the Client. To the extent Contractor Background IP is included in the Deliverables, the Contractor grants the Client a worldwide, perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in or necessary to use the Deliverables. The Contractor may freely use its general skills, ideas, concepts, know-how, and techniques retained in unaided memory (“Residuals”) for any purpose, provided the Contractor does not disclose Client Confidential Information.

**3.2 Deliverables IP Assignment**
Except for Contractor Background IP and Residuals, ownership of Deliverables transfers to the Client upon full and final payment of all fees due under this Agreement.

**3.3 Moral Rights**
The Contractor irrevocably waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law, any and all moral rights in the Deliverables. This waiver does not apply to Contractor Background IP or Residuals.

# **4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE**

**4.1 Confidential Information** means any information of whatever kind that becomes known to the Contractor as a result of and during the course of performing the Services, regardless of source, except: information that is or becomes public other than through breach; information customarily disclosed without restriction; information known to the Contractor before the Services commenced; information obtained from a third party other than through breach; and information whose disclosure is required by judicial or administrative order or applicable law.

**4.2 Non-disclosure**
During the term of this Agreement and for five (5) years thereafter, except for trade secrets which remain protected as long as they retain trade secret status, the Contractor shall maintain all Confidential Information in strict secrecy, guard against unauthorised disclosure, and shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information except with the Client’s prior written approval. The Contractor shall not disclose the terms of this Agreement without the Client’s prior written consent.

**4.3 Return of Confidential Material**
Upon termination, the Contractor shall deliver to the Client all documents, data, and records containing Confidential Information in the Contractor’s possession or control.

# **5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP**

The Parties agree that the Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of the Client. The Client shall determine the general nature of the work; the Contractor shall determine the means by which it accomplishes the work. The Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes, insurance, and fees arising from its activities, including income tax, social security charges, and VAT. The Contractor and its personnel are not entitled to any employee benefits of the Client.

# **6. REPRESENTATIONS OF CONTRACTOR**

The Contractor represents to the Client that: it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; there are no contracts or understandings with third parties that preclude full performance of the Services; during the term it will not enter into any such conflicting contracts; and all intellectual property provided pursuant to this Agreement is the original work of the Contractor or is used pursuant to a disclosed license or assignment.

# **7. INDEMNIFICATION**

The Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold the Client, its officers, directors, shareholders, agents, and employees harmless from any and all claims, losses, damages, and reasonable costs (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising out of or relating to the Contractor’s Services, provided that the Client (i) promptly notifies the Contractor in writing of the claim, (ii) gives the Contractor sole control over the defence and settlement at the Contractor’s option and expense, and (iii) cooperates reasonably at its own expense. The Contractor shall not enter into any settlement imposing obligations on the Client without the Client’s prior written approval, not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

# **8. REMEDIES FOR BREACH**

The Contractor acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may give rise to irreparable injury to the Client not adequately compensable by monetary damages. In the event of an actual or threatened breach, the Client shall be entitled to injunctive relief or other equitable remedy, in addition to any other remedy available including recovery of monetary damages.

# **9. ASSIGNMENT**

This Agreement shall not be transferred or assigned by the Contractor without the Client’s prior written consent. The Client may assign this Agreement provided the assignee assumes all of the Client’s obligations to the Contractor. This Agreement is binding on and inures to the benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

# **10. NOTICES**

All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and deemed delivered when delivered in person or upon receipt if sent by email to:

* Client: BADASS STUDIOS LIMITED, Attention: Rosemary Lokhorst, rosemary@badass-studios.com
* Contractor: Creation Zero Point Holding AB, Attention: Robert Bäckström, robert@aurorapunks.com

Each Party shall give prompt written notice of any change to its notice details.

# **11. TERM AND TERMINATION**

**11.1 Term**
This Agreement commences on the date of the last signature below and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section. The Parties acknowledge that PM Services have been performed by the Contractor since 22 March 2026; this Agreement governs those Services, and the compensation for them as set out in Section 2.1, retroactively from that date.

**11.2 Termination for Cause**
Either Party may terminate for cause upon 30 days’ written notice specifying the breach, unless cured within that period.

**11.3 Termination for Convenience**
Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 30 days’ prior written notice. Fees for Services performed up to the termination date remain payable.

**11.4 Survival**
Sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11.4 and 12 survive termination.

# **12. GENERAL PROVISIONS**

**12.1 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement contains the entire agreement between the Parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions on that subject matter. For the avoidance of doubt, this Agreement is independent of, and does not continue, assume, or amend, any agreement between the Client and any other party.

**12.2 Amendment.** This Agreement shall not be modified except by mutual written agreement between the Parties.

**12.3 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force; an invalid provision shall be construed and enforced as limited so as to be valid.

**12.4 No Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision shall not be construed as a waiver of the right to subsequently enforce it.

**12.5 Applicable Law.** This Agreement shall be construed according to the laws of Sweden, and both Parties irrevocably submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of Sweden, with the District Court of Stockholm (Stockholms tingsrätt) as first venue, in the event of a dispute.

**12.6 Headings.** Headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect construction.

**12.7 Binding Effect.** This Agreement is binding upon and inures to the benefit of the Parties and their respective representatives, successors, and permitted assigns.
