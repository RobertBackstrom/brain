Make London read as London. Keep you in control of the numbers.

// Mapping & tuning

// Mapping & London render

Rather than a runtime map SDK, we reconstruct Soho from open geo data and stylise it - offline, no per-load map cost, as the brief requires.

  • Source, then bake. Soho footprints, roads and heights from open data (OpenStreetMap / Ordnance Survey) reconstructed as 3D geometry, baked into the build. Fully offline, no per-load runtime map cost.
  • Reads-as-London. Regent and Oxford Street as navigational spines, Georgian terraces, black cabs and red phone boxes, stylised Soho facades. Neighbourhood feel, inspired by Soho rather than street-accurate - the efficient route for a CPI/D3 test, and the fidelity your updated brief calls for.
  • Stylise on top. Tech art, AI-assisted, in your chosen direction: real-feel massing and recognisable landmarks, not generic extrusions. Buildings communicate location and gameplay, not architectural accuracy.
  • Transparency & fade. Tall buildings that block the player fade out; bordering areas fade with distance.
  • Licensing clean. Open data (OSM under ODbL, OS OpenData), no runtime mapping dependency. Mapbox kept only as a reference.

// Tuning workflow

Your Google Sheet stays the single source of truth. We build a thin, safe push to the live game.

  • A validated push: schema-checked with a dry-run diff, so a bad value never reaches the live build.
  • The game pulls config on launch, so you re-balance economy, progression and skill values live, with no new build.

Live data is home turf: we have shipped LiveOps and remote-data backends on PlayFab, LootLocker and AccelByte.

07 Questions & answers /////

Your questions for the final round.

Straight answers to the questions you sent ahead of our call.

Q1Is five weeks realistic, and does it leave enough time to play-test and refine?

Yes. The five weeks is the build, not the runway to launch. With soft launch at the end of Q4, a build delivered in the autumn leaves a long tail for tuning, and because economy, progression and difficulty run off remote config, that tuning needs no new build from us.

Inside the build we put a playable on device every Friday, with Mission 1's core loop front-loaded so the feel is proven early, not discovered late. Where the moment-to-moment feel needs more hands-on iteration, we add optional hardening weeks (Q2).

Q2What happens if it needs more iteration, or over-runs the fixed price?

The fixed price means the overrun risk sits with us, not you. €85k covers the defined five-week build whatever our hours come to.

If you want more room to iterate on feel, we add it in weekly blocks: €4,870 per hardening week, up to four weeks, staffed by the same core team that built the game at reduced capacity, so there is no ramp-up:

  • Tech Lead, gameplay & network (Fredrik Laurent) - 50%
  • Platform engineer & UI (Oskar Hansen) - 50%
  • Concept & tech art (Hasan Chenari) - 50%
  • Production / EP (Robert Bäckström) - 25%

Focused on core-loop feel and live-metric response. Anything needed beyond those four weeks is on us. Your cost is capped; the risk past that point is ours.

Q3Is the team locked in for the full project?

Yes. The named senior team is committed for the build and any hardening weeks. We would prefer an October kickoff, and can bring it forward to September if you want more room against your end-Q4 window.

Q4What is the team's mobile and free-to-play experience?

Strong on mobile and shipped-game craft, with real free-to-play exposure, and we will be straight about where the depth sits.

Mobile: we built and shipped Hooja, a live, ads-monetised mobile title, and BlockEm across Steam and browser. Free-to-play: our team worked on The Finals, Embark's free-to-play arena shooter (release management, commerce guidelines and QA); our production lead has shipped F2P on console; and our design advisor brings additional F2P design depth. Our strongest suit is senior build craft and a fast, art-led mobile pipeline, backed by live soft-launch experience with Hooja, which is the shape this test needs.

Q5What are the expected costs for geo data, AI tooling, music, QA hardware, Play Console and the Unity license?

Most are either near-zero or already covered in our price. There is no stack of surprise line items on top of the €85k.

  • Geo data - open-licensed (OpenStreetMap / Ordnance Survey), effectively zero and clean to use.
  • AI tooling - covered in our price, not a separate cost to you.
  • Music - our recommendation is original music from our in-house composer: 4 tracks at €3,000 added to the budget, rights-clean and yours outright on delivery. Licensing existing tracks instead would likely cost more, with rights clearance on top. Either way it is a transparent line, not buried in the build.
  • QA device hardware - covered in the €85k (coverage in Q6).
  • Google Play Console - Aurora Punks already holds a developer account, so nothing to set up.
  • Unity engine license - our build-seat licenses are covered in our price. Post-handover, the ongoing engine license for the shipped product sits with you as the owner, standard for any engine.

The only genuine third-party pass-throughs are billed at cost, with no markup.

Q6Which devices do you cover for QA?

We cover the hardware inside the €85k. Standard co-dev mobile coverage for a soft launch, weighted to your Argentina market:

  • Min-spec floor - budget devices (Samsung Galaxy A14, Motorola Moto G, Xiaomi Redmi 12; Android 12 to 13, 4 GB, MediaTek). The performance floor we protect.
  • Mid-tier - where most soft-launch players sit (Samsung Galaxy A34 / A54, Redmi Note 13, Pixel 7a; Android 14).
  • High-end - performance ceiling and newest OS (Samsung Galaxy S23 / S24, Pixel 8; Android 15).

Across both Snapdragon and MediaTek chipsets, Android 9 (min) to 15, and a spread of screen sizes and aspect ratios. The final list is agreed with you at kickoff.

We would prefer an October kickoff, which fits best around our current slate. If you would rather have more cushion against the end-Q4 soft launch, we can bring it forward to September. Both land your Q4 window - October is our preference, September if you want the extra schedule room.
