01 The studio /////

A senior team that ships competitive multiplayer at scale.

Aurora Punks is a Stockholm-based co-development and co-publishing studio, founded in 2019. "Developers for developers." We are roughly 20 people in Stockholm plus partner studios across Belgrade, Copenhagen and London, and we work full cycle, from prototype through release, porting and live operations.

Flightball is a real-time, 5v5, physics-driven competitive sport. That is exactly the kind of game our people have shipped before: online multiplayer, networked physics, Unreal Engine delivery, cross-platform release and live service. We plug a senior team into a partner's project and deliver against a defined scope and date.

  • Networked multiplayer is core. Our people have built and shipped online multiplayer under live-service load, including Unreal titles like Ready or Not (online co-op, UE4) and The Finals (UE5). Real-time networked play is the discipline at the centre of Flightball.
  • Deep Unreal Engine expertise. Shipped UE4 and UE5 gameplay, engine and porting work, with deep Unreal expertise across the engineering bench, so the build sits on an engine we run in production rather than one we are learning.
  • An existing simulation and game-tech base. We bring a proven technology foundation to the build rather than starting from zero, which is the single biggest factor in de-risking the schedule.
  • Console and cross-platform shipping. Including certification and porting across PC, console and VR.
  • Live-ops and a tunable economy. We run free-to-play games on remote config, so match balance, progression and the in-game economy are tuned live with the community, no code release or new build for each change. The numbers stay in your hands while the live build stays protected.
  • Data-driven, not opinion-driven. We instrument the build for the metrics that decide a free-to-play game (acquisition cost, early retention, conversion) and test visual and feature directions against real player data before the money goes behind them.
  • Full-cycle delivery. Prototype, vertical slice, release and post-launch updates, staged so a first crude playable can be ready alongside the live showcase.
  • Time zone. Central European, one hour ahead of the UK, with full working-day overlap.

04 Technology & IP /////

Clean ownership, on both sides.

The question your investors will ask is who owns what, and the answer here is clean on both sides. Formula Drone owns Flightball: the sport, the brand and all live-sport rights. Aurora Punks builds and owns the game itself and grants Formula Drone a clean lifetime licence to it, with full freedom to exploit the game and its IP. We arrange all of the underlying technology inside the project, so Formula Drone is never tied to any single toolset or supplier.

How Flightball is structured

Formula Drone owns
  • Flightball itself: the sport, its rules and formats
  • Arena and drone designs
  • The Flightball brand and all live-sport rights
  • A clean lifetime licence to the game, with full freedom to exploit the IP
Aurora Punks brings
  • The game itself: built, owned and supported by Aurora Punks
  • All the underlying technology, arranged and licensed by us within the project
  • The senior team that builds and ships the game

The detailed IP and technology framework is being formalised in the partnership agreement between Formula Drone and Aurora Punks AB. The principle is settled: Formula Drone owns Flightball and holds a clean lifetime licence to the game with full freedom to exploit the IP; Aurora Punks builds and owns the game and arranges the underlying technology within the project, so Formula Drone is never locked to a particular toolset.

05 Why this de-risks the build /////

The reasons a funder should be comfortable.

// Proven, not speculative

An existing simulation and game-tech base means the hardest parts are not invented from scratch. The team has shipped networked, physics-driven multiplayer before, at AA scale.

// Staged to the rounds

Development is phased so each stage is funded before it starts, from a first crude playable for the showcase through to a multi-platform release. Spend tracks the raise, not ahead of it.

// One accountable partner

A single senior studio owns delivery end to end, with a named producer and weekly cadence, rather than a loose group of contractors.

// Clean structure, clean IP

Formula Drone owns the sport and the brand and holds a lifetime licence to the game, with the IP boundaries clear on both sides. The structure is built to read cleanly for your investors.

06 Built to grow /////

PC first, then console and mobile.

The build is planned PC-first, where the competitive drone-sport audience is most active and the eSport scene starts. Console and mobile are not a rewrite later, they are the natural next step at 1.0: the same networked codebase, ported and certified across platforms, widening the player base exactly when the marketing push behind 1.0 lands.

  • Console at 1.0. Certification and porting are work our team has shipped before, sequenced after a stable PC release so the game lands on console proven, not raw.
  • Mobile as reach. A mobile client extends the free-to-play funnel to the largest audience and feeds the same live economy, the platform where casual fans meet the sport.
  • One codebase, many storefronts. Cross-platform from the architecture up, so every new platform compounds the install base instead of fragmenting the team.
