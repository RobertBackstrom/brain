The story

Old Aurora Punks IP, now instant-play in the browser.

Block'Em! shipped on Steam in September 2022. Cat Shawl Games built it, Aurora Punks published with IndieArk on board. A party game built around one idea: simple controls, complex outcomes. Place a block, jump on it, push your opponent off it.

Three and a half years on, it found a second life. The game is now live on Wavedash - instant-play in the browser, no install, no download. Drop a link in Discord, four people click, you're in a match.

For a party platformer that lives or dies by friction-to-first-match, that's the unlock.

What's new

No install. No download. Click a link, drop into a match.

The Wavedash launch is the reason this page exists. Block'Em! is a party game - its job is to get four people into the same lobby with as little friction as possible. Steam needs an account, an install, and a working controller setup. Wavedash needs a link.

That changes how the game gets shared. A Discord drop in a group chat now gets you a match in 30 seconds, on whatever laptop is open. Sit-down-on-someone's-couch multiplayer without anyone having bought it yet.

The game itself didn't change. The path to the first match did.
