01 The company

What you are buying into

Aurora Punks AB, org.nr 559256-9718, is the live operating and contracting entity. It is the company that signs the co-development and publishing agreements, and it is the equity in question here.

It is deliberately lean. One salaried role, a contracted senior network on top, and no fixed studio overhead. Capacity scales with the projects rather than ahead of them.

The recurring floor

The question behind "current monthly revenue and how long does it run" is really about durability, so here it is plainly. The company runs on a recurring floor of roughly 290 000 SEK per month, from two streams:

  • The Netlight placement, ~150 000 SEK/month. Open-ended, no fixed end date, invoiced monthly against a signed sub-consultant agreement.
  • Co-development margin, ~140 000 SEK/month. Currently the Raw Fury project, which completes at Gold in December 2026. This line is continuous by design: it is replaced by the next co-development contract rather than falling away.

Average monthly cost is approximately 185 000 SEK. The company is cash-generative across the full year.

04 Disclosures

Open items you should price in

These are stated up front rather than left to be discovered. Each one is live as of today.

1. The 2025 balance sheet carries a negative equity position, and a kontrollbalansräkning obligation is live.

A write-down of the Vessels of Decay intangible takes equity below the statutory threshold as at 31 December 2025. A settlement with Almi in February 2026 produced a gain of 1 404 468 SEK, which restores the position, but it falls in the 2026 financial year and therefore cannot repair the 2025 balance sheet.

On 3 September 2026 the auditor required corrections that reduce equity by approximately a further 326 000 SEK across the two years. That consumes the margin the board's July plan relied on. The question of how equity is restored is open with the accountant and the chair, and must be settled before the 2025 annual report is signed. Under Swedish company law (ABL chapter 25) equity must be restored to the full registered share capital of 198 086.50 SEK by the second control meeting to avoid a liquidation obligation.

2. The shipped catalogue is not inside Aurora Punks AB

BlockEm, Chenso Club, Tears of Adria, Ooglians and 1993 Space Machine were owned by APDS and were acquired out of the bankruptcy estate by Creation Zero Point Holding, not by Aurora Punks AB. An investment into AP captures that catalogue only if CZP contributes or licenses the IP into AP, which is part of the structure proposed below rather than an assumption.

3. Royalty obligations on two catalogue titles

Under the 2023 termination agreements, Curve receives 50% of net receipts on BlockEm and Chenso Club, and 75% of net receipts on ancillary deals until its unrecouped investment is recovered. The catalogue is not 100% owned revenue and should not be valued as such.

4. Operational bookkeeping runs through CZP

Aurora Punks AB has no separate 2026 fiscal year in the accounting system; the year is booked via CZP and allocated. This is a cleanup item for an equity transaction, and it is on the list.

5. Trademark position

The game title BLOCK EM is a registered trademark owned by Aurora Punks AB. The company name "Aurora Punks" itself has no registration on file. A clearance and registrability search is recommended rather than presented as complete.

6. One contingent claim

There is no current litigation and no tax enquiry against Aurora Punks AB. One contingent item exists: a possible final-payment claim from Dark Riviera relating to the Robot Lord Rising comic series. It has not been received and is not quantified.

05 Capability

The network

The company's real asset is a senior Unreal development bench it can stand up on demand, without carrying it as payroll between projects. For a studio in pre-production, this is capacity that can be deployed immediately rather than hired.

  • Core. One salaried engineer placed with Netlight, plus founder and executive producer.
  • The full Raw Fury co-development team, currently shipping and proven together.
  • Partner studios. Red Marmoset, Malformation, and two intermediate Unreal developers at Lost Hive.
  • Senior crew available from the former APDS team. Technical director (ex-Fatshark), release and UGC, release and compliance.
  • Eternal Minds and Ark Island as contracted partner teams.

Shipping credits across that pool include Ready or Not, The Finals, Helldivers 2, Darktide and Ghost Signal, alongside Aurora Punks' own catalogue.

07 Structure

How you get to 30%

Two legs. You buy the early minority holders' shares directly from them, and you subscribe for new shares in the company with money that would otherwise have gone in on top of the purchase price.

Leg 1, buying existing shares

The whole tail below Alexander Bergendahl is available: six to eight holders, 55 999 shares, 14.14% of the company. They came in between 2020 and 2022 and have had no exit path in five years. Roughly 4 486 305 SEK ($459 000), priced per seller rather than as a single company price. The money moves directly from you to them; the company is not a party and the balance sheet is untouched.

Leg 2, a new issue to you

89 790 new shares at 152 SEK each ($15.54), which is 13 648 080 SEK ($1 395 000) paid into Aurora Punks. The 152 SEK subscription price is the registered price from the last completed round in August 2024, so it is a matter of record rather than a negotiating position. It requires a shareholder resolution at two-thirds majority, which any new issue would require regardless.

Why this costs less than buying shares alone

Buying the shares outright and then funding the company separately runs to roughly 21.9M SEK ($2.23M), of which the second tranche buys no additional ownership. This structure reaches the same 30% for 18.1M SEK ($1.85M), a saving of about 3.7M SEK ($380 000). It is the same money doing the same work, except that it counts towards the holding instead of being spent on top of it. It also means a smaller total facility to finance, which makes approval easier rather than harder.

What the new issue does inside the company

  • Clears the related-party owner loans and cleans up the balance sheet, which is what a lender wants to see going in.
  • Funds the co-development pipeline that carries the recurring revenue floor forward.
  • Fills the contingency reserve and the prototyping fund.
