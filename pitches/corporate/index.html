00 How to read this /////

Three asset classes, one operating company.

Aurora Punks AB (org nr 559256-9718) is the live entity. Everything below either sits in it today or is being contributed into it as part of the current restructuring, and each line says which.

  • Equity holdings. Minority positions in four other companies: two UK development studios, a Swedish QA and release-management company, and a Danish studio. These are financial holdings, not consolidated subsidiaries. Aurora Punks does not control any of them.
  • Owned IP. Ten titles and codebases. Part of this catalogue sits in the founder's holding company today and is being contributed into Aurora Punks with no cash consideration.
  • Revenue-share and publishing agreements. Six signed, two in negotiation. These are income rights on titles that other studios own and operate.

Two former subsidiaries, Aurora Punks Development Services AB and White Lines Black Spaces AB, went through bankruptcy and are wound down. Both are disclosed and ring-fenced from the live company. Nothing in this document sits in either estate.

05 What is not in here /////

The exclusions, stated plainly.

A list of assets is only useful if it is also honest about what is missing. These are deliberately excluded.

  • Robot Lord Rising. Legally owned by Runatyr AB, not by Aurora Punks. Aurora Punks holds a 5 MSEK interest-free receivable and a right to a future purchase, which is an agreement to sell rather than a transfer of title. It is a title to acquire, not an asset to count.
  • Tears of Adria and Knives & Gutters. Ark Island's IP. Aurora Punks' interest is the publishing revenue share under negotiation in section 03, nothing more.
  • Gravity Circuit. No stake. The revenue share was sold back to the developer by the former subsidiary.
  • The Elric of Melniboné option. Lapsed. No value.
  • Runatyr AB. Held 50 % by the founder's holding company on paper, contested and unexecuted. Not an Aurora Punks asset and kept out of the perimeter.
  • Aurora Punks Development Services AB and White Lines Black Spaces AB. Both bankrupt and wound down. Historical group revenue sat in these entities, which is why the consolidated history looks larger than the live company. The going-forward business is the operating company described here.
06 Structure /////

Who owns the top.

Aurora Punks AB has 396 173 shares outstanding in a single share class, with no liquidation preference, no anti-dilution ratchet and no employee option pool. Two holders sit above 30 %, two more between 11 and 13 %, and the remaining 14 % is spread across seven smaller holders who came in early.

The board is five people: Mattias Wiking as chair, Alexander Bergendahl, Andreea Chifu, Karl-Magnus Troedsson and Robert Bäckström. Signing authority is two in combination.
