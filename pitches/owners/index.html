00 The short version /////

What changed since February.

  • The ALMI debt is gone. We paid 500 KSEK, ALMI wrote off 1 404 468 kr, and in April they confirmed the guarantee is released. Aurora Punks has no bank debt and no institutional debt.
  • We signed Raw Fury to co-develop Kingdom Two Crowns: Pharaoh Lands, a 5,6 MSEK contract across seven milestones. Three are delivered, the last of them on Friday. Gold in December.
  • 2026 runs to roughly 8,1 MSEK of production value and a +1,25 MSEK result, on one employee.
  • The shareholder loans start amortising 30 July.
  • The 2025 annual report will show negative equity from a write-down. The ALMI write-off falls in 2026 and covers it, so no shareholder contribution is needed.
01 Raw Fury and Kingdom /////

What we are building for Raw Fury.

The big thing that happened since February is Raw Fury.

On 29 April we signed a long-term co-development agreement with them to build the final expansion (DLC) for Kingdom Two Crowns, Raw Fury's own franchise. Aurora Punks owns design, art, programming and production. Raw Fury handles publishing, marketing and porting.

The contract is 5,6 MSEK across seven milestones, running April to December 2026. In July Raw Fury named the DLC: Kingdom Two Crowns: Pharaoh Lands.

02 The ALMI settlement /////

The debt is settled and written off.

The debt to ALMI was carried at roughly 1,9 MSEK, on an amortisation plan the company could not service. We negotiated it down to a single payment of 500 KSEK - which is what your loans funded in February.

ALMI wrote off the rest: 1 404 468 kr. In April they confirmed in writing that the guarantee undertaking Aurora Punks had given is released.

So the balance sheet today: no bank debt, no institutional debt, no guarantees outstanding. The only debt left is 1,2 MSEK to shareholders, on reverser running at the Riksbank policy rate plus 2 %, due May 2028. Amortisation starts 30 July, at a minimum of 35 200 kr a month across all lenders pro rata. The first one goes out this Thursday.

Five of you put up that half million on a week's notice, when it was far from a safe bet. Thank you.

03 How the company is built now /////

A small company with a matched cost base.

Aurora Punks is a small company now.

One person on payroll. Everything else runs on contractors and partner studios, brought in per project and released when the project ends. The Pharaoh Lands team is six people, and not one of them is a fixed cost.

The fixed cost base - the one salary plus everything it takes to keep the lights on - runs about 92 KSEK a month. And that salary is our developer placed with Mojang through Netlight, matched one to one against the contract it is paid for, so it is not exposed overhead either.

Against that we carry a recurring floor of roughly 290 KSEK a month: the Mojang placement at about 150 KSEK, and the Kingdom co-development margin at about 140 KSEK.

The floor covers the fixed base three times over. We do not need a new contract to survive the month, and one client going quiet cannot take the company down - that is what happened to the subsidiary in December.

05 The 2025 annual report /////

The write-down, and what covers it.

Vessels of Decay is being written down to a conservative residual value in the 2025 accounts. That takes equity negative as of 31 December 2025, and it means the board has to draw up a control balance sheet (kontrollbalansräkning).

The ALMI write-off of 1,4 MSEK falls in 2026, so it does not repair the 2025 accounts. It does repair the control balance sheet, which is measured as of today. The board went through this with our accountant on 16 July and agreed the write-off covers it on its own, with margin. No shareholder contribution is needed. The annual report is due 31 July and you will get it.

Mattias has called the AGM for this Thursday, 30 July at 9.00, online. Happy to take any of this there as well.

06 What we are aiming at /////

Three things for the next twelve months.

  • Line up the next co-development. Pharaoh Lands is done in December. The job is to have the next contract signed before then, at similar size or larger. Raw Fury is the first place we look, since we will have delivered a full DLC for them by that point.
  • Hold the Mojang placement. It is open-ended, profitable, and covers the fixed base on its own.
  • Get the catalogue earning. Water Me & You hits Early Access in February 2027, which is the first revenue share we have had in a long time.

Add it all up and it points to roughly 4,5 MSEK on our own books in 2027, against 3,5 MSEK this year, with the upside sitting in what the next co-development turns out to be and how Water Me & You performs. The 4,5 is a conservative number.
