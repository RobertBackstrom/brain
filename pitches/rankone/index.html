What it is

Taste, entered by humans. Not inferred from clicks.

RankOne is a community where players log what they have played, are playing, and want to play, and categorize how those games relate. The aggregate is a taste graph: a living map of how real audiences connect across games, kept current every day. No crawler, store scrape or panel produces this.
