The Game

A cozy game built for two.

There are plenty of cozy games. There are plenty of co-op games. Very few are both.

Water Me & You puts two players in the same warm world - one a water droplet, one a seedling - and asks them to bring a quiet place left behind by people back to life, together. Grow it, tend it, explore it, side by side.

The warmth of Stardew Valley. The togetherness of It Takes Two. Playable on the couch or online, start to finish.

Two players, one world.
