01The Mission

What the job actually is

Read from your 12 August feature list. Three problems sit inside it, and they are not the same problem.

Creative

Two IPs whose players want different things. Our own audience work puts the crossover affinity in PAYDAY 2, not PAYDAY 3: PD2 runs roughly 30k concurrents against PD3's ~900, at a 4x affinity index against 1,25x. The PUBG base is exactly neutral on stealth and strong on loud co-op PvE and SWAT-style tactical play.

We read that as a sequencing and positioning argument, not a case for cutting stealth.

Technical

Engine and 3C work inside PUBG, in Unreal, under multiplayer and live-service constraints. A cert candidate has to be in Krafton's hands at month 10, which sets every date behind it.

Delivery

23 roles from a standing start to peak inside four months, two IP holders in the approval chain, and 12 months to Gold.

The scope as we have read it
  • Four new heists adapted from PAYDAY 3: Turbid Station, 99 Boxes or Touch The Sky, Syntax Error, Under The Surphaze
  • The four Heist Royale stages upgraded
  • Progression rework: EXP to currency, free skill and weapon selection
  • Five new weapons
  • Stealth ingredient expansion
  • Shield enemy, one more difficulty
  • Restart and partial escape
  • UI/UX overhaul
  • PUBG engine and 3C changes

06Ways of working

Embedded to collaborate, autonomous enough to deliver

Ownership

One producer owns the plan and is your single point of contact. Game direction sits with our design lead, working to your creative approval at each gate.

Decisions

We bring you decisions with a recommendation. Where we disagree with a direction we say so before the sprint.

Communication

Leads on site at Birger Jarlsgatan 61, the rest hybrid. Both studios are Stockholm-based. Milestone reviews at all eight gates, weekly written status in between, one shared backlog you can see.

Risk → mitigation

Ramp to 23 inside four months
Both studios are naming people now rather than recruiting later. The month 1-2 ramp is shallow so the seniors set the foundations before the wider team lands.
Two IP holders in the approval chain
Milestone gates are set against Krafton's cert candidate date at month 10, and we work back from it.
Integration with the PUBG engine
Dmitry was on Irons 1. Month 1 is technical mapping, and the plan is re-cut against what it finds.
Scope growth
Every gate carries a named deliverable list. Additions are re-planned in the open, with a number attached.
