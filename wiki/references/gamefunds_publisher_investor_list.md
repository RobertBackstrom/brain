# GameFunds - Free Publisher & Investor List

LinkedIn post by Grzegorz Wątroba announcing a free, open-source GitHub resource for game funding.

## What it is

- **200+ verified entries** of game publishers and investors, worldwide.
- A **pitching guide** based on what actually gets a reply.
- Hosted on GitHub, free - no email gate, no "book a call", no paywalled PDF.
- Repo: https://github.com/GameDevGrzesiek/GameFunds

## Author's rationale (post text)

"Some people sell this data. I think it should be free. I've spent quite some time compiling every game publisher and investor I could verify - 200+ entries, worldwide - plus a pitching guide based on what actually gets a reply. It's on GitHub. Free. No email gate, no 'book a call', no PDF for $99. Here's why: When one indie game lands an investor, we all gain a little. That funded game ships. It sells. It becomes the case study the next founder points at when a fund asks 'has this genre ever worked?' Success compounds. But only if the door is open. Charging developers for a contact list is charging them for the privilege of being poor. The people who most need this information are exactly the people who can't pay for it. So take it. Use it. And if a fund is missing, or a contact is dead, or you got a reply from someone I've marked as unresponsive - open an issue. Make it better for the person after you."

(Post also published in Polish.)
