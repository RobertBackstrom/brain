# RankOne Global AB

CZP/Robert portfolio company. Robert was asked (2026-06-15) to assist CEO **Johan Tjäder** on biz-dev/strategy together with board member **Peter Warman** (Newzoo co-founder, now Authentics.gg). Robert holds ~5% (2020 round). Project home: `projects/rankone/`. See also [[structure_ownership]], [[project_rankone]].

## What it is
- Org nr **559168-5325**, founded 2018, säte **Holmsund** (Umeå region), Sweden. ~5 employees.
- Community "gaming profile" platform — **"Your Life in Games"**. Gamers manually curate what they've played / are playing / want to play (Played / Playing / Want-To-Play). The aggregate of human-curated, authentic data (vs AI-generated/automated imports) powers player-behaviour **Insights** for game developers.
- Surfaces: web, iOS (App Store, launched 2026), Google Play, Twitch extension. B2B at rankone.global/company; Insights at insights.rankone.global.

## Traction (State of RankOne, 2026-05-18)
- Users **101,155** (+1,897 MoM; 95,000 at 2025 year-end — fully organic).
- Reach **77.8M** (total Twitch following of users; 73M at year-end 2025).
- MAU **20%**.
- Data: **2.6M+** manually categorized game relations (+51% in 2025); **413,000** written reviews (+60% in 2025).
- Runway to **2027-06-24**.

## Product roadmap
- **Profile Similarity** — algorithmic matching of users by gaming preference (foundation for matchmaking + discovery).
- **WTP-Drafter** — "Want To Play" backlog picker / draft modes.
- **Personalized Feed** — personal release calendar + aggregated news feed.
- **rankone_bot** — MVP chatbot for Twitch/streamer chats; strategic shift from passive extension to active live-engagement tool.
