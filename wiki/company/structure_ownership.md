## Related-party cross-holdings (matters for contracts)
Several counterparties Robert/AP/CZP contract with are **partly owned by the group**, which triggers ABL 8 kap. 23 § self-dealing safeguards and related-party disclosure on those contracts:
- **Red Marmoset** — AP 15% + Robert co-Director (e.g. K2C art sub).
- **Upstream Arcade** — AP 15%.
- **Bright Gambit** — CZP <10% (e.g. K2C design sub; Andreea Chifu is also at Bright Gambit, so she is conflicted out of signing Bright Gambit contracts).
- **5 Fortress** — CZP <10% (Striden engagement).
- **Dark Riviera** — CZP 6%, Robert chairman (RLR IP).
- **Malformation, RankOne** — CZP 10% / <10%.
When drafting/reviewing a contract with any of these, apply the related-party signatory safeguard (independent signatories, board minute disclosure). See [[lawyer]] learnings + [[czp_project_folder_structure]].
