# Knives & Gutters — Pipeline

## Current State

**Active festival/showcase pipeline. Inbound publisher + showcase-curator interest, no outbound prospecting on record.**

Knives & Gutters is a Mordheim/Trench-Crusade-inspired skirmish game by Stockholm studio **Ark Island** (Jakob Köster, Fredrik Laurent), with Aurora Punks attached as biz-dev / co-publisher partner. The project was originally pitched to Games Workshop as a licensed Mordheim title (DevBrighton 2025; formal pitch 2025-10-02). GW declined the Mordheim license on 2025-10-03 (Owen Rees — "not able to licence any new Mordheim games at this time"). The project pivoted to original IP, was internally renamed "Gangs of Forsaken", and then renamed again to **Knives & Gutters** by 2026-04-23 (calendar event renames). Source: [[raw/gmail/199a4eaebab7aade]], [[memory/project_knives_and_gutters]].

**Robert's BD scope = Campaigns + Community.** Concretely that means: festival/showcase applications, publisher event submissions, Steam-event placement, community/creator alignment ahead of a Steam page launch. The development side is Jakob's (he runs the daily standup; Robert sweeps the calendar entry). Source: [[memory/project_knives_and_gutters]] + active festival submissions traceable in Robert's gmail (DevGAMM Gdańsk, Baltic Showcase on Steam, TactiCon, Second Wind, CobraTekku Dark Dungeon Days).

Steam page is in flight — not live as of late March 2026 (per CobraTekku exchange). Robert told Konstantin Suvorov on 2026-03-16 that the Steam AppID would be ready "by the end of this week" — needs verification whether it landed. Targeted release per Second Wind submission: 2026-07-30.

Genre/positioning per the Second Wind submission: dark-fantasy skirmish; warbands; persistent team-building. Marketing voice (for synopsis use): "Forsaken is a place where ogres collect debts, vampire lords don't bother to ask, and goblins think you look better in pieces. If your crew has the nerve to chase glory here, don't expect fairness — just the privilege of dying interestingly." Source: [[raw/gmail/19cede312476b7a8]].

## Strategic Notes

- **Pipeline is festival/showcase-driven, not outbound publisher-driven.** Of all the K&G threads on record, the only outbound publisher pitch was Games Workshop (Oct 2025, declined). Everything else is curator-side: DevGAMM, TactiCon, Second Wind, CobraTekku Dark Dungeon Days. This matches the engagement scope (Campaigns + Community) — Robert is feeding Ark Island's game into discoverability events, not chasing co-dev or licensing deals.
- **Steam page status is the load-bearing dependency.** Multiple curators (CobraTekku explicitly, Baltic Showcase on Steam structurally) need a live Steam AppID. Robert committed an ETA on 2026-03-16; verify status before the next round of festival prep.
- **Two upcoming dates worth tracking:** (1) DevGAMM Gdańsk physical participation confirmation, (2) DevGAMM Baltic Showcase on Steam — Steam still has to confirm dates and visibility. Both depend on the Steam page being live.
- **GW Kill Team angle is open but cold.** Robert pitched Kill Team / other skirmish licenses to Owen Rees on 2025-10-06; no response on record. If Robert wants to reactivate, the foundational network is still there (DevBrighton history).
- **AP positioning hook for future curators:** "Backed by a group of veterans from the Swedish game dev scene." Persistent warband/team-building mechanics framed as a "multi-platform framework for skirmish games" — that framing came out of the GW post-mortem and is the best one-line for warm pitches.
- **Use of generative AI is disclosed.** Per the Second Wind submission, K&G uses gen-AI for character base meshes (final rigged models human-made). Useful to keep declared the same way across submissions to avoid disclosure inconsistency.
- **No outreach without Robert.** Per `skills/autonomous_decision_framework.md`, contacting K&G counterparts is MUST-ASK.
