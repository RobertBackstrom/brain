## Strategic Notes

### Positioning
Aurora Punks the studio is in three commercial roles simultaneously:

1. **Producer / co-dev for external IP** — Formula Drone (Flightball), Virtuos (when it lands). Selling team capacity + production leadership.
2. **Lite publisher for partner studios** — Atomic Elbow (Sir Whoopass — see `memory/project_sir_whoopass`), Kinda Brave / Windup (Distant Bloom + Go Fight Fantastic). Selling sales calendar work, console submissions, key distribution, sales reporting against retainer + commission.
3. **Broker / network multiplier** — Shosha Games (publishing-partner search), occasional inbound studios pitching for AP publishing or AP brokerage. AP captures commission upside from intros without taking on publishing risk.

### What's NOT in scope here
- **Investor relations** (Behold Ventures, Loot Spawn AB, Alexander Bergendahl etc.) — CorpBot.
- **Cap table / governance / firmateckning / board admin** — CorpBot.
- **Title-specific delivery work** for AP-published catalog (BADASS XR PM, K2C delivery, Sir Whoopass console reports, DBL/GFF community scraping) — PM domain. The relationship lives here, the work tickets live in PM-domain memories and DB tickets.
- **Dedicated BD-project prospects** — Elias, Striden, Blue Scarab, K&G outreach — separate project-pipeline pages.

### Fundraising-stage prospects
Several portfolio prospects are themselves fundraising (Formula Drone — £250K angel round). AP-side delivery commitments need to be staged against those closes. Flag in Open Questions per deal.

### Inbound publisher pitches: triage rule
AP gets a steady-but-low-volume inbound stream of indie devs pitching for publishing (Goblin's Healer, Kiipluu, Chibi Escape — the latter declined Aug 2025 over budget). Default response: lite publishing only fits when (a) genre/scope matches AP's existing lite-pub stack (party / co-op / cozy / niche console-friendly), (b) dev team has cert-ready or near-ready builds, (c) rev share lands at AP's standard split (Sir Whoopass model = 55% AP / 45% dev as ceiling reference). Otherwise, decline gracefully — Robert often offers his **broker network** as an alternative.
