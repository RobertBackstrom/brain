# Disposable Corps - Pipeline

AP production takeover of *Disposable Corps*, a 5v5 trench-warfare shooter by **Armoured Dudes**
(developer, four people) published by **Light Up Games** (publisher and the channel into the deal).
Two counterparties, keep them apart. The 2025 shape was hourly co-dev support and it went on hold.
The 2026 shape is AP as producer and product owner taking the game to Early Access, funded by
project investors LUG is sourcing.

## The blocker, resolved in principle 2026-08-26
Paul would only accept technical support that takes his direction (via Anthony, 2026-07-07). Robert's
formulation in the shared Discord group, which Anthony accepted: AP is "the annoying partner who asks
for fixes and makes sure they understand it's needed, then we discuss solutions and if Paul says he
wanna do it we set a deadline for it." AP owns product decisions and the schedule, Paul keeps the
implementation. Residual risk: no sanction if agreed deadlines slip. The review month tests that in
small scale before twelve months are committed.
