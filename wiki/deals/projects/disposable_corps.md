# Disposable Corps - Pipeline

AP production takeover of *Disposable Corps*, a 5v5 trench-warfare shooter by **Armoured Dudes**
(developer, four people) published by **Light Up Games** (publisher and the channel into the deal).
Two counterparties, keep them apart. The 2025 shape was hourly co-dev support and it went on hold.
The 2026 shape is AP as producer and product owner taking the game to Early Access, funded by
project investors LUG is sourcing.

## The blocker, resolved in principle 2026-08-26
Paul would only accept technical support that takes his direction (via Anthony, 2026-07-07). Robert's
formulation in the shared Discord group, which Anthony accepted: AP is "the annoying partner who asks
for fixes and makes sure they understand it's needed, then we discuss solutions and if Paul says he
wanna do it we set a deadline for it." AP owns product decisions and the schedule, Paul keeps the
implementation. Residual risk: no sanction if agreed deadlines slip. The review month tests that in
small scale before twelve months are committed.

## AP x Rift co-delivery (2026-09-07)
Robert opened a second track: bring **Rift Gaming** (Rift Consulting AB, 559482-0010, Gustav Wassberg
CEO, Victor Roxlin COO) in as co-delivery partner on the same 180k/month envelope, not on top of it.
Rift's seats come out of AP's 150k side. **Jesper Staafjord** on UX, **Dima Garkavenko** bounded to a
network and hosting assessment in the review month, and the dev seat left open between **Chris Allar**
(Rift) and **Oskar Hansen** (AP). Two scenarios: Rift 500 000 / 28 percent if AP keeps the dev seat,
Rift 1 040 000 / 58 percent if Rift takes it. **SUPERSEDED same day, see v2 below.**

**v2 (2026-09-07, Robert's reshape).** Network merged into one developer seat (Dima is an Unreal
developer, not a bounded consult); Jesper carries product owner as well as UX; artist seat dropped;
Robert executive producer at 20 000 cash against a 45 000 notional seat. Seats: founders 30 000,
Jesper (Rift) 55 000, developer 50 000 (open between Rift and AP), Robert 45 000/20 000 cash.
**Total 180 000 and cash 155 000 unchanged.** Rift 660 000 (37 %) or 1 260 000 (70 %) of the
1 800 000 delivery scope. **v4, final of the day: recoup on REAL COST, verified against the Irons 2 internal-cost column**
(Jesper 120 000/mo FT, Dima 108 000, Chris 101 000, Oskar 80 000, Robert 140 000). Allocations
Jesper 60 %, dev 50 %, Robert 25 %, so the team costs 187 500/mo against 155 000 paid and
**32 500/mo is carried, 390 000 over the term**. Each side recovers its own carried cost at 2,5x in
first position: Rift 525 000 on 210 000, AP 450 000 on 180 000. Crossing moves to **52 000
wishlists**. The 180 000/mo "total project cost" of v1 to v3 is retired. **AP asks LUG for 30 % of
net separately, retained inside AP and not mentioned to Rift.** Console port ROFR wholly AP's.
**Verified what LUG has actually seen: 300 000 deferred, 2,5x = 750 000, then 15 percent.** So the
2,5x is not new but the 30 percent is double, and that is the aggressive part, not the recoup. Cash to LUG's financiers is unchanged at 155k/month. Model in
`drafts/rift_split_2026-09-07.md`, page at https://pitch.aurorapunks.com/disposable-corps-rift
(`rift` / `qpFdQhXTL0I0`).

**The commercial analysis of the same date corrects the plan.** Expected units first twelve months
post-EA: 3 000 low, 10 800 base, 31 500 high. The plan's "7 500 units clears the 750 000 recoup" is
wrong (it skipped regional pricing, discounts, VAT and refunds); the real crossing is about 14 400
units in first position and about 50 000 pro rata with the financiers. The 15 percent revshare has no
expected value in low or base. Two asks that follow: **the current wishlist count from LUG** (halves
the width of the whole band) and **reversing the bots-first cut**, which the comparable set says
decides whether the game is still selling in month six.
