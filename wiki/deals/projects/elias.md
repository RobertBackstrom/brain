## Strategic Notes

- **Engine fit is a hard filter.** Custom engines (Avalanche-Apex, Remedy-Northlight, MachineGames-idTech, DICE-Frostbite, Mojang-Bedrock, Housemarque-custom) are excluded by default; middleware doesn't plug into proprietary tech without an integration project they won't fund. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **Investor / cap-table framing beats client-logo lists for peer Swedish prospects.** "KM Troedsson + Linus Blomberg + Fatshark ägarbolag" hits harder than "Arrowhead/Avalanche/Iron Gate kör på det". Use logos only for international targets. Canonical template at `umbrella/elias_bizdev/strategy/outreach_template_warm_email.md`. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **Primary CTA = referral ask, not demo ask.** "Vem på X är den man ska prata med?" is dramatically easier to say yes to than "can we book a demo?". NGC mixer is the secondary low-commitment CTA. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **Behold portfolio is a rich warm-path pool.** AP is a Behold portfolio company — gives Elias "fellow Behold founder" framing across ~22 studios. Behold Summit May 25 in Malmö is the single best venue. Tier A targets (Dead Astronauts, Eddaheim, Wayfinder, Red Rover, Sunscorched) high Elias fit. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **Epic fired the entire Metasound team (2026-04).** Increases Elias relevance for UE-embedded studios that were on the Metasound roadmap. Use as fresh talking point with UE-heavy studios. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **Audio middleware decision-maker fit rubric (high-to-low):** (1) role seniority — Audio Director / Tech Director / Studio Head can say yes; Sound Designer can't. (2) current audio stack — custom/legacy = best entry, Wwise-only = play "complement" angle, FMOD = mid. (3) project timing — pre-prod/early prod = best, shipped/live-service = lock-in. (4) studio size 50-300 = sweet spot. (5) warm path / local proximity. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **NGC Mixer (May 27, Malmö, Luma https://luma.com/7xr8ju9q) is the headline conversion event.** All Tier 1 outreach should carry the Luma link as secondary CTA. See [[elias_ngc]] for the intent-based scan side.
- **Active client deepening (Fatshark):** Erik Brattlöf (Elias) set up shared Slack with Fatshark Apr 10. Existing-client referral leverage strong. Source: [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]].
- **No outreach without Robert.** Per [skills/autonomous_decision_framework](../../../skills/autonomous_decision_framework.md), reaching out to identified prospects is MUST-ASK.
