# Sylvain Runberg

## Role

Dark Riviera co-founder and writer. 90+ graphic novels, 1.5M copies sold, 23 countries. Wrote the
Millennium and Watch Dogs: Legion (with Gabriel Germain, Behemoth Comics, 2021) adaptations.
Originates most of the DR slate and is the door into French/Belgian publishing (Media
Participation group, Dargaud, Le Lombard, Dupuis, Rue de Sèvres). Named Code Name Hope and
Archivist as the two IPs on the slate with the most game material ready (board thread, Jun 2026).

## Relationship to Robert

Board colleague — Robert chairs Dark Riviera, Sylvain co-founded it. Sylvain is the one who brings
game-adaptation opportunities to Robert (e.g. this Boombox intro) since he's closest to the
publishing/creative side and the external contacts.
