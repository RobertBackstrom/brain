## Message 1 — 2025-10-02 — Robert → Owen Rees (GW), Martin

> Hello Owen and Martin,
>
> It's been a while since we had a chat at DevBrighton about the Mordheim game we are developing. Here's the pitch material for it. It's a collaboration between Aurora Punks and the Stockholm-based studio Ark Island (adding the founders Fredrik and Jakob to the mail thread). Backed not only by me but a group of veterans from the Swedish game dev scene, the studio is also building on the immense and growing interest in the classic tabletop. The community around it is very active and healthy.

> Budget: 500 000 USD (fully funded by studio and Aurora Punks)
> Platforms: PC, Console and mobile/handhelds
>
