## Msg 36 — Caspar Gray → Andreea Chifu — 2025-11-20
Nice to meet you too. Also copied are Stephanie, the Founder/CEO of Indiecade, and Willa, who is managing the gamejam end of the project. I'm an Indiecade advisory board member and I'm handling the business side. Background: Indiecade is most known for its annual festival and other events. Since 2018 they've organized a climate-focused gamejam each summer, and this year they aim to release a compilation of the best games from the event, to raise money for climate charities and help fund the jam going forward. As they are not a Steam publisher, they're looking for a company with an ideological fit to act as the publisher — which is where Aurora Punks comes in. Robert said yes to the idea and there's currently a draft contract being reviewed on the Indiecade end. No specific deadline (but before next year's jam would be good). The jammers have made the games; we've selected a shortlist of a dozen or so (including the best from 2024); next stage is reviewing those with you and locking in which are included in the anthology. Then teams make further adjustments, and we have people to make the frontend, integrate the games into a single package, and do key art. To the consumer it's a single product, not a bundle. The jammers collectively pick the benefiting charity from a small selection we provide. The initial contract was from Aurora Punks; we've only asked for minor changes (see Nov 13 email). We'll then need to agree precisely which publishing services AP will provide and the revenue split (section 1.1 and Exhibits B, C & D).
