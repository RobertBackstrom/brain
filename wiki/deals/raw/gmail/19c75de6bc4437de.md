### Investor letter (forwarded for context)

> Hi
> I'm writing to introduce Formula Drone, an experience economy entertainment start-up. Building a new live sport - Flightball - aerial five-a-side football played with specially designed drones and a floating ball. Think Robot Wars meets Quidditch meets Rocket League: fast, visual, and unlike anything audiences have seen before.
>
> To date we have proved the concept can work. We designed and built our first generation of drone bumpers and successfully demonstrated gameplay at a major Esports event at the NEC. The demo showed three critical things:
>
> 1. Technical feasibility — the drones, bumpers and ball all performed as intended.
> 2. Game playability & spectator appeal — the crowd reaction confirmed the potential of Flightball as a live entertainment sport.
> 3. Scalability — the format is simple, safe, and easily repeatable with improved hardware.
>
> A short video of the NEC test can be viewed here: Formuladrone.uk/FBfinal.html
>
> We are now preparing the next phase: a fully produced Formula Drone: Flightball showcase event in 2026, developed in partnership with the DRP Group, one of the UK's leading events and media companies. The plan is to invite Premier League clubs and other high-profile partners / influencers etc - to compete in a professionally staged tournament.
>
> To deliver this next step, we are raising an initial angel investment round of £250,000. Funds will be used for:
> - Engineering and manufacturing of second-generation drones and bumpers
> - Studio hire and production costs for the showcase
> - Marketing, PR and partner outreach
>
> Our Phase 2 plan is for Flightball to become a global franchise offering and is potentially bigger than Laser Quest. To fuel interest in the franchise we plan to open a six arena Flightball HQ in a central London location and run a Flightball UK tour of say 5 city centre venues. These activities will kick start the formation of live sports and Esports leagues with the development of a multi player Esports game similar to Rocket League. The commercial model includes live events, broadcast rights, brand partnerships, merchandise and toy rights.
>
> We would very much welcome the opportunity to discuss how you could be involved as an early angel investor and we would obviously invite you to the showcase to play Flightball for yourself. Your experience and support could help us bring this new sport to the public at scale.
