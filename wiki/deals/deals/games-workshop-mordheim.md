# Games Workshop — Mordheim License (predecessor to Knives & Gutters)

## Snapshot

Predecessor pitch. Before "Knives & Gutters" existed as branding, the Ark Island prototype was pitched to **Games Workshop's Warhammer Licensing arm** as a Mordheim-licensed skirmish game. Robert had a foundational chat with **Owen Rees** at DevBrighton (mid-2025), then sent the formal pitch on 2025-10-02 with Jakob and Fredrik (Ark Island founders) added to the thread.

GW declined the Mordheim license on 2025-10-03 (Owen Rees: "I am not able to licence any new Mordheim games at this time. I can't provide more information than that alas.") Robert pivoted in his 2025-10-06 reply, asking whether GW would consider licensing other skirmish IPs — Kill Team specifically — given the prototype is already a "very solid multi-platform framework for skirmish games" with retention-driving warband/team-building mechanics. **No reply on record.** The project subsequently pivoted to original IP and was rebranded to "Gangs of Forsaken" → "Knives & Gutters" by 2026-04-23.
