## What They Bring / What We Bring
**They bring:** an investor with a stated appetite for a drone simulator aimed at Swedish defence procurement, plus a lawyer's network and a reading of the buyer that we do not have.

**We bring:** the asset and the ability to make it a product. A simulator that already models per-motor thrust, Betaflight-grade PID tuning, wind, air density, radio interference, weather, seasons, light and ballistics, a route to license it, a co-development partner who can build the operator interface, and a structure that pays the developer in a way he will accept.
