# Avalanche Studios Group — R&D

## Snapshot
Avalanche Studios Group (Just Cause, Mad Max, Warhammer 40,000: Darktide; Stockholm + New York + Liverpool + Malmö) is a long-standing Elias-adjacent studio — from the **old Elias music-tool era** they were among the studios with historical relationships to the team. Their shipping engine is the proprietary **Apex (Avalanche Open World Engine)**, which is a standing custom-engine exclusion for audio-middleware outreach. The new angle is **not the shipping studios** — it's the **R&D team**, which builds unreleased prototypes of concept ideas (tested with the community via the **Frontrunners** program). R&D evaluates technology independently of the shipping engine and prototypes freely, so it is not bound by the Apex exclusion the way a production team is. Warm contact into R&D: **Steve Bigras**, Executive Producer on the R&D team (confirmed by Robert 2026-05-21). ## What They Bring / What We Bring
**They bring:** a AAA studio with a dedicated R&D function whose whole job is to prototype new tech and concepts. R&D is exactly the kind of internal team that can evaluate an audio-middleware tool on its merits without the Apex lock-in that excludes the shipping studios. If Elias lands in an R&D prototype, that's both a credibility reference and a possible path back into a future non-Apex production.

**We bring:** Elias as a post-Metasound audio middleware — real-time raytraced acoustics, GPU spatial audio — exactly the kind of cutting-edge tech an R&D team prototypes against. Framing: this is **not** a pitch to swap Apex's audio stack (that's the excluded play); it's "your R&D team evaluates new tech independently — here's something worth a look."

## Why This Is Not the Standard Avalanche Exclusion
Per [[../../agents/memory/bizdev_learnings]], studios on custom engines (Avalanche-Apex, Remedy-Northlight, etc.) are excluded by default because middleware doesn't plug into proprietary tech without an integration project they won't fund. **That rule is about shipping productions.** An R&D team is a separate audience: it prototypes outside the shipping pipeline, evaluates tech for its own sake, and is not committed to Apex for every experiment. Treat this deal as the R&D exception to the Avalanche exclusion — the engine-mismatch filter does not disqualify the R&D track.
