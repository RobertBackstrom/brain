# The Arsenal Agency

## Snapshot
The Arsenal Agency (Caspar Gray) is brokering Aurora Punks into the **IndieCade Climate Jam compilation**. IndieCade has run a climate-focused game jam every summer since 2018; it now wants to release a Steam compilation of the best jam games to raise money for climate charity and to help fund the jam going forward. IndieCade is not a Steam publisher, so it needs a partner with an ideological fit to act as **publisher of record** — that is Aurora Punks. To the consumer it is a **single product, not a bundle**: 8-10 short, English-language games (Unity, a mandated version) curated from the 2024/2025 (possibly 2026) jams. Consumer proceeds go to the **Climate Justice Alliance** charity and to funding the jam, routed through IndieCade; AP is compensated via a **fixed fee recouped from proceeds** (not a rev share) so the "all proceeds to charity" message holds. The deal has run since Apr 2025, originally with Bibbi Wikman (AP, since departed). After a near-dormant Q1 2026 it re-activated: a planning call on 2026-05-07 reset the structure and Caspar sent the redlined contract on 2026-05-08. Now in active contract negotiation. ## What They Bring / What We Bring
**They bring:** A slot as publisher of record on an IndieCade-branded charity compilation — strong brand association for AP, which Robert weighs above the fee. The compilation is 8-10 short English-language games, all built in Unity (a mandated version, to ease integration), PC/Steam only — no console, no certification, no localization. Caspar's side builds the launcher/frontend, integrates the games, and commissions key art; IndieCade handles creator/community outreach and the charity split. **Rights are handled by IndieCade, not AP** — all jam entrants sign an agreement with IndieCade, and the selected anthology teams later sign a comprehensive rights-transfer agreement (AP gets to review it).

**We bring:** Release management — Steam build/upload, store page setup and maintenance, participation in relevant Steam sales/festivals, QA, and consolidated reporting + payouts so all revenue runs through one counterpart. Robert scoped AP's effort as ~a couple of weeks, mostly pre-release.
