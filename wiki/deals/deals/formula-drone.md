## Budget / Commercial Terms (Jon Sturgess model)
Source: Flightball eGame financial model, [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kptOzuzvMOUWy_ZiVwEBFz8itmU9ehKxCsv0Ola5a8o/edit?gid=984988995) (Robert-supplied 2026-06-18; matches the Excel Jon sent 2026-05-07 per [[raw/gmail/19ddea9f55a305ad]]). **These are Formula Drone's figures — the AP terms are explicitly marked "estimated / negotiation starting point", i.e. an opening offer, not agreed.**

Development budget, staged to funding rounds:
- Seed: **£40,000** (prototype v0.1) + **£20,000** additional (functional v1 prototype) = £60k seed dev tranche — funds the "crude" showcase playable.
- Fit-out round: **£200,000** (multi-platform v1 release; ~18 months to launch).
- Growth round: **£530,000** (v2 features, console cert, eSport infra; Year 2–3).
- **Total dev budget across rounds: £790,000.**

Aurora Punks commercial terms (their proposal, to negotiate):
- Upfront licence fee: **£30,000**.
- Revenue share: **20%** (their note: 15–25% typical for licensor rev-share on F2P).

Sheet tabs: `Interactive_>>>` (cover), `Assumptions`, `P&L_Model`, `Sensitivity`, `Strategic_Notes`, `Comparables`.

**4-year P&L (Formula Drone eGame vertical), base case:**
| | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gross revenue | £32k | £309k | £1.58M | £4.53M |
| EBITDA | (£128k) | (£178k) | £274k | £1.90M |

Loss-making Y1–2 (funded from dev rounds), **EBITDA breakeven Y3**, strong Y4. Dev spend phased into P&L at £60k/£200k/£318k/£212k.

**What AP earns (base case):** rev-share is **20% of net IAP revenue, after platform cut, excluding eSport lines** (narrower than 20% of revenue). Rev-share to AP: £4,480 / £41,496 / £206,752 / £591,500 (Y1–Y4) = ~£844k; plus £30k Y1 upfront = **~£874k over 4 years**, almost entirely back-loaded to Y3–Y4 and contingent on the game scaling.

**Negotiation intel (their own internal guidance — important):**
- `Strategic_Notes` tells FD that **20% is AP's "negotiating ceiling" and pushing AP to 15% is worth more at Y4 than doubling conversion**. `Comparables`: every 5pp cut in AP share = ~£30–50k Y4 EBITDA for them. → Expect FD to push Robert toward 15%; treat 20% as the floor.
- They recommend **giving AP no equity** ("keep cap table clean for investor rounds").
- **KEY RISK #1 = "Aurora Punks dependency,"** mitigated (their words) by **clear IP ownership: Flightball rules, arena designs and branding must be Formula Drone IP regardless of sim engine** → expect an IP-assignment ask; Robert to set his engine/IP line.
- Their own model credits **AP's existing sim engine as a key seed-investor de-risking factor** — leverage against a rev-share haircut.
- They flag prioritising **Epic (12% cut) over Steam (30%)** as a margin lever.

`Sensitivity` tab: Y4 EBITDA matrices on conversion × ARPU and on AP rev-share rate.
