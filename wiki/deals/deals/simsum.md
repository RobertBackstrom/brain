# simsum

## Snapshot
Small studio (effectively Vincent, trading as simsum) behind *Gamestonk Simulator: Gone Rogue*
(Steam 3349970) and the in-development *Crack a Pack* (Steam 4678500), an incremental collectathon.
Relationship runs on Discord, not mail, and has now cycled twice: a 2025 publishing conversation
that Vincent declined on timing, and a 2026-08-10 inbound where he sent Robert the Crack a Pack
publisher deck for a read before shopping it to publishers.

**Read the status field carefully: "Interested" describes the counterparty, not us.** Robert's
position decided 2026-09-15 is **advisory plus introductions, no offer**. This is not a live sales
opportunity and should not be worked as one. The value is relationship continuity and the
port/co-dev tail Vincent himself said he wants.

**Open blocker on anything outward-facing: Steam lists Vsoo Games as publisher on both simsum
titles** (and on the new demo). The deck asks for a publishing partner and never mentions them.
Until Vincent explains Vsoo's role and rights, nobody can tell what is actually on offer, and no
introduction should be made.

## What They Bring / What We Bring
**They bring:** a developer who actually ships and actually markets (manual Twitch outreach via
SullyGnome, Reddit organic and paid, Keymailer, a 32,000-name mailing list he grew himself), a
clear cheap-to-build loop, finished distinctive art, and a stated appetite for console ports and
co-dev later. He keeps coming back, which is the asset.

**We bring:** a publisher-grade read on his deck and numbers, market intelligence he does not have
(the *Crack the Pack* collision was ours to find), and introductions into publishers who run
sub-6-dollar Steam volume titles with paid UA. Explicitly **not** capital and **not** a publishing
offer at this stage.
