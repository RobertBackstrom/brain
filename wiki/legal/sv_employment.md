# Swedish Employment Law

Reference. Not legal advice. Real lawyer required for any actual termination, dispute, or collective-bargaining issue.

## Anställningsformer

- Tillsvidare (default) per `LAS 4 §`.
- Visstid — särskild visstidsanställning per `LAS 5 §` (post-2022 reform); konvertering till tillsvidare.
- Provanställning per `LAS 6 §` — max 6 mån.
- Projektanställning — exists only via kollektivavtal; otherwise treated as visstid.

## Uppsägning

- Saklig grund per `LAS 7 §` (arbetsbrist eller personliga skäl).
- Turordning per `LAS 22 §`; undantagsregeln (3 personer, post-2022).
- Uppsägningstider per `LAS 11 §`.
- Avgångsvederlag — not statutory; negotiated.

## Konkurrensklausuler

- 1969 års kollektivavtal — caps on duration, scope, ersättning during binding period.
- Post-employment non-compete — typically max 9-18 months, requires compensation ≥ ~60% of salary.
- Limited industries where these are even arguably enforceable.

## Sekretess

- Lag om företagshemligheter (FHL 2018:558).
- NDA scope toward employees vs contractors.

## Contractor vs Employee

- Skatteverket's substance test — degree of control, integration, economic risk.
- F-skatt as evidence (not determinative) of self-employment.
- Risk to engaging company if reclassified: arbetsgivaravgifter retroactively + sanctions.
