# Publishing Agreement

**1993 Space Machine / 1993 Shenandoah**

This Publishing Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of last signature below, by and between:

**Krister Karlsson**, personal identity number 740403-2513, of Sommarliden 22, 135 61 Tyresö, Sweden (the "Developer"),

and

**Creation Zero Point Holding AB**, reg. no. 559182-7471, c/o Robert Bäckström, Bondegatan 31, 116 33 Stockholm, Sweden (the "Publisher"),

each individually a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## Background

A. The Developer is the creator of the game *1993 Space Machine*, released on personal computers, and of its console version released as *1993 Shenandoah* (together the "Game"), and owns, or is authorised by every other person holding a right in the Game, including its music, to license all intellectual property rights in the Game, its source code and its development tools.

B. The Game was published under a development and publishing agreement dated 20 September 2019 between the Developer and White Lines Black Spaces AB, reg. no. 559217-4196 (the "2019 Agreement"). White Lines Black Spaces AB was declared bankrupt on 25 September 2024.

C. Following the bankruptcy of White Lines Black Spaces AB, the business it carried on, and with it the publisher's position for the Game, passed by a series of transfers to the Publisher, the last of them completed on 16 February 2026. The Publisher today carries out the publishing, distribution and platform administration for the Game, and holds, or is in the process of taking over, the platform accounts through which the Game is sold.

D. The Game has been commercially available since 2020 and has remained on sale throughout. This recital records the position between the Parties and is not an acknowledgement towards any third party.

E. The Parties want to record in writing, between themselves and for the first time, the terms on which the Publisher publishes the Game, and to replace the 2019 Agreement as between the Parties, without any transfer of ownership in the Game or its underlying intellectual property.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Game"** means *1993 Space Machine* and *1993 Shenandoah*, including all versions, builds, updates, patches, ports, downloadable content and soundtrack releases, on every platform on which the Game is or becomes available under this Agreement.

1.2 **"Platforms"** means all digital and physical distribution platforms and storefronts, including without limitation Steam, the Nintendo eShop, the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, key resellers, bundle operators and physical distribution partners.

1.3 **"Territory"** means worldwide.

1.4 **"Gross Revenue"** means all amounts received from Platforms, distributors, Co-Publishers and other third parties in respect of sales and licences of the Game by the Publisher, by any Publisher Group Company or by any sublicensee under section 2.2 (a), whether received on the Publisher's behalf or in its own right. Amounts paid between the Publisher, Publisher Group Companies and Co-Publishers are neither Gross Revenue nor deductible under section 1.5.

1.5 **"Net Revenue"** means Gross Revenue after deduction of the following, and of nothing else:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) taxes on sale or licence, including sales, use, excise and value added taxes;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) royalties, licence fees, revenue shares and other amounts payable to platform holders, storefronts, system licensors and to physical or key wholesalers which purchase or resell copies of the Game, for the right to publish, distribute or manufacture the Game. **Any person engaged by the Publisher to develop, port, market, finance or co-publish the Game is a Co-Publisher under section 9 whatever it is called, and amounts payable to it are not deducted under this paragraph;**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) refunds, chargebacks, returns, credits, price protection and mark-down allowances;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) costs of materials, manufacturing, replication, assembly, packaging and delivery of physical copies of the Game, including freight and fulfilment;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(e) currency exchange fees actually incurred by the Publisher or a Publisher Group Company on amounts received in a currency other than Swedish kronor.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The list in this section is exhaustive. **No marketing, porting, development, internal cost, salary, overhead or management fee of either Party, of any Publisher Group Company or of any Co-Publisher is deducted before the split in section 7.**

1.6 **"Publisher Group Company"** means Aurora Punks AB, reg. no. 559256-9718, and any other company under common control with the Publisher. The Publisher holds the group's Steamworks partner account, and the Game's other platform accounts are, or are being moved to be, held by the Publisher or a Publisher Group Company.

1.7 **"Co-Publisher"** means any co-publisher, financing partner, marketing partner, regional publisher or distribution partner which the Publisher engages for the Game under section 9.

1.8 **"Effective Date"** means the date of the last signature to this Agreement.

## 2. Grant of rights

2.1 The Developer grants the Publisher, for the term of this Agreement, the exclusive right to publish, produce, reproduce, promote, advertise, localise, manufacture, package, market, distribute, display, sell and otherwise commercially exploit the Game on the Platforms in the Territory, and to make the technical and administrative changes required for that purpose.

2.2 The grant includes the right to **sublicense**, in whole or in part, within the scope of section 2.1, to:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) a Publisher Group Company, including the right for Aurora Punks AB to be named as publisher of the Game, to hold platform accounts for it, to distribute it and to receive and invoice the revenue it generates;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) Platforms, storefronts, distributors, key resellers, bundle operators and physical distribution partners, as required to sell and operate the Game; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) a Co-Publisher, on the terms of section 9.

2.3 The Publisher may engage subcontractors and may perform any of its obligations through a Publisher Group Company, and remains fully responsible for their work. **The Publisher remains the Developer's sole counterparty under this Agreement**, and no sublicence under section 2.2 creates any obligation of the Developer towards, or any claim of the Developer against, the sublicensee.

2.4 The Developer grants the Publisher a non-exclusive licence to use the name, logo, artwork and other trade marks of the Game to the extent needed to publish, distribute and market the Game under this Agreement. The Developer may use the same material to promote the Game and the Developer's own work.

2.5 No ownership is transferred. Section 5 applies.

2.6 During the term the Developer shall not itself publish, distribute or sell the Game on any Platform, and shall not licence or authorise any third party to do so.

## 3. Publisher undertakings

3.1 The Publisher shall keep the Game commercially available on the Platforms on which it is released as at the Effective Date, for as long as the platform holder concerned supports it and it is commercially reasonable to do so.

3.2 The Publisher shall administer the store pages, pricing, discounting, sales events, platform certification and platform compliance for the Game, and decides those matters.

3.3 Subject to the following sentence, the Publisher shall procure that the platform accounts through which the Game is sold are held and maintained by the Publisher or a Publisher Group Company. Where the Game is registered on a platform account held in the name of a company which is no longer part of the Publisher's group, the Publisher shall use reasonable efforts to have the Game moved to an account held by the Publisher or a Publisher Group Company, shall keep the Developer informed, and is not in breach of this Agreement if a platform holder or a bankruptcy estate declines or delays the move. Amounts a platform holder has paid to, or withholds from, such a company are Gross Revenue only when actually received by the Publisher or a Publisher Group Company, and the Publisher shall use reasonable efforts to have them released.

3.4 **The Publisher bears its own costs of administering, marketing and porting the Game, and recovers none of them from Net Revenue**, unless the Parties have agreed otherwise under section 6.2. Neither Party commits to a marketing budget.

3.5 The Publisher shall report and pay under section 8.

## 4. Developer undertakings

4.1 The Developer shall grant and maintain the rights required for the Publisher to perform section 3, for the Platforms in the Territory, for the term of this Agreement.

4.2 The Developer shall answer technical questions about the Game and its source code to a reasonable extent, and shall provide the source code, project files and build instructions the Publisher reasonably needs to maintain, rebuild, port or re-certify the Game. **The Developer takes on no ongoing development obligation** and is not required to produce new content.

4.3 **The Developer is solely responsible for any revenue share, royalty or other payment owed by the Developer to any third party in respect of the Game**, including any person who holds a share of the Developer's revenue from the Game. All such amounts are paid by the Developer out of the Developer's share under section 7. The Publisher's only payee under this Agreement is the Developer, and the Publisher has no obligation to account or pay to any such third party.

## 5. Intellectual property

5.1 The Developer remains the owner of the Game and of all underlying material, including the source code, art, music and design. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Game, and any right not expressly granted to the Publisher is reserved by the Developer.

5.2 The Publisher acquires no ownership of the Game. The Publisher's rights are limited to those granted in section 2 and end when this Agreement ends, subject to section 12.

5.3 The Publisher retains ownership of the general tools, services, store assets and marketing material which the Publisher or a Publisher Group Company has created, acquired or creates for the Game and which are not themselves part of the Game. The Developer receives a licence to use store assets and marketing material created for the Game, free of charge and perpetually, after this Agreement ends. The Publisher shall procure that licence from any Publisher Group Company which owns such material.

5.4 The Developer is responsible for ensuring that the Game and the material the Developer provides do not infringe the rights of any third party.

## 6. No recoupment

6.1 **No amount is recouped before the split in section 7.** Any service spend, advance or other recoupable amount which arose under the 2019 Agreement is extinguished on the Effective Date and is not carried into this Agreement. The Developer's share falls due from the first krona of Net Revenue.

6.2 No recoupable advance, porting cost or development funding arises under this Agreement unless the Parties agree it in writing **before the cost is incurred**, stating the amount and that it is recoupable.

## 7. Revenue share

7.1 Net Revenue is shared **fifty (50) per cent to the Publisher and fifty (50) per cent to the Developer**.

7.2 The split applies to all revenue the Game generates on all Platforms in the Territory during the term.

7.3 No amount other than those listed in section 1.5 is deducted before the split.

## 8. Reporting, payment and audit

8.1 The Publisher shall report Net Revenue **quarterly**, within thirty (30) days of the end of each calendar quarter, and shall pay the Developer's share within the same period. The report shall state the amounts received per Platform, the deductions applied under section 1.5, and the resulting split.

8.2 The Parties agree that the Publisher may provide the report as a quarterly updated statement at a private web address to which the Developer has login credentials, and that this satisfies section 8.1. The Publisher shall notify the Developer by email when the statement is updated.

8.3 Payments are made in Swedish kronor against the Developer's invoice, issued on receipt of the report. **Payment made by a Publisher Group Company on the Publisher's behalf discharges the Publisher's obligation.** The Developer shall state on each invoice whether the Developer is approved for Swedish F-skatt and registered for VAT. Where the Developer is not, the Publisher may make the deductions required by law and payment of the net amount discharges the Publisher.

8.4 The figures in the report are taken from the statements the Platforms themselves issue. Where a Platform has not yet issued a statement for a period, the line is marked as estimated and corrected in the following report.

8.5 The Developer may, once per calendar year and at the Developer's own cost, have the underlying records audited by a certified public accountant. The Developer shall give at least ten (10) business days written notice, and the audit shall take place during regular business hours. If the audit shows a discrepancy to the Developer's disadvantage of more than five (5) per cent for the period audited, the Publisher shall pay the shortfall without delay and bear the cost of the audit.

8.6 If refunds or chargebacks are received after a quarter has been reported, the Publisher may set the amount off against later quarters. A quarter without revenue is reported as nil.

8.7 Each Party is responsible for its own taxes on amounts it receives.

8.8 **Minimum payment.** The Publisher shall report every quarter under section 8.1 whatever the amount. Where the Developer's share for a quarter is less than **1,000 SEK**, the Publisher may carry it forward instead of paying it, and shall pay it together with the first later quarter in which the accumulated amount reaches that figure. The report shall state any amount carried forward. **Any amount carried forward is paid in full, whatever its size, on termination of this Agreement and at the Developer's written request at any time.**

## 9. Co-publishers and partners

9.1 The Publisher may bring in one or more Co-Publishers for the Game at its own discretion, **without the Developer's consent and without any renegotiation of this Agreement**.

9.2 **Any Co-Publisher is paid solely out of the Publisher's own share under section 7.1.** The Developer's fifty (50) per cent is not affected, and no amount payable to a Co-Publisher is deducted under section 1.5.

9.3 The Publisher remains solely responsible to the Developer for performance under this Agreement. The Publisher shall notify the Developer in writing when a Co-Publisher is brought in.

## 10. New platforms and versions

10.1 Ports of the Game to further Platforms are within the scope of this Agreement. The Publisher decides whether to make them and **bears the cost, which is not deductible from Net Revenue**, unless the Parties have agreed otherwise under section 6.2.

10.2 A re-release of the Game in substantially its existing form is within the scope of this Agreement. A sequel, remake, remaster or other new game based on the Game, involving new content, is **not** covered by this Agreement. If the Developer decides to develop, publish or licence such a title, or receives an offer to do so, the Developer shall notify the Publisher in writing and the Publisher then has **sixty (60) days** of exclusive negotiation. If the Parties have not reached agreement within that period, the Developer is free to place it elsewhere.

## 11. Term and termination

11.1 This Agreement applies from the Effective Date for a fixed term of **five (5) years**, and is thereafter automatically extended by successive periods of **two (2) years**, unless a Party gives written notice of non-renewal no later than **six (6) months** before the end of the then current period.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

11.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters bankruptcy or company reorganisation, or ceases to conduct its business.

11.4 A failure by the Publisher to provide a report under section 8 for two (2) consecutive quarters is a material breach for the purposes of section 11.2.

11.5 The Developer may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days written notice if the Game has not been commercially available on any Platform for six (6) consecutive months, other than because of a platform holder's decision, a claim under section 15.4 or the Developer's own breach, and the Publisher has not restored availability within sixty (60) days of the Developer's written notice.

## 12. Effect of termination

12.1 The Publisher's rights under section 2 end on termination and all rights in the Game revert to the Developer. The Publisher may continue to sell the Game for up to **ninety (90) days** after termination (the "Wind-down Period") and accounts for that period under section 8. At the end of the Wind-down Period the Publisher shall take the Game off sale on every Platform on which the store presence has not been transferred under section 12.2, unless the Developer has requested otherwise in writing.

12.2 The Publisher shall, and shall procure that any Publisher Group Company holding the account shall, within the Wind-down Period transfer the Game's store presence on each Platform to the Developer or to a party the Developer nominates, so far as the platform holder's own rules permit, and shall carry out the steps required of it without delay. The platform account itself and its financial history do not transfer.

12.3 Revenue accrued up to termination is reported and paid under section 8, including revenue received after termination for sales made before it.

12.4 Sections 4.3, 5, 6.1, 8 (as regards the final period), 12, 13, 14, 15, 16.1 and 16.6 to 16.11 survive termination.

## 13. Credit and marketing

13.1 The Game is credited to the Developer as creator, and to the Publisher or the Publisher Group Company the Publisher designates as publisher.

13.2 Both Parties may use the Game in their own marketing and reference lists.

## 14. Confidentiality

14.1 Neither Party shall disclose the terms of this Agreement or non-public information about the other Party's business or about the Game.

14.2 Disclosure is permitted to advisers and accountants, to Platforms where required, and to potential Co-Publishers under an equivalent obligation of confidentiality, and where disclosure is required by law.

14.3 The Developer may disclose the reports under section 8, and the terms of this Agreement, to a person who holds a share of the Developer's revenue from the Game under section 4.3, under an equivalent obligation of confidentiality.

14.4 This section applies during the term and for three (3) years after termination.

## 15. Warranties, indemnity and liability

15.1 Each Party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it.

15.2 The Developer warrants that the Developer owns or controls the rights granted in section 2, that every other person holding a right in the Game, including its music, has authorised the Developer to grant those rights, and that the Game does not infringe the rights of any third party.

15.3 The Publisher warrants that it will perform its obligations professionally and comply with the Platforms' terms for the Game.

15.4 Each Party shall indemnify the other for documented loss arising from a breach of its warranties in this section, including reasonable legal costs. Where a third party claims that the Game infringes its rights, the Developer shall at the Developer's own cost defend the claim and shall indemnify the Publisher for damages, settlements and reasonable legal costs. The indemnified Party shall notify the other without delay and shall not settle a claim without the other Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The Publisher may suspend distribution of the Game while such a claim is pending, and such suspension is not a breach of this Agreement.

15.5 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, loss of business or loss of data. This does not apply to gross negligence, wilful misconduct or fraud. Amounts payable under sections 7 and 8 are direct loss for the purposes of this Agreement.

15.6 Each Party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amounts paid or payable to the Developer under section 7 in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the claim. **The limit does not apply to the Publisher's obligation to pay the Developer's share under sections 7 and 8, to section 16.1 (c)**, to fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, a breach of section 2.6, or to a third party claim under section 15.4 where the Developer knew of the infringement when granting the rights.

## 16. General provisions

16.1 **The 2019 Agreement.** (a) The Developer consents to **every transfer of the publisher's position under the 2019 Agreement from White Lines Black Spaces AB to the Publisher, however effected and whenever made**, with effect from each such transfer, and confirms that this consent satisfies any requirement for the Developer's consent under the 2019 Agreement. The Developer shall, at the Publisher's request and cost, confirm this consent in writing to a bankruptcy estate or a platform holder.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) As between the Parties, the 2019 Agreement terminates on the Effective Date and is replaced by this Agreement. The Developer confirms that the Developer has no outstanding claim under the 2019 Agreement against the Publisher or any Publisher Group Company, and the Publisher confirms that it has no outstanding claim under the 2019 Agreement against the Developer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) The Publisher shall indemnify the Developer against any claim by the bankruptcy estate of White Lines Black Spaces AB or of Aurora Punks Development Services AB arising from the exploitation of the Game after 25 September 2024 by any person other than the Developer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) This Agreement does not otherwise affect any claim either Party may have in either bankruptcy estate.

16.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the Parties' entire agreement on its subject matter and replaces all earlier oral and written understandings between them concerning the publishing of the Game.

16.3 **Independent parties.** The Parties are independent. Neither Party may bind the other, and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship or a partnership.

16.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. **Section 2.2 and section 9 are not assignments and require no consent**, and neither is the Publisher's performance through a Publisher Group Company under section 2.3. The Publisher may assign this Agreement to a Publisher Group Company, including Aurora Punks AB, on written notice to the Developer.

16.5 **Successors.** The Developer shall not sell, assign, transfer or exclusively licence the Game or the intellectual property rights in it unless the transferee first undertakes in writing to the Publisher to be bound by this Agreement as if it were the Developer. This Agreement binds the Developer's estate and successors in title.

16.6 **Amendments.** Amendments are valid only in writing and signed by both Parties.

16.7 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is responsible for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

16.8 **Severability.** If a provision is held invalid, it shall be limited or removed to the smallest extent necessary, and the remainder of this Agreement stays in force.

16.9 **Notices.** Notices are given in writing by email to mrkristr@gmail.com for the Developer and to robert@aurorapunks.com for the Publisher. Notice of termination under section 11 shall in addition be sent to the receiving Party's registered address as stated at the top of this Agreement.

16.10 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by Swedish law. Disputes shall be settled by the Stockholm District Court (Stockholms tingsrätt) as court of first instance.

16.11 **Language.** This Agreement is made in English. A Swedish translation may be provided for information. In the event of a conflict, the English text prevails.
