# Dark Riviera - IP Catalog

**Internal only.** Parts of this derive from Schedule 3 of the Founders' Agreement, which is
contractually confidential and may not be shared with non-signatories. Nothing here goes to a
partner without Emilie's sign-off.

## Read this first: which list is real

There are three overlapping lists in the mail history and they do not agree. Using the wrong one
in front of a partner is the expensive mistake on this project.

1. **Schedule 3, Founders' Agreement, 27 Sep 2021** (14 IPs). Explicitly labelled *"proposed"* and
   *"non-binding"*. This is what the founders offered to bring in, not what DR owns. Several
   entries were never acquired.
2. **Emilie's game-priority list, 9 Mar 2023** (17 IPs). A working slate with adaptation ideas and
   priority ratings. Useful for the creative read, stale on ownership.
3. **Board meeting summary, 8 Jun 2026** (thread `19ea6648f2297f6f`). **This is the source of
   truth for what DR actually holds today.** It is materially shorter than the other two.

Everything below is anchored on list 3.

## Rights and conflicts

- **RLR is not a clean DR asset.** Runatyr AB is the legal owner of the underlying IP
  (Samarbetsavtal 2023-06-29 §1.1). DR acquired graphic novel rights from Aurora Punk. Aurora Punks
  itself only holds a 5 MSEK claim, not ownership, and that claim may sit in the APDS bankruptcy
  estate. Separately, Yasin Hillborg has a live moral-rights (ideell rätt, URL 3 §) credit claim
  via Scen & Film. See `project_rlr_ip_dispute` and `aurora_punks/ap_ip_ownership_canonical.md`.
  **Never present RLR as an IP DR can license for a game.**
- **Ballistic Pork** - development agreement for Meltdown and Blizzard terminated March 2025,
  went to settlement talks and litigation posture. Anything touching BP-era work needs Emilie first.
- **Aurora Punks is a counterparty, not a synonym for DR.** AP owes DR on invoices, and Creation
  Zero Point carries a DR loan on a repayment plan. If AP is ever proposed as the developer of a DR
  IP, that is a board conversation with Robert's conflict declared, not a line in a partner mail.

General Counsel at Robot Cache to Dec 2019, partner at QED & Associates, executive producer on the

Note `boomboxgames.com` redirects to `boomboxco.com`. There are several unrelated companies called
BoomBox, including a Miniclip-owned mobile puzzle publisher. Philippe's exact vehicle here is worth
confirming on the call, because it decides whether this is a premium or an F2P conversation.

**Read:** Erwin's background is premium and console, plus a strong rights and licensing bent from
the Robot Cache and general counsel years. That points at Hybrid and Primus over UWOC. Both have
real publishing momentum behind them, which is the thing that makes an adaptation easier to fund:
Hybrid has an Urban Comics release landing in the same window he wants to start, and Primus has
three volumes essentially in the can with Créty's art already usable as a visual target.
