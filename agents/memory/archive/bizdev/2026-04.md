## Festival Submissions

Always inventory existing materials (trailers, builds, press kits) before drafting — most submissions already have 80% of required assets. [Aurora Punks, 2026-04-26]

Required: gameplay video (<10 min preferred), playable build or access link, artistic statement (100-300 words), innovation statement (100-300 words), screenshots, contact info. [Aurora Punks, 2026-04-26]

## Reply Drafting Heuristics

- **Outside-sweet-spot referrals via network:** the default is "yes to a 30-min call, no to a retainer". Frame the call as honest input, not a pitch for the biz-side role. Specifically if genre is outside lane (e.g. F2P card game vs Robert's console/PC), say so in the reply — "kan inte lova att jag är rätt person för biz-rollen ni söker, men kan dela ärlig input". - **When a reply draft acknowledges silence, do it once in the first line and move on.** "Sorry för sen återkoppling här, helt under radarn i några veckor." Don't apologize twice, don't over-explain. [gen-214, Theelia, 2026-04-21, voice]
- **First reply to a warm prospect: one title, one link, no call ask.** When Al Hibberd (Pixel Helix) asked "more projects in this vein?", my draft named 2 titles (ToA + BlockEm), added a Gamescom-timing hook, and pushed for a call next week. Robert cut it to ONE title (ToA), ONE Steam link, opened honestly with "We havent focused on retro", and dropped the call ask entirely. Lesson: in a live DM, let the prospect pull. Shorter replies read more confident. Named second title only if first one doesn't land. Never push for a call on the first reply after a positive signal — they'll ask if they want one. [gen-189, Pixel Helix, 2026-04-21, voice]
- **"We havent focused on X" is a stronger opener than "here's how we fit X".** Honest disqualifier builds trust faster than enthusiastic positioning. If the fit is partial, lead with the gap, not the reach. [gen-189, Pixel Helix, 2026-04-21, voice]
