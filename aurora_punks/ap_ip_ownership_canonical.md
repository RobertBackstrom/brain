## E. RLR recovery - Lawyer analysis 2026-07-07 (route CHANGED). Full memo: `drafts/erik_afrime_lawyer_analysis.md`
The "controlled Runatyr bankruptcy → AP buys RLR from estate" plan is **rejected as a återvinning magnet**: all parties are närstående (KonkL 4:3), so KonkL 4:5 has no backward time limit, 4:10 runs 2 years, set-off is barred (5:15-16), and Skatteverket (VAT creditor) would push the trustee to challenge.

**Recommended route instead:** clean the VAT first, then take RLR into AP via a **direct arm's-length purchase from a SOLVENT Runatyr**, the 5M receivable set off against an independently-valued price. Avoid bankruptcy entirely.

**Critical, time-sensitive facts:**
1. **~1,000,000 SEK unreported output-VAT in Runatyr** (Runatyr invoiced APDS 4M + 25% moms for "RLR + Elric källkod/assets" Mar 2025, "paid" by kvittning). Robert as Runatyr VD/firmatecknare carries **SFL 59:13 företrädaransvar** (personal liability). Use the new **rådrum regime (Prop. 2025/26:52, in force 1 Jul 2026)** - regularise + apply for rådrum BEFORE any bankruptcy step.
2. **The 5M receivable / RLR sale right may sit in the bankrupt APDS estate (K 4429-25), not AP.** If AP is the creditor, AP's **bevakning deadline is 21 July 2026 - non-extendable** (see apb-038).
3. Establish who actually holds the receivable before acting; RLR path may run through APDS-trustee Nils Åberg (Carler), not AP.

Intersects `rlr-000-epic` and [[project_rlr_ip_dispute]]. **Do not start any Runatyr wind-down or estate step without the insolvency/tax advokat.**
