## 2. Briefly tell us about your studio and its history

```
Aurora Punks started in 2019 in northern Sweden and is headquartered in Stockholm today. We are a collective of independent micro-studios rather than a classic outsourcing house: around 16 people in the core team and roughly 40 across the collective, which lets us scale a team up and down without hiring against a single project.

We do two things. We make and ship our own games, self-published or with a publisher (Tears of Adria, Ground Zero, Chenso Club, Ooglians, BlockEm!, Hooja, 1993 Space Machine, Sir Whoopass), and we co-develop for larger studios (Ready or Not, The Finals, Helldivers 2, Warhammer 40,000: Darktide, Ghost Signal: A Stellaris Game). We are certified on all console platforms and run our own release management, so a project can go from concept through Steam launch and console versions without changing partner along the way.

Since 2024 we have also built an AI-assisted tech-art pipeline under human art direction. It lets a small senior team carry a lot of art volume in a fixed style, which is what makes short, trailer-first productions work economically for us.
```

## 7. What are your core strengths and unique qualities as a studio?

```
- One team from trailer to shipped game. We can produce the trailer and then build the game it promises, so the look in the trailer is a look we already know we can hold in production. No handover risk between the marketing test and the build.
- Speed with senior people. Our AI-assisted art pipeline, run under human art direction, lets a small team carry high art volume in a locked style. That is what keeps a short production affordable without it looking cheap.
- Style range. We have shipped pixel and 16-bit, stylised 3D, and realistic Unreal work, so we are not tied to one visual lane.
- The full path to market. Steam page and capsule art, wishlist campaigns, release management, console certification and porting are all in-house. If a game tests well, console versions are not a new vendor search.
- Problem solving. A large share of our co-dev work is being brought in to fix something: performance, an engine migration, platform compliance, a release that has to hold a date.
- Straightforward to work with. Swedish company, CET time zone, English working language, clean contracting and invoicing.
```

## 8. What kind of projects or tasks do you NOT take on?

```
- Per-asset outsourcing or staff leasing. We work as a team with our own leads and production, not as individual seats in someone else's backlog.
- Unpaid spec work. We do not build prototypes, trailers or vertical slices at our own cost. We are open to a reduced fee against a revenue share, but production has to be funded.
- Gambling, crypto and NFT projects, and real-money mechanics.
- Adult content, and anything built on hate or harassment.
- MMO-scale backend infrastructure built from scratch.
- Projects with no defined decision point after the trailer stage. We want to know what greenlight looks like before we start.
```
