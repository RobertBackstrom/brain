## Draft post

**Erik Reynolds / Strategic Entertainment - where this stands**

Posting this to everyone so we have the same picture and can decide who joins the meeting. KM has seen most of it already.

Background: Erik posted on LinkedIn looking for co-dev studios for a larger project. It was really an acquihire ad, but I took the call anyway.

It turns out it does not have to be an acquihire. He has funding lined up, bank financing and more, to finance an acquisition of a company for up to 5 M USD. For that money he needs controlling majority. The money would then go into developing Unyverse: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2834630/Unyverse/

Worth noting that this is bigger than what he first came in with. Earlier the conversation was about 1.5-2 M USD as a minority stake in AP. Now it is up to 5 M and control.

He has our background and the deck, he knows the history, and he likes what he sees. I have had two meetings with him and his interest reads as genuine. He likes the AP setup, the hybrid of work-for-hire and backend royalties and the mix of co-dev and our own IP over the years, and he wants to be an active part of it. My impression is that he wants to work operationally to some degree.

Where I stand: I am interested in a structure that keeps the current ownership structure, at least me and Behold. We have only just started to explore after the pivot and I think it is too early to change that.

KM's reflection: if Erik takes control of the company, Behold will want to be bought out as part of the transaction. I have already told Erik that, and floated that the buyout could be structured as a two step effort. He took it fine and wants to keep talking. I do not know yet if control is a hard requirement for him or a negotiating position.

Since this is about ownership and investment I would like at least one more of you to meet him, form your own view and ask the questions you want answered. He is US East Coast, so afternoon or evening CET.

He pinged again yesterday asking when we can meet with KM. So: who wants to be in that meeting, and when are you available this week or next?
