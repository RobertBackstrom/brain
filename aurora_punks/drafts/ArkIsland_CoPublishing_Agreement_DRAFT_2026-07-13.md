## 1. Parties

This Co-Publishing Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of the date of last signature between:

**(1) Aurora Punks AB**, org. nr 559256-9718, a Swedish limited company (**"Aurora Punks"** or **"AP"**); and

**(2) Ark Island Studio AB**, org. nr 559438-1468, a Swedish limited company with its registered office at Skårvägen 8, 603 85 Norrköping (**"Ark Island"** or the **"Developer"**).

Each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## 2. Background

2.1 Ark Island is the developer and **sole owner of the intellectual property** in the video game titles **Tears of Adria** (Steam App ID 2561500) and **Knives & Gutters** (each a **"Title"**, together the **"Titles"**).

2.2 Aurora Punks provides publishing and go-to-market services to games studios and wishes to support the commercial performance of the Titles.

2.3 The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Aurora Punks will provide publishing services for the Titles in exchange for a share of revenue, **without any transfer of ownership** in the Titles or their underlying IP.

## 3. Definitions

- **"Title(s)"** - Tears of Adria and Knives & Gutters, including their base games and any DLC, updates, and ports brought within scope under Section 4.
- **"IP"** - all intellectual property rights in and to the Titles, including game code, art and audio assets, design, characters, world, trademarks, and branding.
- **"Territory"** - Worldwide.
- **"Platforms"** - Steam / PC, plus any subsequent ports brought into scope under Section 4.2.
- **"Net Revenue"** - gross receipts actually received by Ark Island (or by AP on Ark Island's behalf) from sales and monetisation of the Titles, **less**, and with no other deductions: (a) platform/storefront fees (e.g. Steam's 30%); (b) refunds, chargebacks, and returns; (c) VAT, sales, and similar transaction taxes; and (d) mandatory third-party licensor or middleware fees directly attributable to the Titles.
- **"Publishing Costs"** - pre-approved, out-of-pocket costs AP incurs delivering the Services (e.g. paid marketing spend, event/festival fees, localization, QA, porting/cert) - see Section 8 recoup mechanic.
- **"Designee"** - an affiliate or successor entity of AP to which AP may assign this Agreement under Section 12.

## 4. Scope - Titles, Territory, Platforms

4.1 This Agreement covers the Titles listed in Section 2.1 across the Territory (Worldwide) and Platforms (Steam / PC, plus subsequent ports).

4.2 **New platforms / ports.** Where a Title is ported to a new Platform (e.g. console), that Platform comes within scope on the Parties' written agreement, including whether porting/cert costs are treated as recoupable Publishing Costs.

4.3 **DLC and future titles.** DLC and content updates to the Titles are in scope. **No right of first refusal and no option over Ark Island's future titles** is granted - each new Ark Island game is negotiated separately.

## 5. Aurora Punks - Publishing Services

5.1 AP will provide the following publishing services for the Titles, as applicable and as agreed per Title (the **"Services"**):

1. **Marketing and campaigns** - campaign planning, paid and organic marketing, storefront-event and seasonal-sale placement, festival/showcase submissions and coordination.
2. **Store and platform** - storefront page setup and optimisation, Steam/console page management, wishlisting and launch coordination, platform-relationship support.
3. **Community** - community and creator alignment, social content, Discord/creator-guild engagement ahead of and around launch.
4. **PR** - press and influencer outreach, review-key distribution, announcement coordination.
5. **QA** - where agreed, QA coordination and bug triage support.
6. **Localization** - where agreed, localization management.

5.2 **Scope per Title.** The concrete Services differ by Title (e.g. for Knives & Gutters, AP's role is Campaigns + Community; for Tears of Adria, post-release GTM). The Parties will confirm the active Service scope per Title in a short per-Title service schedule (Exhibit A).

5.3 **Marketing spend.** Any paid marketing or event spend AP fronts is pre-approved by Ark Island in writing before it is incurred and treated per the recoup mechanic in Section 8.

5.4 AP performs the Services with reasonable skill and care but **gives no guarantee of commercial results**.

## 6. Ark Island - Developer Obligations

6.1 Ark Island retains full control of development, the build, patching, and creative direction of the Titles.

6.2 Ark Island will: (a) keep the Titles reasonably available for sale on the agreed Platforms during the Term; (b) provide AP with the assets, keys, and store/analytics access reasonably needed to deliver the Services; (c) collect (or authorise AP to collect) revenue and account for it under Section 9; and (d) not knowingly act to circumvent AP's revenue share.

6.3 **Store access.** For Titles where AP or Robert holds Steamworks/store access, that access continues for the purpose of delivering the Services. The Parties will confirm per Title who is merchant of record and receives storefront payouts, since this drives the reporting flow in Section 9.

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 **Ownership stays with Ark Island.** All IP in the Titles is and remains the exclusive property of Ark Island. Nothing in this Agreement transfers, assigns, or co-owns any IP to Aurora Punks.

7.2 **Limited licence to AP.** Ark Island grants AP a **non-exclusive, non-transferable (except per Section 12), revocable licence**, for the Term and Territory, to use the Titles' names, logos, art, and trailers **solely to perform the Services** (marketing, PR, store, community). This licence ends on termination, save for materials already in-market.

7.3 AP acquires no rights in Ark Island's future titles by virtue of this Agreement.

## 8. Revenue Share and Recoup

8.1 **AP publishing revenue share.** In consideration for the Services, Aurora Punks is entitled to a **flat 5% (five percent) of Net Revenue** of each Title across the Territory and Platforms, for the Term.

8.2 **No advance.** AP pays no advance to Ark Island.

8.3 **Recoup of Publishing Costs.** Pre-approved Publishing Costs AP incurs (Section 5.3) are recouped **off the top of Net Revenue before the split** - i.e. AP first recovers its approved out-of-pocket Publishing Costs, then takes its 5% share of the remaining Net Revenue.

8.4 **Per-Title accounting.** Revenue, costs, and AP's share are accounted for **separately per Title** so one Title's performance does not cross-subsidise the other.

## 9. Reporting and Payment

9.1 **Reporting.** The Party that receives storefront payouts (per Section 6.3) reports Net Revenue and AP's share **quarterly, within 30 days of quarter-end**, with a statement itemising gross receipts, deductions, approved Publishing Costs, and the resulting share.

9.2 **Payment.** AP's share (or Ark Island's share, depending on who collects) is paid within **30 days** of each statement, in **SEK**, against a valid invoice. VAT handled per applicable Swedish/EU rules.

9.3 **Audit rights.** Each Party may, on **30 days'** notice and not more than **once per 12 months**, audit the other's Title-related revenue records. If an audit shows an underpayment over **5%**, the audited Party bears the reasonable audit cost.

## 10. Term

10.1 This Agreement runs for an **initial term of 3 years per Title**, from that Title's first in-scope activity.

10.2 **Renewal.** Renewal is by **mutual written agreement only** - no automatic or evergreen renewal.

## 11. Termination

11.1 **For convenience.** Either Party may terminate on **60 days'** written notice. AP's share on revenue from sales during the Term (and on marketing it drove, per Section 11.5) survives per Section 8; after termination AP's share tails only on approved Publishing Costs still unrecouped, and otherwise stops at termination.

11.2 **For breach.** Either Party may terminate on material breach not cured within **30 days** of written notice.

11.3 **Ark Island insolvency.** AP may terminate **immediately** on Ark Island's insolvency, entry into likvidation, konkurs, företagsrekonstruktion, ackord, or a general inability to pay its debts as they fall due. On such termination the publishing licence in Section 7.2 ends, but AP's accrued and unrecouped amounts under Section 8 survive as a claim. *(Note for the real lawyer: enforceability of ipso-facto/insolvency-termination clauses can be limited under Swedish insolvency law (LUS/rekonstruktionslagen) - to be confirmed.)*

11.4 **Effect of termination.** Section 7.1 (Ark Island keeps all IP), accrued payment obligations, Section 13 (confidentiality), and Section 16 (governing law) survive termination.

11.5 **In-market carve-out.** Marketing assets and campaigns already live at termination may run to their natural end; AP's share applies to revenue they generate through that tail.

## 12. Assignment

12.1 **AP assignment to a Designee.** Aurora Punks may **assign or novate this Agreement, in whole or in part, to a Designee** (an AP affiliate or successor entity) on written notice to Ark Island, without Ark Island's further consent. This supports AP's group structuring (e.g. moving publishing into a subsidiary).

12.2 **Ark Island assignment.** Ark Island may not assign without AP's prior written consent (not unreasonably withheld), except to a successor of substantially all its business that assumes the Titles.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 Each Party keeps the other's confidential information (including the terms of this Agreement, revenue figures, unreleased plans, and build materials) confidential and uses it only to perform this Agreement.

13.2 Standard carve-outs apply (already public; independently known; required to be disclosed by law or a Platform, with notice where lawful). Survives termination by **3 years**.

## 14. Related-Party Disclosure and Warranties

14.1 **Related-party disclosure.** The Parties acknowledge that Robert Bäckström holds approximately **5% equity in Ark Island Studio AB** and acts for Aurora Punks. The Parties confirm this Agreement is negotiated at arm's length on the terms set out here, and that the interest has been disclosed. *(Before signature, confirm AP board noting per ABL 8:23 jäv awareness.)*

14.2 **Ark Island warrants** it owns or controls all IP in the Titles and has the right to grant the Section 7.2 licence, and that the Titles do not knowingly infringe third-party rights.

14.3 **AP warrants** it has the right to provide the Services.

14.4 **Note on Knives & Gutters gen-AI:** K&G uses generative AI for character base meshes (final rigged models human-made) and this is disclosed on festival submissions. Keep a consistent IP/AI-provenance representation so store and festival disclosures line up.

## 15. Liability

15.1 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each Party's aggregate liability is capped at the **total amounts paid or payable to AP under this Agreement in the 12 months before the claim**. Standard carve-outs for confidentiality breach, IP infringement, and wilful misconduct.

## 16. Governing Law and Disputes

16.1 **Governing law:** this Agreement is governed by **Swedish law**, excluding its conflict-of-law rules.

16.2 **Disputes:** any dispute is resolved by the Swedish general courts, with **Stockholms tingsrätt** as first instance.

## 17. Miscellaneous

17.1 **Entire agreement / supersession of the prior CoDev arrangement.** This Agreement supersedes and replaces, in their entirety, any prior co-development or co-publishing arrangement between the Parties in respect of Tears of Adria and Knives & Gutters - including the earlier `CoDev_AP_ArkIsland` arrangement (AP 60% of revenue until a recoupable amount ×2 is repaid, plus a monthly service fee). From the effective date, the publishing and revenue terms for these two Titles are governed solely by this Agreement, and no 60%/×2 recoup or service-fee terms apply to them. Any rights or obligations already accrued under the prior arrangement before the effective date are settled separately by the Parties and are not revived by this Agreement.

17.2 Subject to Section 17.1, this Agreement (with its Exhibits) is the whole agreement on its subject and supersedes prior discussions on the Titles' publishing terms.

17.3 **Amendments** - only in writing, signed by both Parties.

17.4 **Notices** - in writing to the Parties' registered addresses / nominated emails.

17.5 **Severability, waiver, counterparts** - standard. Electronic signature (OpenSign) is valid execution.
