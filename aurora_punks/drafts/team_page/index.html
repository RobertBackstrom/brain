00 What the team does /////

A senior team that plugs into a partner's project and ships it.

Aurora Punks is a compact, senior team built around co-development and self-publishing. We take work full-cycle - from concept and prototype through porting, release and live-ops - and we have shipped across PC, console, mobile and VR. The core capabilities below are the ones on the team today.

  • UE5 co-development. Gameplay, tools and engine work on Unreal Engine 5, plugged into a partner's codebase under their direction.
  • Cross-platform porting and release. Console and mobile porting, store setup, commerce and platform compliance.
  • Backend and multiplayer. Networking, live services and industry-standard backends (PlayFab, AccelByte, Firebase, LootLocker), or our own when a project needs it.
  • CERT and QA. Functional and compliance QA carried in the team, with a specialist studio brought in for independent certification.
  • Art pipeline. A tech-art pipeline built to match a partner's style and hold quality at speed, led by a dedicated Tech Art Director.
