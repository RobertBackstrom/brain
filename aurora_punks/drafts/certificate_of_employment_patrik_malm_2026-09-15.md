## Duties and responsibilities

Mr Malm worked as designer and 3D artist on the PC game project *Clönk*, later presented on Steam under the title *IRON EVIL*, as a member of the development team Iron Evil. His responsibilities included:

- game design and 3D art production for the title, from concept work through to playable builds;
- presenting a written design plan to the employer at the start of each development phase;
- delivering an alpha version of the game in phase one and the baseline of a beta version in phase two, both of which were completed and presented to the employer;
- weekly reporting to the employer on progress, risks and blockers;
- acting as the team's main point of contact towards the employer.

## End of employment

Mr Malm's employment ended on 30 April 2023 in connection with a company-wide reorganisation and staff reduction at White Lines Black Spaces AB, carried out in negotiation with the trade union Unionen. The ending of the employment had no connection to Mr Malm's performance or conduct.

## Assessment

Mr Malm carried out his work to our satisfaction and delivered the agreed milestones within the agreed timeframe. He handled the combined role of contributor and team representative reliably, and communication with him was open and timely throughout the engagement. I would work with him again.
