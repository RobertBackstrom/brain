## 2. The signal worth weighing

1. **Profile is operator/marketer, not principal-investor.** Nothing in the public record shows a fund, AUM, a GP role, or a track record of writing $1.5-2M equity cheques. His value-add historically is PR / launch / partnerships, which is real and relevant to AP - but it's a different thing from being the source of $1.5-2M.
2. **Strategic Entertainment reads as a consultancy, not a capital vehicle.** "Funding" appears as one of the services it helps clients with, which usually means sourcing/structuring capital for clients, not deploying the firm's own balance sheet.
3. **His own studio is raising money.** Afrime raised ~$400K in an angel round and is reported to be seeking further capital / a seed round. A founder actively fundraising for his own studio is an odd profile for someone simultaneously deploying $1.5-2M of personal capital elsewhere. Not impossible (the two can be unrelated pots), but it's a flag, not a reassurance.
4. **"as a fixed contract."** Erik's exact words were an infusion of "$1.5-$2M into the company **as a fixed contract**." That phrasing points away from a clean share purchase and toward a contractual/structured arrangement - e.g. funding against a services or publishing agreement, a recoupable advance, a rev-share, or a fund-for-equity structure he'd broker. It may also just be loose language. Either way it's worth pinning down, because **the master draft is built entirely on a Direct-AP-equity frame**, and if Erik is thinking "fixed contract" the frame may not match.
