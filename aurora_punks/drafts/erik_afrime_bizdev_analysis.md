## Part B - Afrime Studios / Unyverse assessment (for a reciprocal AP stake)

### B1. Studio maturity, team, funding
1. **Founded 2023** by **Erik Reynolds** (founder; career PR/marketing/BD - Riot, EA, Atari, THQ, Loot Crate, Vivendi; "games PR unicorn," 100+ launches on the comms side - **not a technical/production or finance principal**) and **Crandon Dillard** (creator). Team distributed **US (Charleston SC) / Ghana / Nigeria**, majority Black-owned.
2. **Funding to date:** ~**$400K angel round**; now entering/seeking a **seed round**. Valuation undisclosed. Team size not disclosed. This is a **pre-seed/seed, pre-revenue, single-title studio.**
3. **Advisory bench (real credibility):** Bruce Hack (ex-Activision Blizzard vice-chairman/CFO), Dr Ray Muzyka (BioWare co-founder), Leo Olebe (ex-Xbox / ex-Meta Games VP). Strong names - lends legitimacy, though advisors are not operators.
4. **Product:** *Unyverse* - Afrofuturist action-RPG fusing RPG systems with fighting-game combat (combos, stances, timing), UE5, semi-open world (continent of Lesser Intabas, D'Zola warrior), bespoke character creator for Black/Afro-descendant options. **Stage: pre-production, "between vertical slice and early demo."** Steam demo live (app 2834630). Target **2027, PC** (some coverage says all platforms incl mobile). Multi-format IP strategy: game + "Unyverse: Black Moon Escape" Fortnite Creator map + an ARK mod + planned anime/comics - sensible low-cost audience-building.

### B2. Market potential - and the "670M" claim, critically
1. **Genre:** action-RPG is large but brutally competitive; the **Afrofuturist action-RPG niche has almost no direct commercial comps at scale.** Nearest reference points: Aurion (Kiro'o, Cameroon, 2016 - African-fantasy ARPG, modest tens-of-thousands sales); the combat comp is closer to Sloclap's Sifu (martial-arts action, 3M+ - but a proven studio with a tight, shippable scope). No Afrofuturist RPG has demonstrated a large paid market. The representation angle is **genuine, underserved differentiation** with a built-in PR narrative (Erik's exact competency) - but differentiation is not proven demand.
2. **The "670M gamers of African descent" TAM claim - treat as pitch-deck inflation.** It conflates *global population of African descent* with *buyers of a premium UE5 action-RPG on PC/console*. A large share of that 670M is in Africa, where PC/console penetration and paid-game spend are low (mobile-first, price-sensitive, thin card/payment rails). The **monetizable Western + diaspora console/PC audience** for this title is a small fraction - realistically low-single-digit-million reachable, and a strong indie outcome would be capturing tens of thousands to low-hundreds-of-thousands of units. **670M is a reach figure, not a sales forecast.** Do not let it anchor any valuation AP pays.

### B3. Development risk - HIGH
1. **Scope/budget mismatch:** a UE5 semi-open-world action-RPG + bespoke fighting-combat + character creator typically costs **$5-20M+** to ship. Afrime has raised ~$400K and needs its seed (and more) to reach the 2027 target. **Large financing gap; if the raise stalls, the project stalls.**
2. **First title at this scale** for the studio - no prior shipped game of comparable ambition. Highest-risk profile.
3. **Distributed team** (US/Ghana/Nigeria) - coordination, infra, timezone, payment-rails risk.
4. **Timeline:** pre-production / "between VS and demo" in mid-2026 with a 2027 ship = aggressive; slip is the base expectation.
5. **Leadership shape:** founder is marketing/BD, co-founder is "creator" - the **production/technical leadership question is open** (who is the technical director / studio production lead carrying an ARPG of this scope?).

### B4. What an Afrime stake is worth, and AP's risks in holding it
1. **Indicative worth:** at seed, a first-time team with one pre-production title usually prices **~$2-6M post-money** (often lower). Any stake AP receives is **mostly optionality, not stored value** - expected value is dominated by a high-probability impairment path (title doesn't ship at target scope, or heavy dilution/pivot) against a low-probability breakout. Downside-adjusted EV is well below face.
2. **Key risks of AP holding Afrime equity:**
   - **Illiquidity** - private, early-stage, no secondary, locked for years.
   - **Single-title / binary** - Afrime's value is ~entirely Unyverse; one outcome decides everything.
   - **Financing/dilution** - Afrime *must* raise more; future rounds dilute AP unless it follows on; down-round risk given a first-time team.
   - **Execution** - budget/scope gap, distributed team, aggressive timeline, non-technical founder.
   - **Triple-exposure / correlation (the biggest structural caution):** if AP (a) holds Afrime equity, (b) co-develops Unyverse for a fee, AND (c) becomes strategically dependent on the relationship, then **one Afrime failure hits AP three ways at once** - impaired equity, an at-risk co-dev receivable, and a strategic hole. Decouple these.
   - **Valuation asymmetry:** Erik would buy into AP at an ~$8M-pre anchor - a revenue-generating, P&L-positive operating company. AP taking Afrime paper values a **pre-revenue, single-title, pre-seed studio.** A reciprocal swap **at face** risks AP trading hard, cash-backed equity for soft optionality. The two currencies are not symmetric.
   - **Cross-border** US/Africa/Sweden legal + tax complexity; weak minority-protection in an unfamiliar cap structure.
3. **Reasoned view:** a reciprocal Afrime stake is defensible **only as small, protected strategic optionality**, and only if:
   - it is **small relative to what Erik puts into AP** (do not match dollar-for-dollar);
   - it carries **downside protection** (liquidation pref / anti-dilution / milestone-gated tranches), not plain common;
   - it is **decoupled from AP's co-dev exposure** so a single Afrime failure does not triple-hit AP;
   - AP **values it at a heavy discount** to any headline post-money and books it as an option, not an asset.
   The more valuable thing Erik brings is arguably **his PR/BD network + a paid co-dev/publishing relationship (cash into AP)** - weight the deal toward those, treat the equity as a sweetener.
4. **Deal-hygiene flag (carried from the counterparty DD, apb-029):** Erik framed his money as "$1.5-2M **as a fixed contract**," his firm reads as a consultancy not a fund, and his own studio is fundraising. Confirm **source of funds + whether it's equity or a structured/services arrangement** before the reciprocal-stake structure is even designed - the two sides of the swap can't be priced until that's known.
