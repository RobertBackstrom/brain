### Material disclosure — 2025 equity position

A write-down of the Vessels of Decay intangible takes equity below the statutory threshold as at
31 December 2025. A settlement with Almi in February 2026 produced a gain of 1 404 468 SEK which
restores the position, but it falls in the 2026 financial year and cannot repair the 2025 balance
sheet.

On 3 September 2026 the auditor required corrections that reduce equity by approximately a further
326 000 SEK across the two years, consuming the margin the board's July plan relied on. How equity
is restored is open with the accountant and the chair and must be settled before the 2025 annual
report is signed.

Under ABL chapter 25 a kontrollbalansräkning obligation is live, and equity must be restored to the
full registered share capital of 198 086.50 SEK by the second control meeting to avoid a
liquidation obligation.

**2025 figures should not be relied on as final until the audit closes.** The 2023 and 2024 annual
reports are public filings and can be verified independently at Bolagsverket.

### Accounting note

Aurora Punks AB has no separate 2026 fiscal year in the accounting system. The year is booked via
Creation Zero Point Holding and allocated. This is a cleanup item for an equity transaction.

### Note on the IP schedule

The IP and rights schedule provided here is the corrected version. An older "Aurora Punks
Intangibles" spreadsheet exists in the wider Drive which predates a July 2026 ownership review and
is **not** accurate. It must not be used. Specifically, the older sheet lists Robot Lord Rising and
Agents of Concordia as wholly owned by Aurora Punks AB, and both are contradicted:

- **Robot Lord Rising is owned by Runatyr Games AB**, per the collaboration agreement of 29 June
  2023, section 1.1. Aurora Punks holds a receivable, not the IP.
- **Agents of Concordia was sold** to Hex Foundry Entertainment AB (559501-1650) in December 2025.
- **Elric of Melniboné** is a lapsed option with no value.
- **The shipped catalogue** (BlockEm, Chenso Club, Ooglians, 1993 Space Machine) was owned by APDS
  and was acquired out of the bankruptcy estate by Creation Zero Point Holding, not by Aurora Punks
  AB. Bringing it into Aurora Punks is part of the proposed transaction structure, not an existing
  fact.
- **Vessels of Decay** ownership requires confirmation against a 2022 transfer agreement before it
  is presented as wholly owned by Aurora Punks AB.
