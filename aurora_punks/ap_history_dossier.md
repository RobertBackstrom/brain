## 1. What Aurora Punks is

"Developers for developers." A Stockholm-based co-development and co-publishing partner for the games industry, founded 2019 in the Arctic part of Sweden (Skellefteå / northern Sweden roots), now HQ'd in Stockholm. Pitches itself as a hands-on co-dev partner and accelerator that takes teams from ideation to release across PC/console and UGC platforms (UEFN, ARK, Roblox).

Legal note: the operating brand "Aurora Punks" today runs through **Aurora Punks AB** (org nr 559256-9718). The earlier **Aurora Punks Development Services AB (APDS)** went into konkurs (bankruptcy administrator Nils Åberg, Carler). Decks from the APDS era still say "Aurora Punks Development Services (fully owned)" - treat that as historical. Verify entity before using any deck claim in legal/financial context.

## 2. Origin & timeline

- **2019** - Founded in northern Sweden. Roots in modding, IP development and problem-solving. Positioned as "the link for newly formed teams to get their games to market."
- **2020** - 1993 Shenandoah / Space Machine released (Amiga demoscene remaster) on all platforms.
- **2022** - BlockEm! and Chenso Club released (both Curve Digital, all platforms). Sir Whoopass released on PC (Aug 2022).
- **2023** - Gravity Circuit released (10% dev rev share). Vessels of Decay released Oct 2023 (HeadUp Games). Hooja mobile game released.
- **2024** - Big co-dev year: Ready or Not (Void Interactive), The Finals (Embark), supporting Oddiko on Meadgard, Fast Travel Games, Malformation, Glitchhammer. Signed publishing for Distant Bloom and Go Fight Fantastic (Kinda Brave). Kwalee and IndieArk partnerships started.
- **2025** - Sir Whoopass console release (Apr 2025) + skin-pack DLC. Ready or Not console release. ~20 FTE in Stockholm. Corporate presentation framing the seed raise.
- **2026 (planned per decks)** - Water Me & You release, Meadgard release, Alara Prime release, 2nd 10+ dev co-dev project; Beyond the Filter Q4 2026; Robot Lord Rising graphic novel + game.

## 3. Business model - three verticals

1. **Co-Development (revenue)** - 360 degree services ideation to release, third-party and proprietary engines, porting to all platforms. Backend/frontend tools, multiplayer & UGC specialists, dedicated UEFN creative team. "Plug-and-play backend" productized from Ready or Not / The Finals experience so small studios can tap a release pipe.
2. **IP Development & Investments (growth)** - Early-stage investor in teams and IPs; in-house IP plus co-creation with renowned developers; service-for-equity / rev-share deals.
3. **Co-Publishing (revenue/growth)** - Funding support (grants, VC, publishers, crowdfunding, private), plus sales, marketing, QA, localization, PR, community.

**Deal structures offered:** Service Fee (fixed cost, dev keeps control) / Service + Revenue Share (reduced fee for rev share, life-of-game partnership) / pure Revenue Share (no upfront, deep partnership). Also frames itself as a hands-on accelerator sitting between incubators and investors/publishers.
