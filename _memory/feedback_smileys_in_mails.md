Text smileys (":)") in client / contact mails are case-by-case — judged by the rapport already established.

**Rule:**
- NEVER use a smiley in a first contact mail to any new person (cold outreach, intro, first project handshake).
- For established correspondents who use friendly / personal tone themselves, smileys are OK and can soften a delivery (e.g., adding a personal opinion that the recipient could otherwise read as pushback).
- Match the recipient's register — if their previous mails are formal, stay formal. If they signal warmth and personal voice (e.g., Rosy Lokhorst's "Hello Both", emoji use, casual abbreviations), reciprocate.

**Why:** Robert wants warmth in established relationships but not premature familiarity with new contacts — the latter reads as presumptuous. Confirmed on 2026-05-11 in the BADASS merged-file delivery mail edit (Robert added a smiley after acknowledging he still thinks v3 is right long-term, softening the moment of taking the L on direction).

**How to apply when drafting:** Default to NO smiley. Only add if:
1. The thread has at least one prior mail from this contact, AND
2. The contact has signalled warmth/personality (used "Hi" not "Dear", used emoji or emoticons themselves, used casual abbreviations or personal asides), AND
3. The smiley softens a moment where you're expressing a personal opinion / mild disagreement / strategic-view-while-deferring — not just decorating a routine message.
