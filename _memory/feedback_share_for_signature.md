When Robert says "share the document(s) with the signatories," do two things (and it means the review round, NOT sending for signature yet):

1. **Share the working copy on Google Drive** with the signatories (comment access — they redline via Suggesting mode; never overwrite the baseline).
2. **Draft an accompanying letter** (Gmail draft, never auto-send) that asks them to **review and redline** changes, and states the **next step is signed copies via OpenSign** (self-hosted eSignature — see [[digital-signatures-self-hosted-opensign]]). Language tracks company nationality — English for foreign companies, Swedish for Swedish companies — **except Bright Gambit, whose letter is English anyway** (anglophone rep). Apply the same recipient-language logic to other Swedish companies with anglophone signatories; flag when unsure.

**Why:** standardises the pre-signature step so contracts get a redline round before eSignature.
**How to apply:** full procedure in [[share_for_signature]]. eSignature tool is **OpenSign** (self-hosted; [[digital-signatures-self-hosted-opensign]]) — Google Sign / Google Drive eSignature is ruled out (no API). Present Drive links clickable ([[feedback_drive_links_clickable]]).
