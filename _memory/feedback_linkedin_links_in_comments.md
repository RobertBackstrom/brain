When a LinkedIn post wants to point at a URL (session page, panel info, article, store page), Robert posts the link as a top comment instead of embedding it in the post body.

**Why:** LinkedIn's algorithm demotes posts with external links in the body (they reduce time-on-platform). Same content with the link moved to a comment ranks meaningfully better. Robert confirmed this is his standard practice after the NG26 speaker post (db-118, 2026-05-19) - he posted the announcement caption clean, then added the panel URL as his own first comment.

**How to apply:** When drafting any LinkedIn post that benefits from a URL (event/session pages, store links, longer-form references), keep the post body link-free and stage the URL as a "first-comment" addendum in the deliverable. For multi-link posts, batch them into one comment with short labels rather than scattering links. Doesn't apply to tags (@mentions) or hashtags — only external URLs.
