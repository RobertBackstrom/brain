**Disposable Corps** is a tactical low-poly 5v5 trench-warfare shooter (WW1 plus steampunk) by **Armoured Dudes** (four people: main programmer, junior programmer, designer/CEO/producer, artist; no UI/UX), published by **Light Up Games (LUG)**. Steam app 3579070, demo 3617330. Prefix `dsc`, project home `disposable_corps/`, epic `dsc-000`.

**The deal changed shape in August 2026.** 2025 was timebilled co-dev support at 35 EUR/h, put on hold. Now **Magnus Lysell (LUG) wants AP to take over production** (2026-08-17, WhatsApp): drop the dedicated-server dependency, deliver a tight core loop, release inside 6-12 months, funded by project investors he meets at Gamescom, modest cash plus incentives. Plan delivered 2026-08-26: internal source `disposable_corps/drafts/dev_plan_high_level.md`, gated page **pitch.aurorapunks.com/disposable-corps** (`lug` / `kE4hhTF44sXN`).

**The commercial model (2026-08-26, both sides in one budget).** Anthony asked for the founders to be included and set their price himself: **30 000 SEK a month for both**. AP seats: lead programmer 50 percent (45 000, Fredrik Laurent at the K2C/Ark Island rate), UI/UX designer 50 percent (40 000), artist 50 percent (40 000, Prateek), producer 25 percent (25 000, Robert at 25 percent of his K2C salary). **Project cost 180 000 a month.** AP caps its own cash at 1 500 000 over twelve months and defers 300 000; the founders' 30 000 is cash on top. **Cash requirement 155 000 a month, 1 860 000 over the term.** Deferred recoups at **2.5x (750 000)** on release, then **15 percent revenue share**. Commitment levels: 155k for the review month, 775k through month 5, 1,86M full term.

**The team on their side is now two people.** The earlier four-person team is gone. Paul codes, Hammer carries vision and game design. AP's programmer seat is at half time because Paul codes day to day; UX stays staffed because nobody on their side covers it.

**The mandate problem is resolved in principle (2026-08-26).** Robert's formulation, accepted by Anthony in the shared Discord group **LUG <> AP Disposable Corps** with Magnus present: AP is "the annoying partner who asks for fixes and makes sure they understand it's needed, then we discuss solutions and if Paul says he wanna do it we set a deadline for it." AP owns product decisions and the schedule, Paul keeps the implementation. Residual risk: no sanction if deadlines slip. That group is now the live channel and LUG will package the plan for their finance partners.

**Product state:** never released, no date, no reviews, **no public update since 2026-01-10** after two playtests where the second one refactored the foundation rather than closing the product.
