In any client- or partner-facing material (pitches, decks, proposals, intro mails, contracts), **do not name the subcontractor** doing the co-dev work. Refer to them generically: "our co-dev team", "Aurora Punks' subcontract engineering", "our engineer". Only use the actual name (e.g. **Robin Hofström** on Curveball) when Robert explicitly tells you to.

**Why:** AP fronts the engagement; who AP subcontracts to is AP's internal arrangement, not something a client/partner needs named. Naming the subcontractor up front exposes the supply chain and can undercut AP's positioning as the deliverer. (Established 2026-06-29 on the Curveball / Light Up Games pitch - Robert had me strip "Robin" from the pitch page.)

**How to apply:**
- Scrub subcontractor personal names from anything outward-facing before it ships; use a generic role label.
- Internal masterbrain notes (project memory, CLAUDE.md, output_log) may name them - the rule is about client/partner-facing artifacts.
