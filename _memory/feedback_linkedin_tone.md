Robert's LinkedIn voice leans warmer and more personal than the general writing voice guide suggests for "casual-professional". Key patterns from the Striden post he approved:

- Opens with genuine enthusiasm ("So cool to be working with...")
- Names people directly, not just companies
- Mixes factual game info with personal commentary
- Ends with a personal anecdote or callout (e.g., KM Troedsson playtime shoutout)
- Parenthetical humor works well: "(investment)game"
- YouTube/media links inline between paragraphs, not at the end
- Hashtags at the bottom, standard set
- Spacing between paragraphs for readability

**Why:** Robert tweaked the draft to be more personal and enthusiastic than what I initially wrote. My first draft was too "press release" - his version felt like a real person talking about people he likes working with.

**How to apply:** When drafting LinkedIn posts, lead with the personal connection, not the product pitch. Let Robert's excitement come through. Don't over-polish.
