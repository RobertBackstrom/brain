# Target Rate Card (co-dev / service quotes)

Robert's canonical target hourly rates for pricing co-dev and service proposals. All figures **SEK, ex. VAT**. Bracketed figure = **AAA-tier rate** to use when the client is a well-funded AAA/publisher engagement; unbracketed = default/indie/soft-launch target. Named people are the reference talent for each band. Source: Robert direction 2026-07-03 (Teef reprice).

| Discipline | Reference people | Target SEK/h | AAA SEK/h |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Producer / Fractional COO | Robert | 1000 | 1500 |
| Senior Dev | Semi, Hassan, Tim, Fredrik, Oskar, Robin, Gustav | 650–800 | 1000 |
| Intermediate Dev | Petter, Ducktape devs (Nordingrå) | 500–650 | 800 |
| Sound / Music | Carolina | 400–500 | 700 |
| Art Director / Art Lead | Imi | 650–800 | 1000 |
| Artists | Prateek | 400–500 | 700 |
| Level Design | Elias Strandberg | 400–500 | 700 |

**Retainer anchor:** 100K SEK/month is the target monthly dev retainer for a **mixed-discipline** job (use when no per-line breakdown is needed, or as a sanity check on a bottom-up build-up).

**How to apply:**
- Build proposals bottom-up: FTE allocation × build weeks × 40h × the band rate, then blend. Pick within each range by seniority/market; drop to the low end for lean soft-launch tests, top end or AAA column for funded AAA clients.
- These are **target sell rates** (already include margin), not internal cost. Don't stack another margin on top.
- Historical context for calibration: older AP quotes used a flat ~€80/h (~912 SEK) blended build rate (e.g. Teef v1); the new card is generally lower — applying it strictly to an already-anchored deal can leave money on the table, so surface the anchor-vs-card gap to Robert as a pricing-strategy call.
- Currency: quote clients in their currency (EUR for Teef/UK). Convert at the live EUR/SEK (~11.4 mid-2026) and state the rate used.
