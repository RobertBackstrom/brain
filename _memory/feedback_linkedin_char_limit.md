LinkedIn connection-request personal notes are limited to **200 characters** for new contacts (not 300 as older docs state). Draft connection notes to fit within 200 chars, including the URL.

**Why:** Robert confirmed 2026-04-20 — prior drafts (e.g. gen-189 Al Hibberd note) were written at ~280 chars and wouldn't have sent cleanly. LinkedIn cut the Premium-vs-free limit; 200 is the current ceiling for free-tier initiators.

**How to apply:**
- When writing any LinkedIn connection-request note (BizDev outreach, Robert's personal adds, prospect intros), count characters including URL and keep under 200.
- If the pitch needs more than 200 chars to land, put the short hook in the note and save the longer context for a follow-up DM after the connect is accepted (pattern already used in [drafts/gen-189/linkedin-messages.md](drafts/gen-189/linkedin-messages.md)).
- Update older LinkedIn draft files if you encounter ones that claim "limit: ~300 chars."
