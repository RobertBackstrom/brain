# Dark Riviera (dr)

Dark Riviera AB, 559319-0597. French/Swedish IP house built around **Sylvain Runberg** (90+ graphic
novels, wrote the Millennium and *Watch Dogs: Legion* adaptations). CEO **Emilie Blézat**, co-founder
**David Dusa**. Robert is a founder and **board chair**; the equity is **6% held via CZP**, not
personally ([[project_czp_finances]]).

Robert's function here is **business development: placing DR IP with game studios and publishers.**
Project folder `dark_riviera/`, catalog at `dark_riviera/ip_catalog.md`, prefix `dr`.

## The one thing that will bite you

**Three IP lists exist and they disagree.** Use the right one:

1. Founders' Agreement **Schedule 3, Sep 2021** (14 IPs) - explicitly *"proposed"* and
   *"non-binding"*, and contractually confidential. It is **not** an ownership list.
2. Emilie's game-priority list, **Mar 2023** (17 IPs) - good creative read, stale on ownership.
3. **Board meeting summary, 8 Jun 2026** (thread `19ea6648f2297f6f`, personal mailbox) - **this is
   what DR actually holds.** Materially shorter.

Eight IPs from the 2021 list are absent from the 2026 one (Hasugi, Dinosorus, Homeless, Paradise
Institute, The Missing Chapter, School of Treason, Renegades, Powder Puff). **Meltdown and Bernays
are being transferred out to Sciapode.** Offering any of these to a partner would be the expensive
mistake.

## What is actually sellable right now

- **Hybrid / Hybrid Paradox** - graphic novel green-lit with Urban Comics, publication 2026/27.
- **Primus / Arcavision** - three-volume run, vol 1 inked Dec 2025, art by Stéphane Créty.
- **Code Name Hope** and **Archivist/Blizzard** - Sylvain names these as the most game-ready.
- **RLR is NOT sellable for a game.** Runatyr owns the underlying IP, and there is a live moral-rights
  claim. See [[project_rlr_ip_dispute]].

## Conflict of interest, standing rule

Robert is on three sides: DR chair, Aurora Punks (a possible *developer* and a debtor to DR on
invoices), and CZP (holds the 6% and carries a DR loan). **Never blur DR and AP in a partner mail
where Emilie is copied.** If AP should build a DR IP, that is a board conversation with the conflict
declared.
