---
name: Full-bleed gameplay backgrounds
description: All social content must have gameplay filling the entire screen, text overlaid directly on footage, no black boxes
type: feedback
---

Gameplay footage should fill the whole screen on every format (static, carousel, reel). Text callouts go directly on the footage, not on black boxes or darkened panels above/below.

**Why:** Black boxes waste screen space and look amateurish. The gameplay IS the visual hook. Every pixel should show the game.

**How to apply:** When building social images/slides, resize gameplay to cover the full 1080x1350 canvas. Use text shadows, strokes, or subtle gradient overlays for readability instead of opaque black rectangles.
