# Web Scan — 1993 Space Machine / 1993 Shenandoah

Scanned 2026-04-17 for pitch to Al Hibberd (Pixel Helix).

## Story hooks (high-value for pitch)

- **"Found during a house renovation"** — Krister Karlsson rediscovered the 1992 Amiga demo during housing renovations after it had been shelved for decades. Perfect nostalgia / reboot angle. [Nintendo Life, Two Beard Gaming]
- **Authentic Amiga code, not pastiche** — 1993 Space Machine reworked the original Amiga code itself, calibrated for widescreen. It's literally the 1993 game, not a modern "retro-style" imitator.
- **~30 years from conception to release** — original 1992 dev → Amiga magazines of the era (Amiga Format, Commodore User) featured it → shelved → Steam 2016 (Space Machine) → Switch 2020 (Shenandoah).

## Provenance

- **Original devs:** Krister Karlsson + 3 friends (1992, Amiga 500 era)
- **Current dev studios:** Exceed + Limit Break
- **Publisher:** Aurora Punks
- **Featured in period media:** Amiga Format, Commodore User

## Critical reception

- **Gamereactor / PC Zone / Game Raven:** positive — praise for retro aesthetic, enemy variety, music, no framerate drops with "dozens of enemies on screen"
- **Gamesfreezer (Shenandoah Switch):** 7.5/10 — "A Great Game To Pick Up For Those Fans of Old Amiga Shooters"
- **Nintendo Life (Shenandoah):** polarized — Amiga-era fans love it, newcomers confused. Sound mix criticized (music too quiet)
- **Known negatives:** difficulty frustrating, sound effects uneven, thin story

## Content callouts (useful for pitch copy)

- **Influences:** Gradius, R-Type, Life Force (Space Machine's own framing)
- **Design lineage:** Bitmap Brothers art style, Turrican / Shadow of the Beast MOD soundtrack, Xenon 2: Megablast weapon system (Shenandoah description)
- **Game content:** 9 ships, 25 weapons × 3 power levels, 14 levels, 10 bosses, co-op
