The Story

A demo found during a house renovation.

In 1992, Krister Karlsson and three friends were building a shoot'em up for the Amiga 500 called Shenandoah: Daughter of the Stars. Life got in the way, the project was shelved, and the disks went into storage.

Decades later, Krister found them during a house renovation.

Instead of rebuilding in a modern engine, the team went back to the original Amiga code, calibrated it for widescreen, and shipped it. The result is period-authentic, not retro-styled. Bitmap Brothers art. Turrican-style MOD soundtrack. Xenon 2 weapon system. R-Type influences. Featured in Amiga Format and Commodore User back in the day.

Released on Steam as 1993 Space Machine (2016). Ported to Switch as 1993 Shenandoah (2020). Over 100,000 copies sold across PC and Switch. PS4 released, iOS and Android builds ready for release.

Gameplay

Dozens of enemies on screen. Zero framerate drops.
