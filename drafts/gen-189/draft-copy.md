## Hero

**1993 SPACE MACHINE**
*An Amiga shmup lost for twenty years. Rediscovered and released.*

## Story

In 1992, Krister Karlsson and three friends were building a shoot'em up for the Amiga 500 called *Shenandoah: Daughter of the Stars*. Life got in the way and the disks went into storage.

Decades later, Krister found them during a house renovation.

Instead of rebuilding in a modern engine, the team went back to the original Amiga code, calibrated it for widescreen, and shipped it. The result is period-authentic, not retro-styled — Bitmap Brothers art, Turrican-style MOD soundtrack, Xenon 2 weapon system, R-Type influences. Featured in Amiga Format and Commodore User at the time.

Released on Steam as 1993 Space Machine (2016). Ported to Switch as 1993 Shenandoah (2020). Over 100,000 copies sold across PC and Switch. Mobile ports ready.

## Why it fits Pixel Helix

Actual Amiga-era code, not a modern imitation. Period-authentic design from the people who built it in 1992. Already released on PC and Switch with mobile ports ready to go.
