## Story

Tears of Adria is a real-time strategy adventure with autobattles. You start with one hero. Soon you have a party, a dragon problem, a few questionable artifacts, and far too much confidence.

Old-school RPG bones — orcs, dwarves, elves, undead, all with their own rivalries — wrapped around a 20×20 grid auto-battle system where positioning, gear, and skills all interact in ways the game doesn't bother explaining.

> *"Tears of Adria doesn't care where you go, or why. What it can't stand is weaklings."*

Your main character is locked in. The rest are replaceable. You can scale a party up to 80 henchmen if you want to bring chaos to the land. Or play it small and stealthy with six Rangers. Or be a lone Necromancer with an undead legion. Same map, different runs.

Hidden paths. Strange encounters. Rewards with no introduction. Some things only exist if you notice them.

Self-published in November 2023 by **Ark Island Studio**. **94% Very Positive** on Steam, with reviewers logging hundreds to thousands of hours per save.
