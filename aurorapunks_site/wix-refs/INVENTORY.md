## Design System

### Colors
- Site background: #0c0c1c (very dark navy/near-black)
- Accent teal primary: #65ede8 / rgb(101, 237, 232) - H1 headline color
- Accent teal secondary: #1ab1ab / rgb(26, 177, 171) - H2, CTA headings
- Accent teal muted: #5bbeba / rgb(91, 190, 186) - spans, links
- Body text: #ffffff (white)
- Dark purple accent: #adaddc / rgb(173, 173, 220)
- Button bg dark: #282626 / rgb(40, 38, 38)
- Form input bg: rgba(12, 12, 28, 0.01) with white text
- Submit button: white bg, black text

### Typography
- Display/Headings: madefor-display-extrabold (Wix proprietary). Best Google Fonts substitute: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 800 or Rajdhani Bold.

- Nav items: Chakra Petch 400, 15px, white, letter-spacing 0.75px
- H1 hero: madefor-display-extrabold equivalent, 45px, #65ede8
- H2 subtitle: equivalent, 20px, #1ab1ab
- H2 CTA: equivalent, 35px, #1ab1ab
- Body paragraphs: Chakra Petch 400, 18px, white
